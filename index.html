Jure Tandara je bio, prema arhivskoj dokumentaciji, sin Ivana Tandarića koji je kasnije ustalio obiteljsko prezime u Tandara. Bio je oženjen s Matijom Lekić i imali su sina Franu i dvije kćeri, Anđu i Anu.
Frane je bio oženjen s Božom Ćosić i 1792. godine su dobili sina Nikolu.
Kod popisa stanovništva u Ričićama iz 1806. godine nije zabilježen niti jedan član Jurine obitelji i nije poznato što se dogodilo s njegovom obitelji. Pretpostavka je da su odselili nakon čega im se izgubio svaki trag.

According to archival records, Jure Tandara was the son of Ivan Tandarić, who later established the family surname as Tandara. He was married to Matija Lekić and they had a son, Frane, and two daughters, Anđa and Ana.
Frane was married to Boža Ćosić and in 1792 they had a son, Nikola.
In the 1806 census of Ričići, no member of Jure’s family was recorded, and it is not known what happened to his family. It is assumed that they moved away, after which all trace of them was lost.
