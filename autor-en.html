About the Author

Personal Introduction

Dear visitor, welcome to the web pages dedicated to the history, origin and heritage of the surname Tandara.

My name is Jure Tandara, a road traffic engineer. I am 70 years old and have been retired for six years. I live in Zagreb with my wife Ruža. I am the father of two sons — Ivica Julio and Marin Tino — and the grandfather of five grandchildren: Vigo, David, Jakov, Kaia and Laura.

After a long working life, today I dedicate myself to my family, to researching our roots, and to everything that connects me with nature and creativity.

Professional Career

I began my professional career as the Head of the Croatian Railways Rolling Stock Repair and Maintenance Facility in Zagreb, where I was responsible for the organization, supervision and quality of maintenance, as well as the reliability of the rolling stock. This initial engagement gave me a strong technical and organizational foundation for my further work in the field of traffic systems.

After that, I worked as the Head of the Container Terminal in Zagreb, where I was responsible for managing terminal operations, organizing freight flows and coordinating logistics processes.

I then took over the position of Head of the Traffic Safety Service on the Rijeka–Zagreb Motorway. In cooperation with my colleague, the Head of the Maintenance Sector, Mr. Dinko Pešut, we laid the foundations of a modern motorway traffic safety maintenance and management system, the first such system in the Republic of Croatia. Our work was also recognized by the European Commission, which awarded the Tuhobić Tunnel recognition as the safest tunnel in Europe in 2009, as the tunnel that achieved the greatest safety improvement after a poor rating in the first testing in 2004.

An important part of my professional work was also the management of the implementation project of the RDS-TMC system in the Republic of Croatia, which laid the foundation for modern traffic information and the integration of road information into the European network of dynamic traffic management.

Sport and Technical Creativity

Alongside my professional work, I devoted a large part of my life to sport and technical creativity. I hold a black belt in karate and am a former member of the Karate Club Tempo representative team, and for many years I was involved in skiing, mountaineering and cycling.

I found special joy in making models of historic ships, replicas of Roman and ancient war machines, and wooden toys for my grandchildren.

Family and Everyday Life

Today I most enjoy nature and the time spent with my grandchildren, creating memories with them that transcend generations. My family, children, grandchildren and friends remain my greatest source of inspiration and joy in life.

About the Project

This web project is in the initial phase of development. The collected materials are numerous, and new ones are still being added, so it will take quite some time to arrange, process and present everything as a complete digital archive of the Tandara family line. The website has been opened so that you can follow its development and gradual expansion, and understand the way in which the content is growing and being upgraded.

The website is primarily intended for use on a laptop or personal computer, because the initial display of the family tree and other graphic elements contains a large number of details that cannot be fully displayed on the screen of a mobile phone or tablet. On mobile devices, the user has to scroll in several directions, zoom in, and in some cases rotate the screen by 90°, which is not intuitive or practical for many people. In order to avoid the possibility of misinterpreting data or missing important information, the use of a computer is recommended, where the display is clear, stable and fully visible.

This project was created out of the desire to collect, preserve and systematically present in one place information about our ancestors, family branches, the places where they lived and the events that shaped the history of the Tandara family line.

Over time, many valuable records have been lost, forgotten or scattered across different archives and private collections. Therefore, the goal of these pages is to preserve family heritage, historical identity and memories for present and future generations.

The family trees and data published on these pages are the result of many years of research into parish archives, registry books, historical sources, archival materials and testimonies of family members.

A special place in this research belongs to the work of Milan Lažeta, whose publication “Genealogy of the Tandara Clan” laid the foundation for further study of the history and origin of our family and remains one of the most valuable sources for understanding the heritage of the Tandara family line.

Every piece of information found represents a small but significant part of the mosaic that together forms the story of our origin.

These are the first web pages dedicated to the Tandara surname. Their purpose is to lay the foundation for a shared family genealogy and to encourage future generations to continue researching, supplementing and preserving the history of our family line.

As part of the further development of the project, the production of T-shirts in different sizes is planned, featuring the logo of the home page – the family tree with four main branches and the right-side diagram of the first families bearing the Tandara surname. Since the entire project is financed exclusively from my pension, the number of T-shirts will be limited. Several copies will be given as gifts to the first or prominent members of our family line, those who will proudly wear the surname on their chests.

If there is interest in producing a wall poster – either as room decoration or as a large wall map – please contact me through the contact information provided on the website. I will gladly consider the possibilities of production and arrange the details with everyone who would like to have such a display of family history.
