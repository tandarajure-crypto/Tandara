About the Author

Personal Introduction

After a long and fulfilling professional career, I now devote my time peacefully to my family, to researching our roots, and to everything that connects me with nature and creativity.

About the Project

This project was created out of a desire to collect, preserve and systematically present information about our ancestors, family branches, the places in which they lived, and the events that shaped the history of the Tandara family line.

Over time, many valuable records have been lost, forgotten or scattered across various archives and private collections. For this reason, the goal of these pages is to preserve family identity, historical heritage and memories for present and future generations.

The family trees and data published on this website are the result of many years of research in church archives, parish registers, historical sources, archival materials and testimonies of family members.

A special place in this research belongs to the work of Milan Lažeta, whose publication “Genealogy of the Tandara Clan” laid the foundation for further study of the history and origins of our family and remains one of the most valuable sources for understanding the heritage of the Tandara lineage.

Every discovered piece of information represents a small but meaningful part of the mosaic that together forms the story of our origins.

These are the first web pages dedicated to the Tandara surname. Their purpose is to establish the foundation for a shared family genealogy and to encourage future generations to continue researching, expanding and preserving our family history.

I hope this project will continue to grow and develop over time, becoming a lasting place for gathering information, memories and stories connected to the Tandara name.
