FRANE’S SUB-BRANCH

Frane Jurin and Boža née Ćosić

Frane Jurin: ★ unknown  –  ✝ unknown

Wife Boža née Ćosić: ★ unknown  –  ✝ unknown

Frane Jurin was the son of Jure Tandara and Matija née Lekić. He belonged to the oldest branch of the first Tandara family.

According to the records currently available, Frane and Boža had one known son, Nikola, Frane’s son (★ 1792  –  ✝ unknown).

It is not known whether Nikola married or had children. As no later records have been found for this sub-branch, it is possible that the family moved away from Ričice, after which every trace of its members and descendants was lost.

How to use the page and diagram

  1. To return to the main family-tree page, select the home button in the left-hand menu.
  2. Select the Croatian or English version by clicking the corresponding HR or EN flag.
  3. Enter a name, code or detail in the search field, then select Find.
  4. Click a person’s box to open the available details about that person and family.
  5. Use the + or − symbol on a person’s box to open or close the descendants.
  6. Expand all displays the entire Frane sub-branch, Collapse closes expanded sections, and Three generations displays the first generations of the sub-branch.
  7. Fit adjusts the complete diagram to the available area, while the + and − buttons zoom in or out.
  8. On a computer, drag the diagram with the mouse. On a phone or tablet, drag it with your finger.
