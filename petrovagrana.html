Druga grana prve obitelji TANDARA

Petar Tandara(1734-1817)

Petar Tandara bio je nesumnjivo sin Ivana Tandarića, najvjerojatnije drugi ili treći po redu, ovisno o korelaciji s livanjskim Filipom kao jednim od potencijalnih Ivanovih potomaka. Umro je 1817. godine u osamdeset i trećoj godini života, pa se procjenjuje da je rođen oko 1734. godine.

Petar je bio oženjen Jelom Budić. Imali su sinove Grgu, Jakova, Martina, Marijana, Miju, Tadiju i Marka te kćeri Mandu i Jelu. Sinovi Jakov, Marijan i Marko ne pojavljuju se na Popisu duša iz 1806. godine, a Tadija je izostavljen s popisa iz 1819. godine, pa se može pretpostaviti da više nisu bili među živima ili je netko od njih odselio iz Ričica.

Sinovi Grgo, Martin i Mijo jedini su nastavili lozu.

Grgo Petrov (1770- )

Najstariji Petrov sin oženio se Ružom Pušić. Imali su sina Šimuna i kćer Ivu. Šimun nije imao muškog nasljednika tako da se Grgina grana ugasila.

Martin Petrov (1775-1844)

Petrov sin Martin oženio se Ružom Lekić. Imali su sinove Ivana, Juru, Miju, Grgu, Filipa i Josipa te kćeri Matiju i Anu.

Za Juru, Filipa i Josipa nisu pronađeni drugi zapisi osim onih o njihovu krštenju, pa nije poznato jesu li umrli mladi ili su se odselili. Sinovi Ivan, Mijo i Grgo su jedini nastavili lozu.

Mijo Petrov (nema g. rođenja i smrti)

Mijo Petrov bio je oženjen Ružom nepoznatog djevojačkog prezimena. Imali su kćer Matiju. Muškog nasljednika nisu imali pa se Mijina grana ugasila.

Ivan Martinov (1807-1877)

Najstariji Martinov sin godine 1826. oženio se Anticom Lažeta. Ivan i Antica imali su sinove Marijana, Tomu i Juru, te kćeri Ružu, Ivu, Jozu i Matiju. Marijan je umro kao malo dijete. O Juri nisu pronađeni drugi podaci osim zapisa o njegovu rođenju. Toma je bio jedini nasljednik Ivanove loze.

Mijo Martinov (1814- )

Treći Martinov sin i njegova žena Matija rođ. Parlov imali su sinove Martina i Miju (koji je umro kao malo dijete), te kćeri Lucu, Mandu, Božu, Jozu, Mariju i Matiju. O Martinu nema drugih zapisa osim onoga o njegovu rođenju. Ne zna se da li je umro ili se odselio. Nakon Martina Mijina se loza okončala.

Grgo Martinov (1818- )

Četvrti Martinov sin ženio se dvaput. Iz prvog braka s Rosom rođ. Budimir imao je sina Lovru i kćer Jaku. U drugom braku s Jakom rođ. Knežević, Grgo je imao sinove Matu (koji je umro kao malo dijete) i Miju, te kćeri Matiju, Jozu i Veroniku.

TROMIĆI

Toma Ivanov (1830- )

Toma je drugi Ivanov sin, a bio je oženjen Tomicom Kovač. Imali su sinove Ivana, Matu, Juru i ponovo Ivana koji je umro kao dijete. Od svih jedino Jure je imao potomstvo. Tomini potomci bili su u Zavelimu prepoznatljivi pod nadimkom Tromići. Prema predaji bili su izrazito visoki i spori, odnosno tromo su se kretali. Po tim svojim atributima dobili su nadimak Tromići. U nastavku će se obrađivati samo Jurina loza koji je jedini Tomin nasljednik.

LOVRO U RIČICAMA

Godine 1819. obitelj Martina Tandare, Lovrina djeda, bila je u Zavelimu. Na tom popisu su i Martinovi sinovi Ivan, Mijo i Grgo. Kad se 1835. radio austrijski katastar, Martin Tandara pok. Petra, upisan je kao posjednik nekretnina u Ričicama. Većina ričićkih plemena imala je u to vrijeme nekretnine u Ričicama, Zavelimu, Podbiloj i Viru. Navedena sela imala su zajedničku župu u Ričicama. Mnoge obitelji unutar jednog roda su selile s jednog lokaliteta na drugi, čak i u samim Ričicama. Broj stanovnika je naglo rastao i tražili su se novi prostori za život. U tom kontekstu možemo razumjeti i prihvatiti činjenicu da je neka obitelj, ili njezin dio, kod jednog popisa bila u Ričicama, a kod sljedećeg u Zavelimu ili obrnuto.

Lovro Grgin (1842-1922)

Najstariji Grgin sin oženio se Ivom Parlov i s njom imao sinove Antu, Ivana i Marijana, te kćeri Ivu i Lucu. O Ivanu nema drugih podataka osim zapisa o njegovu rođenju. U Matičnoj knjizi umrlih župe Ričice zapisano je da su Lovrinu kćer Lucu u trinaestoj godini života „ubile stine“.

Mijo Grgin (1856-1933)

Treći Grgin sin, oženio se Ivom Tandara. Imali su sinove Martina (koji je umro u dobi od 19 godina), Nikolu i Matu (koji je umro kao malo dijete) i ponovo Nikolu, te kćeri Ivu i Maru. O Nikoli mlađem nisam našao drugih podataka osim zapisa o njegovu rođenju dok je stariji Nikola preminuo kao dijete. Mijina djeca nisu imala nasljednike loze.

Jure Tomin (1874-1931)

Treći Tomin sin i jedini nasljednik uzeo je za ženu Ivu Lažeta. Imali su sinove Tomu i Ivana (koji su umrli kao mala djeca), zatim Pia, Dominika i Matu, te kćer Matiju.

Pio Jurin (1916-1990)

Treći Jurin sin uzeo je za suprugu Tereziju Budimir sa kojom su dobili sinove Ivana, Juru i Jozu, te kćer Maru. Najstariji sin Ivan (1936-2025) sa suprugom Marijanom Petter ima sina Dina i kćer Romanu. Dino sa suprugom Matildom Klepikapić ima kćeri Hedvigu, Leonardu i Luciju. Obitelj danas živi u Zagrebu.

Dominik Jurin (1919-1987)

Četvrti Jurin sin sa suprugom Marom Knezović je dobio sinove Antu, Barišu, Nikolu, Pia, Svetu i Ivana, te kćer Olgu. Samo sin Ivan ima sinove Dominika i Davida koji nastavljaju Dominu granu.

Mate Jurin (1922-1963)

Peti Jurin sin oženio je Maru Budić sa kojom je dobio sinove Tomislava, Miju i........, te kćer Matiju. Dva najmlađa sina su umrli u petoj godini života. Tomislav je oženio Božicu Golubiček sa kojom ima sinove Martina i Matu. Mate se oženio sa suprugom Ivanom Trošelj sa kojom je dobio kćer Tenu. Potomci Mate Jurina danas žive u Zagrebu.

Doktor Bruno rođen je 7. listopada 1937. u Ričicama. Godine 1968. oženio se Vanjom Brkljača i s njom dobio sina Zvonimira, te kćeri Zrinku i Tomislavu. Zvonimir sa suprugom Anetom Gacek ima sina Oskara.

Dr. Ante Bruno Tandara (1937.–1989.) bio je hrvatski liječnik, saborski zastupnik i društveni djelatnik koji je aktivno sudjelovao u pokretu Hrvatskog proljeća krajem 1960-ih i početkom 1970-ih godina. Kao član hrvatskog reformnog pokreta zalagao se za veća prava Hrvatske unutar tadašnje jugoslavenske federacije, te je sudjelovao u radu Matice hrvatske i drugim javnim aktivnostima usmjerenima na promicanje hrvatskog nacionalnog i kulturnog identiteta.

Nakon sloma Hrvatskog proljeća 1971. godine bio je uhićen, osuđen i zatvoren zbog svojega političkog djelovanja. U hrvatskoj javnosti ostao je zapamćen kao jedan od istaknutih sudionika Hrvatskog proljeća i simbol političkih progona toga razdoblja.

The Second Branch of the First TANDARA Family Line

Petar Tandara (1734–1817)

Petar Tandara was undoubtedly a son of Ivan Tandarić, most likely his second or third son, depending on the correlation with Filip from Livno as one of Ivan’s possible descendants. He died in 1817 at the age of eighty-three, which suggests that he was born around 1734.

Petar was married to Jela Budić. They had sons named Grgo, Jakov, Martin, Marijan, Mijo, Tadija, and Marko, as well as daughters Manda and Jela. The sons Jakov, Marijan, and Marko do not appear in the 1806 Census of Souls, while Tadija is absent from the 1819 census, indicating that they had either died or moved away from Ričice.

Only the sons Grgo, Martin, and Mijo continued the family line.

Grgo Petrov (1770– )

Petar’s eldest son married Ruža Pušić. They had a son, Šimun, and a daughter, Iva. Since Šimun had no male heir, Grgo’s branch of the family eventually became extinct.

Martin Petrov (1775–1844)

Martin, Petar’s son, married Ruža Lekić. They had sons Ivan, Jure, Mijo, Grgo, Filip, and Josip, as well as daughters Matija and Ana.

Apart from their baptismal records, no further information has been found about Jure, Filip, or Josip, so it remains unknown whether they died young or moved away. Only the sons Ivan, Mijo, and Grgo continued the family lineage.

Mijo Petrov (birth and death years unknown)

Mijo Petrov was married to Ruža, whose maiden name is unknown. They had a daughter, Matija. As they had no male heir, Mijo’s branch of the family came to an end.

Ivan Martinov (1807–1877)

Martin’s eldest son, Ivan, married Antica Lažeta in 1826. Ivan and Antica had sons named Marijan, Toma, and Jure, as well as daughters Ruža, Iva, Joza, and Matija. Marijan died in early childhood, while no records of Jure have been found other than his birth entry. Toma was the only one to continue Ivan’s family line.

Mijo Martinov (1814– )

Martin’s third son, together with his wife Matija (née Parlov), had sons Martin and Mijo (who died in early childhood), and daughters Luca, Manda, Boža, Joza, Marija, and Matija. No records have been found concerning Martin other than his birth, so it is unknown whether he died young or moved away. With him, Mijo’s branch of the family came to an end.

Grgo Martinov (1818– )

Martin’s fourth son married twice. From his first marriage to Rosa (née Budimir), he had a son, Lovro, and a daughter, Jaka. In his second marriage, to Jaka (née Knežević), Grgo had sons Mate (who died as a small child) and Mijo, as well as daughters Matija, Joza, and Veronika.

THE TROMIĆ FAMILY

Toma Ivanov (1830– )

Toma was Ivan’s second son and was married to Tomica Kovač. They had sons Ivan, Mate, Jure, and another Ivan, who died in infancy. Of all their sons, only Jure left descendants. Toma’s descendants in Zavelim were widely known by the nickname “Tromići.” According to local tradition, they were exceptionally tall and moved rather slowly, which earned them this nickname. The following section focuses solely on Jure’s branch, as he was Toma’s only heir.

LOVRO IN RIČICE

In 1819, the family of Martin Tandara, Lovro’s grandfather, was recorded in Zavelim. The census also lists Martin’s sons Ivan, Mijo, and Grgo. When the Austrian cadastral survey was conducted in 1835, Martin Tandara, son of the late Petar, was registered as a landowner in Ričice. At that time, most clans from Ričice owned property not only there but also in Zavelim, Podbila, and Vir. These villages belonged to the same parish centered in Ričice. Many families within the same kinship group moved from one locality to another, sometimes even within Ričice itself. As the population grew rapidly, new places for settlement became necessary. In this context, it is understandable that a family, or part of it, could appear in one census in Ričice and in the next one in Zavelim, or vice versa.

Lovro Grgin (1842–1922)

Grgo’s eldest son married Iva Parlov. Together they had sons Ante, Ivan, and Marijan, and daughters Iva and Luca. No records have been found concerning Ivan other than his birth entry. The Parish Register of Deaths of Ričice notes that Lovro’s daughter Luca was “killed by falling rocks” at the age of thirteen.

Mijo Grgin (1856–1933)

Grgo’s third son married Iva Tandara. They had sons Martin (who died at the age of nineteen), Nikola, Mate (who died in early childhood), and another Nikola, as well as daughters Iva and Mara. No records have been found for the younger Nikola apart from his birth, while the elder Nikola died as a child. None of Mijo’s children produced descendants who continued the family line.

Jure Tomin (1874–1931)

Toma’s third son and only heir married Iva Lažeta. They had sons Toma and Ivan, both of whom died in early childhood, followed by Pio, Dominik, and Mate, and a daughter named Matija.

Pio Jurin (1916–1990)

Jure’s third son married Terezija Budimir. They had sons Ivan, Jure, and Joza, and a daughter Mara. Their eldest son Ivan (1936–2025), together with his wife Marijana Petter, had a son, Dino, and a daughter, Romana. Dino and his wife Matilda Klepikapić have daughters Hedviga, Leonarda, and Lucija. The family now lives in Zagreb.

Dominik Jurin (1919–1987)

Jure’s fourth son married Mara Knezović. They had sons Ante, Bariša, Nikola, Pio, Sveto, and Ivan, as well as a daughter, Olga. Only their son Ivan had male descendants, his sons Dominik and David, who continue Dominik’s branch of the family.

Mate Jurin (1922–1963)

Jure’s fifth son married Mara Budić. They had sons Tomislav, Mijo, and another son whose name is unknown, as well as a daughter named Matija. The two youngest sons died at the age of five. Tomislav married Božica Golubiček, and they have sons Martin and Mate. Mate later married Ivana Trošelj, with whom he has a daughter, Tena. Today, the descendants of Mate Jurin live in Zagreb.

Ante’s fifth son married Tomica Budimir. They had sons Ante (who died in early childhood), Drago, Jerko, and Ivan (who also died as a small child), along with daughters Matija, Branka, and Marija. The sons of Drago continue to live in Ričice.

Lovro Ivanov (1925– )

Lovro was Ivan’s eldest son. Together with his wife Rozika Peška, he had two daughters, Štefica and Ana. There are no male descendants to continue his branch of the family.

Mirko Ivanov (1928– )

Ivan’s second son married Danica Lukić. They had two sons, Ivan and Veselko, and a daughter, Mirjana. Mirko’s elder son Ivan married Nada Kegalj, and they have two sons, Robert and Kristijan. The family lives in Predavac near Bjelovar. His younger son Veselko married Martina Hohmann, and they have a son, Mirko, and a daughter, Janina.

Mate Ivanov (1932–1970)

Ivan’s third son married Marija Srebrnjak. They had only one child, a daughter named Sandra. He left no male descendants.

Ante Vlado Ivanov (1934– )

Ivan’s fourth son married twice. In his first marriage, to Karina Mesch, he had two sons, Johan and Franz, and a daughter, Veronika. His elder son Johan and his wife Katarina Koniks have a son, Gerd, and a daughter, Madita. In his second marriage, to Tonka Zanne, he had no children.

Ante Bruno Tandara Matin (1937–1989)

Dr. Bruno was born on 7 October 1937 in Ričice. In 1968, he married Vanja Brkljača, and together they had a son, Zvonimir, and two daughters, Zrinka and Tomislava. Zvonimir and his wife Aneta Gacek have a son named Oskar.

Dr. Ante Bruno Tandara (1937–1989) was a Croatian physician, member of parliament, and public figure who actively participated in the Croatian Spring movement during the late 1960s and early 1970s. As part of the Croatian reform movement, he advocated for greater rights for Croatia within the then Yugoslav federation and took part in the activities of Matica hrvatska and other public initiatives promoting Croatian national and cultural identity.

Following the suppression of the Croatian Spring in 1971, he was arrested, convicted, and imprisoned because of his political activities. He remains remembered in Croatian public life as one of the prominent participants in the Croatian Spring and as a symbol of the political persecution of that era.

Frane Matin (1947– )

Mate’s younger son, Frane, married Matija Topalušić. They have two sons, Mate and Ante, and a daughter, Elza. Their son Mate married Ana Marija Vlahović, and they have two sons, Ante and Frane, as well as a daughter, Leonarda. The family lives in Ričice.

Drago Nikolin (1952– )

Nikola’s second son, together with his wife Zdenka Budimir, had two sons, Nikola and Ivica. Ivica and his wife Jelena Perić have a son, Nikola, and a daughter, Laura. They live in Ričice.

Jerko Nikolin (1953– )

Petar Marijanov (1915– )

Marijan’s third son married Zorka (née Pavić). They had one son, Ante, who died in his second year of life, and daughters Mila, Marija, Ankica, Mira, Zorka, and Rosa. Petar left no male heir to continue his branch of the family.

Vinko Mijin (1930– )

Mijo’s eldest son married Mara Dragica Budimir. They had sons Milenko, who died at the age of one, and Svetko, as well as daughters Dragica and Milosava. Svetko married Nevenka Tavra, and they have a son named Kristijan.

Milan Mijin (1938– )

Milan married Stana Tandara. They had three sons—Nediljko, Željko, and Tihomir—and one daughter, Slavica.

Nediljko and his wife Vesna Mandić have one son, Milan, and two daughters, Ana Marija and Elizabeta.

Željko and his wife Valerija Glavota have three daughters: Mihaela, Marija, and Stana.

Tihomir and his wife Anica Miloš have one son, Milan, and one daughter, Ivana.

Marijan Mijin (1942– )

Marijan married Milenka Budimir. They have one son, Mario, and one daughter, Silvana. However, his branch of the family has no male descendants to carry on the lineage.

Alojz Marinov (1946– )

Alojz married Dragica Jurčević. They have one son, Robert, and one daughter, Zrinka. Robert and his wife Sabine Patzak have a son, Marc Marin, and two daughters, Caroline Victoria and Laura Nadine.

Blaž Marinov (1953– )

Blaž married Zorana Čarija. They had three sons—Mario, Ante, and Vice. Mario and his wife Josipa Artić have a son, Lovro, and a daughter, Tonka. Ante and his wife Tanja Koren have a son named Petar.
