Druga grana prve obitelji TANDARA

Petar Tandara(1734-1817)

Petar Tandara bio je nesumnjivo sin Ivana Tandarića, najvjerojatnije drugi ili treći po redu, ovisno o korelaciji s livanjskim Filipom kao jednim od potencijalnih Ivanovih potomaka. Umro je 1817. godine u osamdeset i trećoj godini života, pa se procjenjuje da je rođen oko 1734. godine.

Petar je bio oženjen Jelom Budić. Imali su sinove Grgu, Jakova, Martina, Marijana, Miju, Tadiju i Marka te kćeri Mandu i Jelu. Sinovi Jakov, Marijan i Marko ne pojavljuju se na Popisu duša iz 1806. godine, a Tadija je izostavljen s popisa iz 1819. godine, pa se može pretpostaviti da više nisu bili među živima ili je netko od njih odselio iz Ričica.

Sinovi Grgo, Martin i Mijo jedini su nastavili lozu.

Grgo Petrov (1770- )

Najstariji Petrov sin oženio se Ružom Pušić. Imali su sina Šimuna i kćer Ivu. Šimun nije imao muškog nasljednika tako da se Grgina grana ugasila.

Martin Petrov (1775-1844)

Petrov sin Martin oženio se Ružom Lekić. Imali su sinove Ivana, Juru, Miju, Grgu, Filipa i Josipa te kćeri Matiju i Anu.

Za Juru, Filipa i Josipa nisu pronađeni drugi zapisi osim onih o njihovu krštenju, pa nije poznato jesu li umrli mladi ili su se odselili. Sinovi Ivan, Mijo i Grgo su jedini nastavili lozu.

Mijo Petrov (nema g. rođenja i smrti)

Mijo Petrov bio je oženjen Ružom nepoznatog djevojačkog prezimena. Imali su kćer Matiju. Muškog nasljednika nisu imali pa se Mijina grana ugasila.

Ivan Martinov (1807-1877)

Najstariji Martinov sin godine 1826. oženio se Anticom Lažeta. Ivan i Antica imali su sinove Marijana, Tomu i Juru, te kćeri Ružu, Ivu, Jozu i Matiju. Marijan je umro kao malo dijete. O Juri nisu pronađeni drugi podaci osim zapisa o njegovu rođenju. Toma je bio jedini nasljednik Ivanove loze.

Mijo Martinov (1814- )

Treći Martinov sin i njegova žena Matija rođ. Parlov imali su sinove Martina i Miju (koji je umro kao malo dijete), te kćeri Lucu, Mandu, Božu, Jozu, Mariju i Matiju. O Martinu nema drugih zapisa osim onoga o njegovu rođenju. Ne zna se da li je umro ili se odselio. Nakon Martina Mijina se loza okončala.

Grgo Martinov (1818- )

Četvrti Martinov sin ženio se dvaput. Iz prvog braka s Rosom rođ. Budimir imao je sina Lovru i kćer Jaku. U drugom braku s Jakom rođ. Knežević, Grgo je imao sinove Matu (koji je umro kao malo dijete) i Miju, te kćeri Matiju, Jozu i Veroniku.

TROMIĆI

Toma Ivanov (1830- )

Toma je drugi Ivanov sin, a bio je oženjen Tomicom Kovač. Imali su sinove Ivana, Matu, Juru i ponovo Ivana koji je umro kao dijete. Od svih jedino Jure je imao potomstvo. Tomini potomci bili su u Zavelimu prepoznatljivi pod nadimkom Tromići. Prema predaji bili su izrazito visoki i spori, odnosno tromo su se kretali. Po tim svojim atributima dobili su nadimak Tromići. U nastavku će se obrađivati samo Jurina loza koji je jedini Tomin nasljednik.

LOVRO U RIČICAMA

Godine 1819. obitelj Martina Tandare, Lovrina djeda, bila je u Zavelimu. Na tom popisu su i Martinovi sinovi Ivan, Mijo i Grgo. Kad se 1835. radio austrijski katastar, Martin Tandara pok. Petra, upisan je kao posjednik nekretnina u Ričicama. Većina ričićkih plemena imala je u to vrijeme nekretnine u Ričicama, Zavelimu, Podbiloj i Viru. Navedena sela imala su zajedničku župu u Ričicama. Mnoge obitelji unutar jednog roda su selile s jednog lokaliteta na drugi, čak i u samim Ričicama. Broj stanovnika je naglo rastao i tražili su se novi prostori za život. U tom kontekstu možemo razumjeti i prihvatiti činjenicu da je neka obitelj, ili njezin dio, kod jednog popisa bila u Ričicama, a kod sljedećeg u Zavelimu ili obrnuto.

Lovro Grgin (1842-1922)

Najstariji Grgin sin oženio se Ivom Parlov i s njom imao sinove Antu, Ivana i Marijana, te kćeri Ivu i Lucu. O Ivanu nema drugih podataka osim zapisa o njegovu rođenju. U Matičnoj knjizi umrlih župe Ričice zapisano je da su Lovrinu kćer Lucu u trinaestoj godini života „ubile stine“.

Mijo Grgin (1856-1933)

Treći Grgin sin, oženio se Ivom Tandara. Imali su sinove Martina (koji je umro u dobi od 19 godina), Nikolu i Matu (koji je umro kao malo dijete) i ponovo Nikolu, te kćeri Ivu i Maru. O Nikoli mlađem nisam našao drugih podataka osim zapisa o njegovu rođenju dok je stariji Nikola preminuo kao dijete. Mijina djeca nisu imala nasljednike loze.

Jure Tomin (1874-1931)

Treći Tomin sin i jedini nasljednik uzeo je za ženu Ivu Lažeta. Imali su sinove Tomu i Ivana (koji su umrli kao mala djeca), zatim Pia, Dominika i Matu, te kćer Matiju.

Pio Jurin (1916-1990)

Treći Jurin sin uzeo je za suprugu Tereziju Budimir sa kojom su dobili sinove Ivana, Juru i Jozu, te kćer Maru. Najstariji sin Ivan (1936-2025) sa suprugom Marijanom Petter ima sina Dina i kćer Romanu. Dino sa suprugom Matildom Klepikapić ima kćeri Hedvigu, Leonardu i Luciju. Obitelj danas živi u Zagrebu.

Dominik Jurin (1919-1987)

Četvrti Jurin sin sa suprugom Marom Knezović je dobio sinove Antu, Barišu, Nikolu, Pia, Svetu i Ivana, te kćer Olgu. Samo sin Ivan ima sinove Dominika i Davida koji nastavljaju Dominu granu.

Mate Jurin (1922-1963)

Peti Jurin sin oženio je Maru Budić sa kojom je dobio sinove Tomislava, Miju i........, te kćer Matiju. Dva najmlađa sina su umrli u petoj godini života. Tomislav je oženio Božicu Golubiček sa kojom ima sinove Martina i Matu. Mate se oženio sa suprugom Ivanom Trošelj sa kojom je dobio kćer Tenu. Potomci Mate Jurina danas žive u Zagrebu.

Mate Ivanov (1932-1970)

Ante Bruno Tandara Matin (1937-1989)

Doktor Bruno rođen je 7. listopada 1937. u Ričicama. Godine 1968. oženio se Vanjom Brkljača i s njom dobio sina Zvonimira, te kćeri Zrinku i Tomislavu. Zvonimir sa suprugom Anetom Gacek ima sina Oskara.

Dr. Ante Bruno Tandara (1937.–1989.) bio je hrvatski liječnik, saborski zastupnik i društveni djelatnik koji je aktivno sudjelovao u pokretu Hrvatskog proljeća krajem 1960-ih i početkom 1970-ih godina. Kao član hrvatskog reformnog pokreta zalagao se za veća prava Hrvatske unutar tadašnje jugoslavenske federacije, te je sudjelovao u radu Matice hrvatske i drugim javnim aktivnostima usmjerenima na promicanje hrvatskog nacionalnog i kulturnog identiteta.

Nakon sloma Hrvatskog proljeća 1971. godine bio je uhićen, osuđen i zatvoren zbog svojega političkog djelovanja. U hrvatskoj javnosti ostao je zapamćen kao jedan od istaknutih sudionika Hrvatskog proljeća i simbol političkih progona toga razdoblja.

The Second Branch of the First TANDARA Family Line

Petar Tandara (1734–1817)

Petar Tandara was undoubtedly a son of Ivan Tandarić, most likely his second or third son, depending on whether Filip of Livno is identified as another of Ivan’s descendants. Since Petar died in 1817 at the age of eighty-three, his birth is estimated to have occurred around 1734.

Petar was married to Jela Budić. They had seven sons—Grgo, Jakov, Martin, Marijan, Mijo, Tadija, and Marko—and two daughters, Manda and Jela. The sons Jakov, Marijan, and Marko do not appear in the 1806 Census of Souls, while Tadija is absent from the 1819 census, suggesting that they had either died or moved away from Ričice.

Of Petar’s sons, only Grgo, Martin, and Mijo continued the family line.

Grgo (son of Petar) (1770– )

Grgo, the eldest son of Petar, married Ruža Pušić. They had one son, Šimun, and one daughter, Iva. As Šimun left no male heir, Grgo’s branch of the family eventually became extinct.

Martin (son of Petar) (1775–1844)

Martin, a son of Petar, married Ruža Lekić. Together they had six sons—Ivan, Jure, Mijo, Grgo, Filip, and Josip—and two daughters, Matija and Ana.

Apart from their baptismal records, no additional information has been found about Jure, Filip, or Josip, so it remains unknown whether they died young or moved away. Only Ivan, Mijo, and Grgo continued Martin’s family line.

Mijo (son of Petar) (birth and death years unknown)

Mijo, another son of Petar, was married to Ruža, whose maiden name is unknown. They had a daughter named Matija but no male heir, and consequently Mijo’s branch of the family came to an end.

Ivan (son of Martin) (1807–1877)

Ivan, the eldest son of Martin, married Antica Lažeta in 1826. They had three sons—Marijan, Toma, and Jure—and four daughters: Ruža, Iva, Joza, and Matija. Marijan died in early childhood, while no records concerning Jure have been found beyond his birth entry. Toma was the only son to continue Ivan’s family line.

Mijo (son of Martin) (1814– )

Mijo, Martin’s third son, married Matija (née Parlov). They had two sons, Martin and Mijo, the latter of whom died in early childhood, together with six daughters: Luca, Manda, Boža, Joza, Marija, and Matija.

No records have been discovered concerning Martin other than his birth, so it is unknown whether he died young or left the area. With him, Mijo’s branch of the family appears to have come to an end.

Grgo (son of Martin) (1818– )

Grgo, Martin’s fourth son, married twice. In his first marriage, to Rosa (née Budimir), he had a son named Lovro and a daughter named Jaka. In his second marriage, to Jaka (née Knežević), he had two sons, Mate (who died in early childhood) and Mijo, along with three daughters: Matija, Joza, and Veronika.

THE TROMIĆ FAMILY

Toma (son of Ivan) (1830– )

Toma, Ivan’s second son, married Tomica Kovač. They had four sons—Ivan, Mate, Jure, and another Ivan, who died as an infant. Of these, only Jure left descendants.

Toma’s descendants in Zavelim became widely known by the nickname Tromići. According to local tradition, they were exceptionally tall and notably slow in their movements, from which the nickname originated. The following account focuses exclusively on Jure’s branch, as he was Toma’s sole successor in the male line.

LOVRO IN RIČICE

In 1819, the household of Martin Tandara, Lovro’s grandfather, was recorded in Zavelim. The census also lists Martin’s sons Ivan, Mijo, and Grgo. When the Austrian cadastral survey was conducted in 1835, Martin Tandara, son of the late Petar, was registered as a landowner in Ričice.

At that time, most families from the Ričice clan owned property not only in Ričice but also in Zavelim, Podbila, and Vir. These settlements belonged to the same parish centred in Ričice. It was common for branches of the same extended family to relocate between neighbouring villages, and sometimes even within Ričice itself, as population growth created the need for additional land and new places to settle.

In this context, it is entirely understandable that a family—or part of one—might appear in one census in Ričice and in the next in Zavelim, or vice versa.

Lovro (son of Grgo) (1842–1922)

Lovro, the eldest son of Grgo, married Iva Parlov. Together they had three sons—Ante, Ivan, and Marijan—and two daughters, Iva and Luca. No records concerning Ivan have been found other than his birth entry. The Parish Register of Deaths in Ričice records that Lovro’s daughter Luca was “killed by falling rocks” at the age of thirteen.

Mijo (son of Grgo) (1856–1933)

Mijo, Grgo’s third son, married Iva Tandara. They had four sons: Martin, who died at the age of nineteen; Nikola; Mate, who died in early childhood; and another son who was also named Nikola. They also had two daughters, Iva and Mara.

No records have been found for the younger Nikola beyond his birth entry, while the elder Nikola died as a child. None of Mijo’s descendants continued this branch of the family line.

Jure (son of Toma) (1874–1931)

Jure, the third son of Toma and his only male heir, married Iva Lažeta. They had five sons—Toma and Ivan, both of whom died in early childhood, followed by Pio, Dominik, and Mate—as well as a daughter named Matija.

Pio (son of Jure) (1916–1990)

Pio, Jure’s third son, married Terezija Budimir. They had three sons, Ivan, Jure, and Joza, together with a daughter, Mara.

Their eldest son, Ivan (1936–2025), and his wife Marijana Petter had a son, Dino, and a daughter, Romana. Dino and his wife Matilda Klepikapić have three daughters: Hedviga, Leonarda, and Lucija. The family now resides in Zagreb.

Dominik (son of Jure) (1919–1987)

Dominik, Jure’s fourth son, married Mara Knezović. They had six sons—Ante, Bariša, Nikola, Pio, Sveto, and Ivan—and one daughter, Olga.

Of these, only their son Ivan had male descendants. His sons, Dominik and David, continue Dominik’s branch of the Tandara family.

Mate (son of Jure) (1922–1963)

Mate, Jure’s fifth son, married Mara Budić. They had sons Tomislav, Mijo, and another son whose name is unknown from the available records, as well as a daughter named Matija. The two youngest sons died at the age of five.

Tomislav married Božica Golubiček, and they have two sons, Martin and Mate. Mate later married Ivana Trošelj, and they have a daughter named Tena. Today, the descendants of Mate (son of Jure) live in Zagreb.

CONTINUATION OF THE LARGEST BRANCH OF THE TANDARA FAMILY – THE LINE OF LOVRO (1842–1922)

As mentioned earlier, Lovro had two sons, Ante and Marijan, who were the only ones to carry this branch of the family forward. Together, their sons went on to have thirty-three children.

Ante (son of Lovro) (1871–1933)

Ante married Tomica Lukić. They had four sons—Ivan, Marijan, Mate, and Nikola—and three daughters: Marta, Iva, and Matija.

Marijan (son of Lovro) (1881– )

Marijan married Luca Parlov. Together they had four sons—Mijo, Marin, Petar, and Ivan, who died in early childhood—and four daughters: Matija, Iva, Ana, and Tomica.

THE BRANCH OF ANTE (SON OF LOVRO) (1871–1933)

Ivan (son of Ante) (1896– )

Ivan, Ante’s eldest son, married Mara Mršić. They had five sons—Lovro, Mirko, Mate, Ante Vlado, and Nikola—and one daughter, Tomica.

Ivan later moved to Slatina. Today, his descendants live in Predavac near Bjelovar, Slatina, Obrovac, Zagreb, and Germany.

Marijan (son of Ante) (1898– )

Ante’s second son married Milica (née Mikulić). They had two sons, Frane, who was killed in Vienna in 1945 at the age of eighteen, and Ante, who died in early childhood, as well as two daughters, Marija and Stana.

Marijan died of pneumonia at the age of thirty-five. His branch of the family did not continue beyond his own children.

Mate (son of Ante) (1910–1991) – Father of Dr. Bruno Tandara

Mate, Ante’s fourth son, married Mara Knežević. They had two sons, Ante Bruno—better known as Dr. Bruno Tandara—and Frane, as well as three daughters: Ana, Dinka, and Olga.

Nikola (son of Ante) (1919–1981)

Nikola, Ante’s fifth son, married Tomica Budimir. They had four sons: Ante, who died in early childhood; Drago; Jerko; and Ivan, who also died as a young child. They also had three daughters: Matija, Branka, and Marija.

The descendants of Drago continue to live in Ričice.

Lovro (son of Ivan) (1925– )

Lovro, Ivan’s eldest son, married Rozika Peška. They had two daughters, Štefica and Ana. As there were no male descendants, this branch of the family line ended with him.

Mirko (son of Ivan) (1928– )

Mirko, Ivan’s second son, married Danica Lukić. They had two sons, Ivan and Veselko, and one daughter, Mirjana.

The elder son, Ivan, married Nada Kegalj, and they have two sons, Robert and Kristijan. The family lives in Predavac near Bjelovar.

The younger son, Veselko, married Martina Hohmann. They have one son, Mirko, and one daughter, Janina.

Mate (son of Ivan) (1932–1970)

Mate, Ivan’s third son, married Marija Srebrnjak. They had only one child, a daughter named Sandra. He left no male descendants.

Ante Vlado (son of Ivan) (1934– )

Ante Vlado married twice. In his first marriage, to Karina Mesch, he had two sons, Johan and Franz, and a daughter, Veronika.

Johan and his wife Katarina Koniks have one son, Gerd, and one daughter, Madita. Ante Vlado had no children from his second marriage to Tonka Zanne.

Ante Bruno Tandara (son of Mate) (1937–1989)

Dr. Ante Bruno Tandara was born on 7 October 1937 in Ričice. In 1968, he married Vanja Brkljača, and they had one son, Zvonimir, and two daughters, Zrinka and Tomislava. Zvonimir and his wife Aneta Gacek have one son, Oskar.

Dr. Ante Bruno Tandara (1937–1989) was a Croatian physician, Member of Parliament, and public figure who played an active role in the Croatian Spring movement during the late 1960s and early 1970s. As part of the Croatian reform movement, he advocated greater autonomy and rights for Croatia within the former Yugoslav federation and participated in the work of Matica hrvatska and other initiatives promoting Croatian national and cultural identity.

Following the suppression of the Croatian Spring in 1971, he was arrested, convicted, and imprisoned because of his political activities. He is remembered in Croatian history as one of the prominent participants in the movement and as a symbol of the political persecution of that period.

Frane (son of Mate) (1947– )

Frane, the younger son of Mate, married Matija Topalušić. They have two sons, Mate and Ante, and one daughter, Elza.

Their son Mate married Ana Marija Vlahović. They have two sons, Ante and Frane, and one daughter, Leonarda. The family lives in Ričice.

Drago (son of Nikola) (1952– )

Drago, Nikola’s second son, married Zdenka Budimir. They had two sons, Nikola and Ivica.

Ivica and his wife Jelena Perić have one son, Nikola, and one daughter, Laura. The family lives in Ričice.

Jerko (son of Nikola) (1953– )

Jerko, Nikola’s third son, married Stanka Racetin. They have one daughter, Ana, but no male descendants to continue the family line.

THE BRANCH OF MARIJAN (SON OF LOVRO) (1881–1939)

Mijo (son of Marijan) (1908– )

Mijo, the eldest son of Marijan, married Slavka (née Sablić). They had five sons—Vinko, Ivan, Milan, Marijan, and another Ivan, who died at the age of two—as well as four daughters: Mila-Iva, Anica, Nada, and Maca.

Today, the descendants of Vinko, Milan, and Marijan live in Ričice, Split, Zagreb, Austria, and Germany.

Marin (son of Marijan) (1912–1989)

Marin, Marijan’s second son, married Matija Žuljić in 1938. They had five sons: Marijan, who died at the age of two; Alojz; Luka, who died at the age of one; Vice and Blaž. They also had three daughters: Dragica, Nevenka, and Anka.

Vice (son of Marin) is married to Ružica Barnjak, and they have no children. Together with Milan Lažeta, Vice made one of the most significant contributions to the compilation and completion of the Genealogy of the Tandara Clan. I remain deeply and sincerely grateful to him for his invaluable assistance and dedication to this project.

Marin’s descendants today live in Ričice, Split, Zagreb, and Germany.

Petar (son of Marijan) (1915– )

Petar, Marijan’s third son, married Zorka (née Pavić). They had one son, Ante, who died in his second year of life, and six daughters: Mila, Marija, Ankica, Mira, Zorka, and Rosa.

Since Petar left no surviving male heir, his branch of the family line came to an end.

Vinko (son of Mijo) (1930– )

Vinko, the eldest son of Mijo, married Mara Dragica Budimir. They had two sons, Milenko, who died at the age of one, and Svetko, together with two daughters, Dragica and Milosava.

Svetko married Nevenka Tavra, and they have one son, Kristijan.

Milan (son of Mijo) (1938– )

Milan married Stana Tandara. Together they had three sons—Nediljko, Željko, and Tihomir—and one daughter, Slavica.

Nediljko and his wife Vesna Mandić have one son, Milan, and two daughters, Ana Marija and Elizabeta.

Željko and his wife Valerija Glavota have three daughters: Mihaela, Marija, and Stana.

Tihomir and his wife Anica Miloš have one son, Milan, and one daughter, Ivana.

Marijan (son of Mijo) (1942– )

Marijan married Milenka Budimir. They have one son, Mario, and one daughter, Silvana.

As Mario has no male descendants to continue the paternal line, Marijan’s branch does not continue through the male lineage.

Alojz (son of Marin) (1946– )

Alojz married Dragica Jurčević. They have one son, Robert, and one daughter, Zrinka.

Robert and his wife Sabine Patzak have one son, Marc Marin, and two daughters, Caroline Victoria and Laura Nadine.

Blaž (son of Marin) (1953– )

Blaž married Zorana Čarija. They have three sons: Mario, Ante, and Vice.

Mario and his wife Josipa Artić have one son, Lovro, and one daughter, Tonka. Ante and his wife Tanja Koren have one son, Petar.
