Contact & Collaboration

Collaboration Centre of the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family

If you bear the surname Tandara, descend from the Tandara family line or are researching this family, you can use this page to send information, additions, photographs, documents, questions or suggestions for the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family. You may also request that published information be removed, concealed or replaced with updated information.

Contact form

You can send your message directly using this form. If you are requesting access to the private archive, select the appropriate type of inquiry and name a person who can confirm your identity or family relationship. Submitting a request or confirming an e-mail address does not automatically grant access: each request is reviewed separately and access is granted only to properly identified persons.

⚠️ Important notice:
Please complete all required fields and select answers to the questions about public publication of the message and publication of your name and place. For a request to access the private archive, provide accurate relationship details and the name of a person who can confirm your identity. The form will not be sent until all required fields have been completed.

How can you help?

The Digital Archive grows with the help of everyone who preserves a part of family history. Especially valuable are:

  • information about ancestors, descendants and family connections,
  • information about belonging to Jure’s, Petar’s, Ante’s or Mate’s branch,
  • old family photographs, letters, documents and registry records,
  • information about emigration, places of residence and family migrations,
  • family stories, memories, nicknames, occupations and customs,
  • corrections or additions to already published information.

What to include in your message

To make the message easier to process and correctly connect with the archive, it is useful to include:

  • your name and surname,
  • the city and country from which you are writing,
  • an e-mail address for reply,
  • how you are connected with the Tandara surname,
  • which family branch you belong to, if known,
  • the type and reason for your inquiry,
  • if you are requesting private access, the name of a person who can confirm your identity or family relationship,
  • whether you want your message to be published in the Visitors’ Messages section.

Photographs and documents can be sent as e-mail attachments. If you are not sure what is important, send even what may seem like a small detail — in genealogy, such traces often open important connections.

Permission for publication

Messages, photographs and documents will not be publicly published without the sender’s permission. If you want your message to be published in the Visitors’ Messages section, you can clearly write:

"I give permission for my message to be published in the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family."

If you are sending the message only for research purposes and do not want public publication, it is enough to write:

"The message is for research purposes only and is not for public publication."

Before sending personal information

Please send only the information needed for your inquiry or for verification of a genealogical relationship. Sensitive information is not published automatically, and detailed rules on public and protected data are provided in the Privacy Policy.

If you are requesting the correction or removal of information, please identify the person or entry concerned as precisely as possible so that the change can be reviewed without affecting unrelated parts of the genealogy.

What happens after your message

Each message is first reviewed and classified according to the type of inquiry. If it contains new genealogical information, it is compared with existing records and, where necessary, the sender is asked for additional explanation or supporting material.

Messages intended for the Visitors’ Messages page are published only with the author’s permission, while research information is incorporated into the archive only after review. This keeps the Contact page focused on collaboration and the Genealogy section focused on verified family information.
