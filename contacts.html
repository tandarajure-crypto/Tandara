Contact & Collaboration

Collaboration Center of the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family Line

If you bear the surname Tandara, descend from the Tandara family line, or are researching the history of this family, you are invited to become part of this shared archive through your knowledge, documents and family stories.

The purpose of this page is not only to establish contact, but also to connect people bearing the surname, descendants and researchers from Croatia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Europe and the whole world. Every photograph, document, family memory or correction of information can help preserve the history of the Tandara family line for future generations.

Project e-mail address

A separate e-mail address has been opened for all messages related to this project, so that correspondence concerning the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family Line remains separate from private mail:

tandarajure@outlook.com

You may use this address to send family information, genealogical additions, photographs, documents, questions, suggestions and messages for the future visitors’ messages page.

How you can help

The Digital Archive grows with the help of everyone who preserves a part of family history. Especially valuable are:

  • information about ancestors, descendants and family connections,
  • information about belonging to the Jurina, Petrova, Antina or Matina branch,
  • old family photographs, letters, documents and registry records,
  • information about emigration, places of residence and family migrations,
  • family stories, memories, nicknames, occupations and customs,
  • corrections or additions to already published information.

What to include in your message

To make it easier to process your message and correctly connect it with the archive, it is useful to include:

  • your name and surname,
  • the place and country from which you are writing,
  • an e-mail address for reply,
  • how you are connected with the Tandara surname,
  • which family branch you belong to, if known,
  • whether you would like your message to be published on the visitors’ messages page.

Photographs and documents may be sent as e-mail attachments. If you are not sure what is important, send even what may seem like a small detail — in genealogy, such traces often open important connections.

Permission to publish

Messages, photographs and documents will not be published publicly without the sender’s permission. If you want your message to be published on the visitors’ messages page, you may clearly write in your e-mail:

"I give permission for my message to be published in the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family Line."

If the message is intended only for research and you do not want public publication, simply write:

"This message is for research purposes only and is not for public publication."

Privacy

Private e-mail addresses, telephone numbers and personal information about living persons will not be published without explicit consent. Information received through this page is used only for communication, verification of genealogical links and development of the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family Line.

Together we preserve the history of the Tandara family line

The history of one family is not only a list of names and dates. It is a story about people, their lives, journeys and memories that deserve to be preserved.

Every family keeps one part of our shared history. Thank you for helping build the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family Line.
