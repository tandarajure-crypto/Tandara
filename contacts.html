Tandara Digital Family Archive Collaboration Centre

If you bear the surname Tandara, descend from the Tandara family, or are researching this family, you can use this page to send information, additions, photographs, documents, questions or suggestions for the Tandara Digital Family Archive.

You may also report incorrect or outdated information and request that it be reviewed, corrected, replaced or removed.

How You Can Help

The Digital Archive grows with the help of everyone who preserves part of the family's history. Particularly valuable contributions include:

  • information about ancestors, descendants and family relationships,
  • information about belonging to the Jure, Petar, Ante or Mate branch,
  • old family photographs, letters, documents and civil or parish records,
  • information about emigration, places of residence and family migration,
  • family stories, memories, nicknames, occupations and customs,
  • corrections or additions to information already published.

What to Include in Your Message

To make it easier to process your message and connect it correctly with the archive, it is helpful to provide:

  • your full name,
  • your city / place and country,
  • an e-mail address for a reply,
  • how you are connected with the Tandara surname,
  • your family branch, if known,
  • the type and reason for your request,
  • whether you want your message to be published in Visitor Messages.

If you are unsure what may be important, send even information that appears minor — in genealogy, such small clues can often reveal important connections.

Permission for Publication

Messages, photographs and documents will not be published publicly without the sender's permission.

If you indicate that you allow your message to be published, it may be added to Visitor Messages after review. Your e-mail address will not be published.

Before Sending Personal Information

Please send only information that is necessary for your request or for verification of a genealogical connection. Detailed rules are available in the Privacy Policy.

If you are requesting a correction or removal of information, please state precisely which person or record the request concerns so that the change can be reviewed without affecting unrelated parts of the genealogy.

What Happens After Your Message

Each message is first reviewed and classified according to the type of request. If it contains new genealogical information, it is compared with existing records and, where necessary, the sender may be asked to provide additional explanation or a source.

Messages intended for Visitor Messages are published only with the permission of their author, while new genealogical information is included in the archive only after verification.
