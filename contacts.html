Contact & Collaboration

Collaboration Centre of the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family

If you bear the surname Tandara, descend from the Tandara family line, or are researching this family, you can use this page to send information, additions, photographs, documents, questions or suggestions for the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family. You can also request that any published information be removed or substituted with updated details..

Contact form

You can send your message directly using the form below. If you are requesting access to the private archive, select the appropriate request type and name a person who can confirm your identity or family relationship. Submitting a request and confirming an e-mail address do not automatically grant access: every request is reviewed individually and access is granted only to validly identified and approved persons.

Important: All required fields must be completed, including the publication permission choices. For a private archive access request, provide accurate relationship details and name a person who can confirm your identity. The form cannot be submitted until all required information has been entered.

How can you help?

The Digital Archive grows with the help of everyone who preserves a part of family history. Especially valuable are:

  • information about ancestors, descendants and family connections,
  • information about belonging to the Jurina, Petrova, Antina or Matina branch,
  • old family photographs, letters, documents and registry records,
  • information about emigration, places of residence and family migrations,
  • family stories, memories, nicknames, occupations and customs,
  • corrections or additions to already published information.

What to include in your message

To make the message easier to process and correctly connect with the archive, it is useful to include:

  • your name and surname,
  • the city and country from which you are writing,
  • an e-mail address for reply,
  • how you are connected with the Tandara surname,
  • which family branch you belong to, if known,
  • the type and reason for your request,
  • if requesting private access, a person who can confirm your identity or family relationship,
  • whether you want your message to be published in the visitors' messages section.

Photographs and documents can be sent as e-mail attachments. If you are not sure what is important, send even what may seem like a small detail — in genealogy, such traces often open important connections.

Permission for publication

Messages, photographs and documents will not be publicly published without the sender's permission. If you want your message to be published in the visitors' messages section, you can clearly write:

"I give permission for my message to be published in the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family."

If you are sending the message only for research purposes and do not want public publication, it is enough to write:

"The message is for research purposes only and is not for public publication."

Privacy

Private e-mail addresses, telephone numbers and personal information about living persons will not be published without explicit consent. Public records include persons born in 1930 or earlier and persons born after 1930 whose death has been entered or confirmed. Records of persons born after 1930 without an entered or confirmed year of death are protected and are not available without prior verification and valid approval. Submitting the form, confirming an e-mail address or stating a family relationship does not itself grant access. Data received through this page are used exclusively for communication, identity and genealogical verification, and development of the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family. Further information is available in the Privacy Policy.

Together we preserve the history of the Tandara family line

The history of one family is not only a list of names and dates. It is a story about people, their lives, journeys and memories that deserve to be preserved.

Every family preserves a part of our shared history. Thank you for helping build the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family.
