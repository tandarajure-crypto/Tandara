Contact & Collaboration

Collaboration Center of the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family Line

This page is intended for everyone who wishes to contribute information, documents, photographs, memories or family stories related to the Tandara surname and family line.

Every message, even a short one, may help complete a missing part of the family history.

Project e-mail address

For the purpose of this project, a separate e-mail address is used so that correspondence related to the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family Line remains separate from private mail:

tandarajure@outlook.com

You may use this address to send family information, genealogical additions, photographs, documents, questions, suggestions and messages for the future visitors’ messages page.

How you can help

You can contribute by sending:

  • names and surnames of family members and ancestors,
  • dates and places of birth, marriage and death,
  • information about family branches, migrations and settlements,
  • old family photographs, documents, letters or records,
  • family stories, memories, nicknames and oral traditions,
  • corrections or additions to already published information.

Visitors’ Messages

Messages from members of the Tandara family, descendants, relatives and researchers may be included in a separate visitors’ messages page.

Open Visitors’ Messages

Permission to publish

Messages, photographs and documents will not be published publicly without the sender’s permission. If you want your message to be published in the visitors’ messages page, you may clearly write:

"I give permission for my message to be published in the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family Line."

If the message is intended only for research and you do not want public publication, simply write:

"This message is for research purposes only and is not for public publication."

Privacy

Private e-mail addresses, telephone numbers and personal information about living persons will not be published without explicit consent. Information received through this page is used only for communication, verification of genealogical links and development of the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family Line.

Together we preserve the history of the Tandara family line

The history of one family is not only a list of names and dates. It is a story about people, their lives, journeys and memories that deserve to be preserved.

Every family keeps one part of our shared history. Thank you for helping build the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family Line.
