TANDARA – SURNAME

Collaboration Centre of the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family

If you bear the Tandara surname, descend from the Tandara family line, or are researching the history of this family, you are warmly invited to contribute your knowledge, documents and family stories to this shared archive.

Project e-mail address

For all messages related to this project, please use the dedicated e-mail address:

tandarajure@outlook.com

This address is used only for the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family, so that project messages can be followed, reviewed and preserved separately from personal correspondence.

How can you help?

Genealogical information

Names of ancestors, dates of birth and death, places of residence, family connections, migrations and branches of the family line.

Photographs and documents

Old family photographs, registry records, letters, emigration documents, gravestone inscriptions and family memories.

Family stories

Memories of ancestors, old homes, customs, nicknames, occupations and events that shaped the family history.

Looking for relatives

If you are searching for a family connection or trying to identify your branch, please send the information you have.

Privacy and permission

Private e-mail addresses, telephone numbers and personal information about living persons will not be published without explicit permission. Photographs, documents and messages are published only after review and with the sender’s consent.

The history of a family is more than a list of names and dates. It is the story of people, their lives, journeys and memories that deserve to be preserved.
