TANDARA – SURNAME

Collaboration Centre of the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family

If you carry the Tandara surname, descend from a Tandara family branch, or are researching the history of this family, you are warmly invited to contribute your knowledge, documents and family stories to this shared archive.

Project e-mail address

All messages related to this project should be sent to the dedicated project address:

tandarajure@outlook.com

This address is used only for the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family, so project correspondence can be followed, reviewed, preserved and handled separately from private mail.

How can you contribute?

Genealogical information

Names of ancestors, dates of birth and death, places of residence, family connections, migrations and links to the Jurina, Petrova, Antina, Matina or another family branch.

Photographs and documents

Old family photographs, church or civil records, letters, school or military photographs, migration documents, gravestone inscriptions and personal memories.

Family stories

Memories of ancestors, stories about migrations, old family homes, customs, nicknames, occupations and events that shaped the family history.

Finding relatives

If you are searching for a family connection or wish to identify your branch, send the information you have. Even an incomplete clue may be valuable.

Send a message using a prepared template

Click one of the buttons below. Your e-mail program will open with prepared questions. Fill in only what you know and what you wish to share.

How will messages be handled?

  1. The message arrives at the project address tandarajure@outlook.com.
  2. The project author reads it, reviews the content and may ask for additional clarification.
  3. Genealogical information is used for research and for improving the archive only according to the permission provided.
  4. Public publication in the Visitors' Messages section is made only when the sender has clearly given consent.

Privacy and permission

Private e-mail addresses, phone numbers and personal data of living persons will not be published without explicit consent. Photographs, documents and messages are published only after review and with the sender's permission.

The project author reserves the right not to publish content that is unrelated to the project, insufficiently verifiable or could affect the privacy of other people.

Why your contribution matters

Every family preserves a part of our shared history. One photograph, one record or one memory can help connect dispersed branches of the Tandara family and preserve what might otherwise be forgotten.

The history of a family is more than a list of names and dates. It is the story of people, their lives, journeys and memories that deserve to be preserved.
