TANDARA – SURNAME

Collaboration Centre of the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family

If you carry the Tandara surname, descend from the Tandara family, or research its history, you are warmly invited to contribute your knowledge, documents and family stories to this shared archive.

Project e-mail address

All messages related to this project should be sent to the dedicated project e-mail:

tandarajure@outlook.com

This address is used only for the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family, so that project correspondence can be followed, checked, preserved and processed separately from personal mail.

How can you help?

Genealogical information

Names of ancestors, years of birth and death, places of residence, family links, migrations and connection to a known branch of the Tandara family.

Photographs and documents

Old family photographs, church or civil records, letters, school or military photographs, migration documents, gravestone inscriptions and memories.

Family stories

Memories of ancestors, stories about migration, old family homes, customs, nicknames, occupations and events that shaped the family.

Searching for relatives

If you are looking for a family connection or trying to identify your branch, send the information you have. Even a small clue may be valuable.

Privacy and permission

Private e-mail addresses, phone numbers and personal data of living persons will not be published without explicit permission. Photographs, documents and messages are published only after review and with the sender’s consent.

Why your contribution matters

Every family preserves a part of our shared history. One photograph, one record or one memory can help connect scattered branches of the Tandara family around the world.

The history of a family is more than a list of names and dates. It is the story of people, their lives, journeys and memories that deserve to be preserved.
