Contact & Collaboration

Collaboration Centre of the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family

If you bear the surname Tandara, descend from the Tandara family line, or are researching this family, you can use this page to send information, additions, photographs, documents, questions or suggestions for the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family.

Official project e-mail address: tandarajure@outlook.com

Contact form

You can send your message directly through this form. The message will be delivered to the official e-mail address of the Digital Archive of the Tandara Family: tandarajure@outlook.com.

✓ Thank you! Your message has been sent successfully. We will reply as soon as possible.

Fields marked with an asterisk are required. Photographs and documents can be sent later as attachments in e-mail communication.

How can you help?

  • genealogical information about ancestors, descendants and family connections,
  • information about belonging to the Jurina, Petrova, Antina or Matina branch,
  • old family photographs, letters, documents and registry records,
  • information about emigration, places of residence and family migrations,
  • family stories, memories, nicknames, occupations and customs,
  • corrections or additions to already published information.

What to include in your message

  • your name and surname,
  • city and country from which you are writing,
  • e-mail address for reply,
  • how you are connected with the Tandara surname,
  • which family branch you belong to, if known,
  • whether you want your message to be published in the visitors’ messages section.

Permission for publication

Messages, photographs and documents will not be publicly published without the sender’s permission. Private e-mail addresses, telephone numbers and personal information about living persons will not be published without explicit consent.
