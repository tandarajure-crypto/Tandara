FILIP’S SUB-BRANCH

Filip Tandara and Matija born Parlov

Filip Tandara: ★ unknown  –  ✝ unknown

Wife Matija born Parlov: ★ unknown  –  ✝ unknown

Filip was the second son of Ante Tandara and Matija born Brnas. Unlike his brother Ante, he remained in Ričice, where he continued his life. He married Matija Parlov.

They had sons Martin, Mate and Ivan, and daughters Joza, Jela, Jurka, Antica and Ruža.

Only the birth records of Ivan and Mate have been found, so it is assumed that they either died young or moved away from Ričice. The family line was continued by their son Martin.

Martin’s family line continued through his sons Jerko and Ilija. Their descendants later moved to Split, Bjelovar, Zagreb, Germany, Canada and other places.

How to use the diagram

  1. Enter the required term in the Name, code or detail field and select Find.
  2. Selecting a person’s name box opens the available details about that person and their family.
  3. The + or − symbol on a person’s box opens or closes their descendants.
  4. The Expand all, Collapse and Three generations buttons determine how many generations are displayed.
  5. Fit adjusts the complete diagram to the available area, while the + and − buttons zoom in or out.
  6. On a computer, drag the diagram with the mouse. On a phone or tablet, drag it with your finger.
  7. The View full screen button opens the prepared diagram area across the entire screen.
  8. Because many people share the same first name, enter the person’s name, father’s name and mother’s name for a precise match. You may also add the surname Tandara, but a first name and surname alone are often not enough to identify one person; the search includes both parents’ names.
