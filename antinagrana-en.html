THIRD BRANCH OF THE FIRST TANDARA FAMILY

3.0. Ante Tandara – son of Ivan (born c. 1735)

Ante Tandara was the third son of Ivan Tandara. Ante was approximately one year younger than Petar, which fits well with the known birth order of Ivan’s children. He married Matija Brnas, with whom he had sons Ante, Filip and Pavao, and daughters Jaka, Luca and Ruža. Apart from the record of Pavao’s birth, there is no further information about him, suggesting that he probably died very young or left Ričice before leaving any trace in the parish registers.

Ante’s lineage remained firmly connected to Ričice, where it developed most clearly during the 19th and 20th centuries. A larger number of bearers of the Tandara surname emerged from this core, which is why Ante’s line is considered one of the more developed and numerous among Ivan’s descendants. In later generations, individual members of this branch gradually began to move away—first to nearby settlements, and later much farther, to various European Union countries, as well as Canada, the United States and Australia. Despite this global dispersion, the original Ante line remained firmly rooted in Ričice, preserving the continuity of family belonging and the richest written record of its beginnings.

FIRST SUB-BRANCH OF ANTE, SON OF ANTE

3.1. Ante (1769.–1849.)

Ante, the eldest son of Ante Ivanov, married Matija Jukić in 1801. They had sons Lovro, Mijo and Petar, and daughters Pera, Ruža, Antica, Iva and Marija. There is no information about Petar other than his birth record, so it is assumed that he died young or moved away from Zavelim. The family line was continued by the sons Lovro and Mijo. Mijo’s descendants received the nickname Tucići, while Lovro’s descendants through his son Marijan received the nickname Lovrinovići.

According to the 1819 census, Ante lived in Zavelim, while his brother Filip lived in Ričice. Their descendants later spread throughout Croatia, Europe, the United States and Canada.
