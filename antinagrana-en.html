THIRD BRANCH OF THE FIRST TANDARA FAMILY

3.0. Ante Tandara – son of Ivan (born c. 1735 – )

Ante Tandara is considered the third son of Ivan Tandara. He was roughly one year younger than Petar, which fits well with the known birth order of Ivan’s children. He married Matija Brnas, with whom he had sons Ante, Filip and Pavao, and daughters Jaka, Luca and Ruža. Apart from the record of Pavao’s birth, no other information about him exists, suggesting that he likely died very young or left Ričice before leaving any trace in the parish books.

Ante’s lineage remained firmly rooted in Ričice, where it developed most clearly throughout the 19th and 20th centuries. A large number of Tandara surname bearers descend from this core, making Ante’s line one of the most numerous and developed among Ivan’s descendants. In later generations, members of this branch gradually began to move — first to nearby settlements, and eventually much farther, to various countries of the European Union, as well as Canada, the United States and Australia. Despite this global dispersion, the original Ante line remained deeply anchored in Ričice, preserving continuity of family identity and the richest written record of its beginnings.

Branch – Ante, grandson of Ivan

3.1. Ante (1769–1849), eldest son of Ante Ivanov

Ante, the eldest son of Ante Ivanov, married Matija Jukić in 1801. They had sons Lovro, Mijo and Petar, and daughters Pera, Ruža, Antica, Iva and Marija. There are no further records about Petar apart from his birth, so it is assumed he died young or moved away from Zavelim. The family line continued through sons Lovro and Mijo. Mijo’s descendants received the nickname Tucići, while Lovro’s descendants, through his son Marijan, became known as Lovrinovići.

According to the 1819 census, Ante lived in Zavelim, while his brother Filip lived in Ričice. Their descendants later spread throughout Croatia, Europe, the USA and Canada.

Križanovići – Descendants of Križan, son of Lovro

3.1.1.2. Križan, son of Lovro (1843–1910)

He married Pera Budimir. They had 15 children, seven of whom died before the age of ten. A notable detail is that his second son Ilija died from a bull goring. Descendants of Križan, through sons Mate, Ante, Ivan and Jure, carry the nickname Križanovići and live in Zagreb, Đakovo, Kutjevo, Bektež, Strizivojna, Vrpolje and several European countries.

3.1.1.2.1. Mate, son of Križan (1866–1948)

The eldest son of Križan, he married Iva Pirić. They had sons Mijo, Marijan and Jozo, and daughters Mara and Matija. Mijo never married. The family lives in Bektež near Kutjevo.

3.1.1.2.1.1. Marijan, son of Mate (1902–1974)

He married Anica Maras; they had sons Ante, Jozo, Mato and Ivan, and a daughter Mara. Jozo never married. The family lives in Bektež near Kutjevo.

3.1.1.2.1.1.1. Ante, son of Marijan (1934– )

In his first marriage with Elizabeta Miletić he had a son Darko (1958–) and a daughter Gordana. In his second marriage with Ana Kajtar he had no children. They live in Strizivojna.

3.1.1.2.1.1.1.1. Darko, son of Ante

In his first marriage with Angela he had sons Mario (1982–) and Markus (1987–). In his second marriage with Suzana he had a son Klaus Ante (2006–). Darko and his family live in Germany.

3.1.1.2.1.1.2. Mato, son of Marijan (1945– )

He married Anica Bogdanović; they had a son Alen (1969–) and a daughter Laura. Alen married Melita Pjela; they have a son Marin (1993–) and a daughter Ena (1996–). The family lives in Slavonski Brod.

The fourth son of Marijan, he married Ljiljana Cobović. They have a son Marijan and daughters Lana and Paulina. They live in Đakovo, where Ivan runs his own law office.

The youngest son of Mate and Iva Pirić, he married Jaka Kilić. They had sons Mijo, Jozo and Ivan, and a daughter Mara. Ivan never married. Jozo was killed in World War II, and Mijo’s family today lives in Bektež near Kutjevo.

He married Zorica, with whom he has three daughters: Marijana, Nikolina and ?. The family lives in Bektež near Kutjevo and is engaged in winemaking. They are a well‑known winemaking family with the Tandara wine brand.

Jure, son of Križan (1883–1947) – Đulko

He was the ninth son of Križan and married three times.

His first marriage was to Pera Matišić, who came from a well‑known Croatian musical family. From that marriage he had my grandfather Josip. Pera died in 1919 from the Spanish flu. His second marriage was to a girl from the Budić family. They had a son Martin, who was killed in 1942 near Bihać. His third marriage was to Joza Dragun, with whom he had no children.
