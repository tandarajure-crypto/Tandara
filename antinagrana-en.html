THE THIRD BRANCH OF THE FIRST TANDARA FAMILY

3.0. Ante Tandara – son of Ivan (born c. 1735)

Ante Tandara was the third son of Ivan Tandara. Ante was approximately one year younger than Petar, which fits well with the known order of Ivan’s children. He married Matija Brnas, with whom he had sons Ante, Filip and Pavao, and daughters Jaka, Luca and Ruža. Apart from the record of Pavao’s birth, no other information about him has been found, suggesting that he probably died at a very young age or left Ričice before leaving any trace in the parish registers.

Ante’s family line remained firmly connected to Ričice, where it developed most clearly during the nineteenth and twentieth centuries. A large number of people bearing the Tandara surname descended from this family nucleus, and Ante’s line is therefore considered one of the more developed and numerous lines among Ivan’s descendants. In later generations, individual members of this branch gradually began to move away—first to nearby settlements, and later much farther afield, to various countries of the European Union, as well as to Canada, the United States and Australia. Despite this worldwide dispersion, the original line descended from Ante remained firmly rooted in Ričice, preserving the continuity of family identity and the richest written record of its beginnings.

FIRST SUB-BRANCH OF ANTE, SON OF ANTE

3.1. Ante (1769.–1849.)

Ante, the eldest son of Ante, son of Ivan, married Matija Jukić in 1801. They had sons Lovro, Mijo and Petar, and daughters Pera, Ruža, Antica, Iva and Marija. No information about Petar has been found apart from his birth record, and it is therefore assumed that he died young or moved away from Zavelim. The family line was continued by the sons Lovro and Mijo. Mijo’s descendants acquired the nickname Tucići, while Lovro’s descendants, through his son Marijan, acquired the nickname Lovrinovići.

According to the 1819 census, Ante lived in Zavelim, while his brother Filip lived in Ričice. Their descendants later settled throughout Croatia, Europe, the United States and Canada.

3.1.1. Lovro, son of Ante (1811.–1903.)

He married Matija Sičenica. They had sons Marijan and Križan.

THE LINE OF KRIŽAN, SON OF LOVRO – THE KRIŽANOVIĆI

3.1.1.2. Križan, son of Lovro (1843.–1910.)

Križan married Pera Budimir. They had fifteen children, seven of whom died before reaching the age of ten. An interesting fact is that his second son, Ilija, was killed after being gored by a bull. Križan’s descendants, through his sons Mate, Ante, Ivan and Jure, bear the nickname Križanovići and today live in Zagreb, Đakovo, Kutjevo, Bektež, Strizivojna, Vrpolje and several European countries.

3.1.1.2.4. Jure, son of Križan (1883.–1947.) – nickname Đulko

Jure was Križan’s ninth son and married three times, a record within the Tandara family.

His first wife was Pera Matišić, who came from the well-known Croatian musical Matišić family. From this marriage, he had a son, Josip. Pera died of the Spanish flu in 1919, which was popularly known as "Grla." His second marriage was to a young woman from the Budić family. They had a son, Martin, who was killed near Bihać in 1942. His third marriage was to Joza Dragun, and they had no children.

3.1.1.2.4.1. Josip, son of Jure (1911.–1946.)

Josip married Anica Dragun. They had sons Ivan and Milan, and daughters Slava, Mara, Neda and Maca. He was killed in 1946 above the cooperative building in Zavelim.

THE LINE OF MIJO, SON OF ANTE – THE TUCIĆI

3.1.2. Mijo, son of Ante (1818.–1878.) – nickname Tucići

Mijo, the second son of Ante, married Manda Martinović. They had nine children: sons Jozo, Ante, Mate, Mijo and Petar, and daughters Iva, Matija, Ruža and Antica.

No information about their son Mijo has been found apart from his birth record. It is therefore assumed that he died at a young age or moved away to an unknown destination. The descendants of Jozo, Mate and Petar bear the nickname Tucići, which originated from the family members' reputation for romantic adventures.

SECOND SUB-BRANCH OF FILIP, SON OF ANTE

3.2. Filip, son of Ante

Filip was the second son of Ante, son of Ivan. Unlike his brother Ante, he remained in Ričice, where he continued his family line. He married Matija Parlov, with whom he had sons Martin, Mate and Ivan, and daughters Joza, Jela, Jurka, Antica and Ruža. Only birth records exist for Ivan and Mate, so it is assumed that they died young or moved away from Ričice.

3.2.1. Martin, son of Filip (1811.–)

Martin married Manda Manenica. They had sons Jerko, Ilija, Ivan, Martin and another son also named Ivan, as well as daughters Tomica, Lucija, another daughter also named Lucija, and Iva. Only Jerko and Ilija continued the family line.
