The Third Branch of the TANDARA Family Tree

Ante Tandara, son of Ivan (born around 1735 – )

Ante Tandara, probably about one year younger than Petar, was the son of Ivan Tandara. He married Matija Brnas, and they had three sons: Ante, Filip, and Pavao, and three daughters: Jaka, Luca, and Ruža. There is no additional information about Pavao apart from his birth record.

Sub-branch of Ante, son of Ante

Ante Antin

Ante, the eldest son of Ante Ivanov, married Matija Jukić in 1801. They had three sons: Lovro, Mijo, and Petar, and five daughters: Pera, Ruža, Antica, Iva, and Marija. There is no additional information about Petar apart from his birth record.

According to the 1819 population census, Ante lived in Zavelim, while his brother Filip lived in Ričice. Their descendants later spread across Croatia, Europe, and Canada.

Lovro Antin

He married Matija Sičenica. They had two sons: Marijan and Križan.

Marijan Lovrin

He married Matija Kovač. They had four sons: Jure, Marko, Stipan, and Jozo, and three daughters: Iva, Mara, and Matija.

There is no record of Stipan apart from his birth entry, so it is assumed he either died young or moved away.

The descendants of Marijan through his sons Marko and Jure are known by the nickname Lovrinovići. The nickname comes from Marijan’s father Lovro. Today they live in Popovača, Sarajevo, and Stobreč.

Marko Marijanov

He married Božica Pušić. They had one son, Mirko, and one daughter, Iva.

Mirko Markov

He married Anica Kliić. They had two sons, Milan and Vladimir, who died at the age of 14, and three daughters: Dragica, Marija, and Nediljka.

Milan Markov

He married Svjetlana Trifunović. They had two sons: Vladimir and Dalibor.

Vladimir Milanov married Claire Tacher. They have a son, Alexander James, and live in London.

Dalibor married Amela Nezirović. They have a son, Damir, and a daughter, Tea. They live in Sarajevo.

Križan Lovrin

He married Pera Budimir. They had 15 children, seven of whom died before the age of ten.

An interesting fact is that Križan’s second son Ilija was killed by a bull.

Descendants of Križan through his sons Mate, Ante, Ivan, and Jure are known as Križanovići, and today live in Zagreb, Đakovo, Kutjevo, Bektež, Strizivojna, Vrpolje, and several European countries.

Mate Križanov

The eldest son of Križan, he married Iva Pirić. They had three sons: Mijo, Marijan, and Jozo, and two daughters: Mara and Matija. Mijo never married.

Marijan Matin

Marijan married Anica Maras. They had four sons: Ante, Jozo, Mate, and Ivan, and one daughter, Mara.

Ante Marijanov

From his first marriage to Elizabeta Miletić, Ante had a son, Darko, and a daughter, Gordana. In his second marriage to Ana Kajtar, he had no children.

Darko and his family live in Germany.

Jozo Marijanov

Jozo never married.

Mate Marijanov

Mate married Anica Bogdanović. They had a son, Alen, and a daughter, Laura.

Alen and his family live in Slavonski Brod.

Ivan Marijanov

The fourth son of Marijan, Ivan married Ljiljana Cobović. They have a son, Marijan, and two daughters, Lana and Paulina.

The family lives in Đakovo, where Ivan runs his own law office.

Jozo Matin

The youngest son of Mate and Iva Pirić, Jozo married Jaka Kliić. They had three sons: Mijo, Jozo, and Ivan, and one daughter, Mara.

Jozo was killed during the Second World War. The family of his son Mijo now lives in Bektež near Kutjevo.

Mijo Jozin

Mijo lives in Bektež near Kutjevo and has three daughters.

Jozo Jozin

Jozo lives in Slovenia. He has a son, Marijan, and a daughter, Romana.

Marijan has a son, Martin, and two daughters, Tamara, and Petricija.

Ante Križanov

The third son of Križan, Ante married Iva Trogrlić. They had one son, Stipan, and two daughters, Jela and Mara.

Stipan moved to Serbia, and it is unknown whether he had descendants.

Ivan Križanov

Ivan, the eighth son of Križan, married Ruža Serdarušić. They had three sons: Ante (who died at age five), Križan, and Marijan (who died in childhood), and two daughters, Mara and Ana.

Križan Ivanov

Križan married Marica Pušić. They had one son, Ivan.

Križan was killed in Zagreb in 1944.

Ivan Križanov

Ivan married twice.

From his first marriage to Dragica Dragun, he had a son named Križan.

Križan Ivanov (Vrpolje branch)

Križan married Ljerka Maričić. They have two sons, Toni and Dinko, and live in Vrpolje.

From his second marriage to Anka Nikolić, Ivan had two daughters, Biljana and Belina.

Jure Križanov

Jure, the ninth son of Križan, married three times.

His first wife was Pera Matišić, from a well-known Croatian musical family, with whom he had a son, Josip. Pera died in 1919 from the Spanish flu.

His second marriage was to a woman from the Budić family. They had a son, Martin, who was killed near Bihać in 1942 and never married.

His third marriage was to Joza Dragun, with no children.

Josip Jurin

Josip married Anica Dragun. They had two sons, Ivan and Milan, and four daughters: Slava, Mara, Neda, and Maca.

Ivan Josipov

Ivan married twice.

From his first marriage to Matija Pušić, he had two sons, Jure and Vladimir, and a daughter, Mihaela (baptized Milena).

Jure married Ruža Kosić and they have two sons: Ivica Julio and Marin Tin.

Vladimir married Ljubica Kosić and they have two sons: Filip and Leo.

This is an example of two brothers marrying two sisters.

From his second marriage to Verka Nikolovski, Ivan had no children.

Ivan died in Zagreb in 2025 at the age of 93.

May he rest in peace.

Mijo Antin

Mijo, the second son of Ante, married Manda Martinović. They had nine children: Jozo, Ante, Mate, Mijo, Petar, Iva, Matija, Ruža, and Antica.

No further information exists about his son Mijo beyond the birth record. It is assumed he either died young or moved away.

Descendants through Jozo, Mate, and Petar are known as Tucići, a nickname given due to the family’s reputation for romantic adventures.

Jozo Mijin

Jozo married Iva Bilojelić. They had three sons: Mate, Jozo, and Nikola (who died at age six), and four daughters: Matija, Jela, Mara, and Iva.

Jozo Jozin

Jozo never married.

Ante Jozin

Ante married Jela Škorić. They had four sons: Marijan, Mate, Pilip, and Nikola, and four daughters: Jela, Mara, Kata, and Mila.

Marijan Antin (Đika)

Marijan married Iva Čorbić. They had two sons, Ivan and Milan, and four daughters: Mara, Dragica, Kata, and Marija.

Ivan Marijanov (Đikin)

Ivan married Ana Pušić. They have a son, Zdenko, and a daughter, Kristina.

Zdenko married twice. From his first marriage he has a son, Tomislav.

From his second marriage to Kristina Topić, he has a daughter, Ana.

Milan Marijanov (Đikin)

Milan married twice.

From his first marriage to Marija Pušić, he has a son, Nedjeljko, and three daughters: Dragica, Ljubica, and Tanja.

Nedjeljko and Ivana Božičević have two daughters, Marija and Tina.

Milan had no children from his second marriage.

Mate Antin

Mate, another son of Ante, married Matija Gudelj. They had no children.

Marijan Antin

The youngest son of Ante, Marijan married Maša Gudelj and had one son, Ante.

Ante married Renata Nagy and had a son also named Ante.

Ante Antin and his wife Valentina Juriša also had a son named Ante.

It is notable that three consecutive generations gave their sons the same name, Ante.

Ante Mijin

Ante never married and died at the age of 26.

Mate Mijin

Mate married twice.

From his first marriage to Tomica Kovač, he had a son Ivan, who died at age seven.

From his second marriage to Iva Vlajčić, he had two sons, Martin and Jure, and a daughter, Iva.

Martin died at the age of 24.

Jure Matin (Đuka)

Jure married Matija Škorić. They had two sons, Ante and Mate, and three daughters: Mila, Stana, and Iva.

Ante Jurin

Ante married Mila Đikić. They had three sons: Ivan, Milan, and Srećko, and one daughter, Matija.

Milan and Srećko are married, each with two sons. They live in Đakovo.

Mate Jurin

Mate married Danica Malenica. They have one son, Tomislav, and three daughters: Marija, Mila, and Ana.

Tomislav married Vlatka Dolić. They have a son Matej and a daughter Danijela. They live in Podstrana near Split.

Petar Mijin

Petar, the youngest son of Mijo, married Matija Tandara. They had four sons: Mate, Stipan, Ante (who died as a child), and Ilija, and one daughter, Anđa.

Mate Petrov

Mate married Kata Cimerman. They had one son, Ivan, and one daughter, Ankica.

Ivan Matin

Ivan married Pavica Kralik. They had a son Emil and a daughter Ivana.

Emil married Zorica Nađ. They have two daughters, Marijana and Nikolina. They live in Kutjevo and are known for wine production.

Stipan Petrov

Stipan married Joza Malenica. They had one son Ivan and one daughter Ankica.

Ivan Stipanov

Ivan married Ana Budimir. They had one son Mirko and three daughters: Mirjana, Marija, and Ankica.

Mirko married Dragica Čamber. They have two sons, Ivan and Josip, and one daughter, Ana Marija. They live in Dugo Selo.

Ilija Petrov (Grabunjaš)

Ilija, the youngest son of Petar, married Anica Škorić. They had two sons, Marijan and Mirko, and three daughters: Maca, Neda, and Dragica.

Marijan Ilijin (Marinko Grabunjašev)

Marijan married Franka Kovač. They had two sons, Nediljko (who died as a child) and Mirčo, and three daughters: Ivanka, Marija, and Neda.

Mirčo and his wife Vesna Zetović have a son, Miroslav, and a daughter, Sonja. They live in Đakovo.

Mirko Ilijin (Grabunjašev)

Mirko married Kata Kovač. They had three sons, Marinko, Ivica, and Ante, and two daughters, Anka and Marija.

Marinko, Ivica, and Ante live in Zagreb and have families.

Sub-branch of Filip, son of Ante

Filip Antin

Filip, the second son of Ante, remained living in Ričice. In 1802 he married Matija Parlov. They had three sons: Martin, Mate, and Ivan, and five daughters: Joza, Jela, Jurka, Antica, and Ruža. For Mate and Ivan, only birth records exist, suggesting they either died young or moved away.

Martin Filipov

Martin married Manda Manenica. They had five sons: Jerko, Ilija, Ivan, Martin, and Ivan (younger), and three daughters: Tomica, Lucija, and Iva.

The elder Ivan and Martin died in childhood. No records exist for the younger Ivan, suggesting he either died young or emigrated.

Jerko Martinov

Jerko married Iva Topalušić. They had three sons: Martin, Luka (who died as a child), and Ivan.

Martin Jerkov

Martin married Iva Kovač. They had three daughters: Matija, Mara, and Iva.

Ivan Jerkov

Ivan married Jaka Martinović. They had two sons, Mate and Martin, and two daughters, Ana and Tomica.

Mate Ivanov (Pikić)

Born in 1898, Mate moved to his mother’s estate in Martinovići. He married Matija Ležeta, and his descendants are known by the nickname Pikići.

It is said of Mate: “Such a man could not even be born from a mother who did not try hard.” From this saying, he earned the nickname Piko, and his descendants became known as Pikići.

Mate and Matija had three sons: Nikola, Mirko, and Marijan, and two daughters: Milica and Dragica.

Nikola Matin

Nikola married Matija Kilić. They had two sons, Damir and Željko, and two daughters, Vera and Marija.

Damir Nikolin

Damir married twice.

From his first marriage to Marina Čelan, he had a son, Tin.

From his second marriage to Lucija Modrić, he had a son, Leo, and two daughters, Žana and Doris.

Damir and his family live in Germany.

Željko Nikolin

Željko married Anita Košćak. They have two sons, Nikola and Marko. They live in Split.

Mirko Matin

Mirko, the second son of Mate, married Anđelka Dragica Mršić. They had three sons: Nedjeljko, Zdravko, and Miroslav.

Nedjeljko Mirkov

Nedjeljko married Danica Jaginović. They had three sons: Mirko, Ivan, and Križan.

Zdravko Mirkov

Zdravko married Dubravka Brajković. They had two sons: Ante and Vedran.

Miroslav Mirkov

Miroslav, the youngest son of Mirko, married Ružica Krajinović. They have a son Josip and two daughters, Ines and Ana. The family lives in Split.

Marijan (third son of Mate)

Marijan married Mara Tandara. They had a son, Mate, and a daughter, Snježana.

Mate married Zdravka Guić. They have three sons: Marijan, Ivan, and Ante. The family lives in Split.

Martin (younger son of Ivan)

Martin married Anica Lekić. They had three sons: Ivan, Jerko, and Marijan (who died as a child), and two daughters: Mara (who died at the age of five from burns) and Matija.

Ivan Martinov

Ivan married Ana Radmanić. They have a son, Darko, who is married to Dragica (née Sabolović). They have a son Ivan and a daughter Monika. They live in Bjelovar.

Jerko (younger son of Martin)

Jerko married Ružica Sekulić. They have a son, Renato, who married Tanja Lancoš and has a daughter, Lea. They live in Zagreb.

Ilija (second son of Martin and Manda Manenica)

Ilija was married to Luca Tavra. They had three sons: Mate, Ivan, and Šimun, and three daughters: Tomica, Iva, and Mara.

Ivan died at the age of six, and Šimun died as a small child.

Mate Ilijin (Rumić branch)

Mate reportedly had a very reddish complexion and was therefore nicknamed Rumić, and his descendants became known as Rumići.

Mate married Mara Topalušić. They had four sons: Ivan, Božo, Mijo, and Toma, and two daughters: Tomica and Mara.

No further records exist for Ivan and Božo apart from their birth entries, suggesting they either died young or moved away.

Mijo Matin

Mijo married Joza Štrljić. They had two sons, Vinko and Milan.

Vinko (older son of Mijo)

Vinko married twice. From his first marriage to Krsta Mršić, he had five sons: Mijo, Ante, Milan, Zdravko, and Vinko.

From his second marriage to Matija Sičenica, Vinko had no children.

Mijo (eldest son of Vinko)

Mijo married Jelena Krezo. They had two sons: Ivica and Zoran.

Zoran married Vesna Belan. They have a son, Luka, and a daughter, Mija. The family lives in Germany.

Ante (second son of Vinko)

Ante married Vesna Topalušić. They have three daughters: Kristina, Marina, and Nikolina. They live in Germany.

Milan (third son of Vinko)

Milan married Nediljka Matušić. They had three sons: Kristijan, Antonio, and Dino, and one daughter, Valentina. They live in Predavac near Bjelovar.

Zdravko (fourth son of Vinko)

Zdravko married Brigita Horvatinčić. They had two sons, Mate and Bruno, and two daughters, Anita and Petra. They live in Predavac near Bjelovar.

Vinko (fifth son of Vinko)

Vinko married Slavica Matišić. They had a son, Mario, and a daughter, Marina. They live in Predavac near Bjelovar.

Milan (younger son of Mijo)

Milan married Iva Lukić. They had two sons, Željko and Dragan, and two daughters, Rosa and Dragica.

Toma Matin

The youngest son of Mate and Mara Topalušić, Toma married Tomica Tandara. They had five sons: Petar, Marijan (killed in WWII), Slavko, Ante, and Mate, and four daughters: Mila, Dragica, Ana, and Nevenka.

Petar Tomin

Petar, the eldest son of Toma, married Kata Novosel. They had one son, Ivica.

Ivica married Ljubica Kozić. They have a son, Hrvoje, and a daughter, Sabina, a well-known Croatian journalist working at RTL. The family lives in Zagreb.

Slavko Tomin

Slavko, the third son of Toma, from his marriage to Savka Vukobratović, had a son, Marko. They live in Zagreb.

Ante Tomin

Ante, the fourth son of Toma, married Zlata Vučemil. They had a son, Draženko.

Draženko married Nada Medić. They have a son, Luka, and two daughters, Maja and Mateja. They live in Bjelovar.

Mate Tomin

Mate, the youngest son of Toma, married Tonka Vukičević. They had a son, Željko.

Željko married Ankica Šitum. They have two sons and live in Canada.
