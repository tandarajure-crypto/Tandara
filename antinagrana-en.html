THE THIRD BRANCH OF THE FIRST TANDARA FAMILY

3.0. Ante Tandara – Ivanov (born c. 1735)

Ante Tandara was Ivan Tandara's third son. Ante was about a year younger than Petar, which fits well with the known birth order of Ivan's children. He married Matija Brnas, with whom he had three sons, Ante, Filip and Pavao (1777–), and three daughters, Jaka (1771–), Luca and Ruža. Apart from the record of Pavao's birth, no other information about him has been found, suggesting that he probably died at a very young age or left Ričice before leaving any trace in the parish registers. Ante's line remained firmly connected to Ričice, where it developed most clearly during the nineteenth and twentieth centuries. This family nucleus produced the largest number of people bearing the Tandara surname, and Ante's line is therefore regarded as the most extensive among Ivan's descendants. In later generations, some members of this branch gradually began to move away—first to nearby settlements and later much farther afield, to various European Union countries, as well as Canada, the United States and Australia. Despite this worldwide dispersal, the original Ante line remained deeply rooted in Ričice, preserving the continuity of family identity and the richest written record of its beginnings.

FIRST SUB-BRANCH OF ANTE ANTIN

3.1. Ante (1769–1849)

As the eldest son of Ante Ivanov, Ante married Matija Jukić in 1801. They had three sons, Lovro, Mijo and Petar (1821–), and six daughters, Pera (1802–), Ruža (1804–), Antica (1807–), Iva (1808–), a second daughter named Antica (1814–), and Marija (1823–). No information about Petar has been found apart from his birth record, so it is assumed that he died young or moved away from Zavelim. The family line was continued by the sons Lovro and Mijo. Mijo's descendants became known by the family nickname TUCIĆI, while Lovro's descendants through his son Marijan became known as LOVRINOVIĆI, and those through his son Križan as KRIŽANOVIĆI. According to the 1819 census, Ante lived in Zavelim, while his brother Filip lived in Ričice. Their descendants later spread throughout Croatia and Europe, as well as to the United States and Canada.
