THE THIRD BRANCH OF THE FIRST TANDARA FAMILY

Ante Tandara, son of Ivan (born around 1735 – )

Ante Tandara is regarded as the third or possibly fourth son of Ivan Tandara, with the additional possibility that Filip from Livno may have belonged to the same generation, although surviving records do not provide definitive confirmation. Ante was approximately one year younger than Petar, which aligns well with the known sequence of Ivan’s children. He married Matija Brnas, with whom he had sons Ante, Filip and Pavao, and daughters Jaka, Luca and Ruža. Apart from the record of his birth, no further information exists about Pavao, suggesting that he either died at a very young age or left Ričice before leaving any trace in the local parish registers.

Ante’s branch remained rooted in Ričice, where it developed most clearly throughout the 19th and 20th centuries. From this core emerged the largest number of bearers of the Tandara surname, making Ante’s line the most developed and most numerous among Ivan’s descendants. In later generations, some members of this branch gradually began to disperse — first to nearby settlements, and eventually much farther, to various countries across the European Union, as well as to Canada, the United States and Australia. Despite this global dispersion, the original line of Ante remained firmly anchored in Ričice, preserving the continuity of family identity and the richest written record of its origins.

Podgrana – Ante, son of Anto

3.1. Ante, son of Anto (1769–1849)

Ante, the eldest son of Anto, son of Ivan, married Matija Jukić in 1801. They had sons Lovro, Mijo and Petar, and daughters Pera, Ruža, Antica, Iva and Marija. There are no further records about Petar apart from his birth, so he either died young or moved away from Zavelim.

According to the 1819 census, Ante lived in Zavelim, and his brother Filip in Ričice. Their descendants later spread throughout Croatia, Europe, the USA and Canada.

3.1.1. Lovro, son of Anto (1811–1903)

He married Matija Sičenica. They had sons Marijan and Križan.

3.1.1.1. Marijan, son of Lovro (1836– )

He married Matija Kovač. They had sons Jure, Marko, Stipan and Jozo, and daughters Iva, Mara and Matija. There are no data about Stipan except his birth, so it is assumed he died young or moved away. Marijan’s descendants, through his sons Marko and Jure, bear the nickname Lovrinovići, derived from Marijan’s father Lovro. Lovrinovići today live in Popovača, Sarajevo and Stobreč.

3.1.1.1.1. Marko, son of Marijan (1869– )

He married Božica Pušić. They had a son Mirko and a daughter Iva.

3.1.1.1.1.1. Mirko, son of Marko (1901– )

He married Anica Kliić. They had sons Milan and Vladimir (who died at the age of 14), and daughters Dragica, Marija and Nediljka.

3.1.1.1.1.2. Milan, son of Marko (1939– )

He married Svjetlana Trifunović. They had sons Vladimir and Dalibor.

Vladimir, son of Milan (1969–), married Claire Tacher; they have a son Alexander James and a daughter Sophie Elizabeth (2008–), and live in London. The second son, Dalibor (1970–), married Amela Nezirović; they have a son Damir and a daughter Tea. They live in Sarajevo.

Jozo, son of Marijan (1882–1944) – Trošići

He married Iva Kovač. They had sons Marijan and Luka.

Marijan, son of Jozo (1910–1984)

He married Anica Budimir; they had sons Nikola and Jozo (who died at the age of 6), and daughters Mara and Zorka.

Nikola, son of Marijan (1939– )

He married Marija Lukić; they had three daughters, Ana, Marijana and Radmila, and a son Vlado.

Vlade, son of Nikola (1970– )

He married Silvana Ruščić; they have a son Franjo Josip and a daughter Tea.

Luka, son of Jozo (1912–1987)

He married Anđa Kovač; they had three daughters: Iva, Mara and Zorka.

Križan, son of Lovro (1843–1910) – nickname Križanovići

He married Pera Budimir. They had 15 children, of whom seven died before the age of ten. An interesting detail is that his second son Ilija died from a bull’s goring. The descendants of Križan, through his sons Mate, Ante, Ivan and Jure, bear the nickname Križanovići and live in Zagreb, Đakovo, Kutjevo, Bektež, Strizivojna, Vrpolje and several European countries.

Mate, son of Križan (1866–1948)

The eldest son of Križan, he married Iva Pirić. They had sons Mijo, Marijan and Jozo, and daughters Mara and Matija. Mijo never married. The family lives in Bektež near Kutjevo.

Marijan, son of Mate (1902–1974)

He married Anica Maras; they had sons Ante, Jozo, Mato and Ivan, and a daughter Mara.

Ante, son of Marijan (1934– )

In his first marriage with Elizabeta Miletić he had a son Darko (1958–) and a daughter Gordana. Darko, in his first marriage with Angela (surname unknown), has sons (1982–) and Markus (1987–). In his second marriage with Ana Kajtar he has no children. Darko lives with his family in Germany.

Jozo, son of Marijan (1941–2002)

He did not marry.

Mato, son of Marijan (1945– )

In marriage with Anica Bogdanović he had a son Alen (1969–) and a daughter Laura. Alen married Melita Pjela; they have a son Marin (1993–) and a daughter Ena (1996–). The family lives in Slavonski Brod.

Ivan, son of Marijan (1953– )

The fourth son of Marijan, he married Ljiljana Cobović. They have a son Marijan and daughters Lana and Paulina. They live in Đakovo, where Ivan runs his own law office.

Jozo, son of Mate (1904– )

The youngest son of Mate and Iva Pirić, he married Jaka Kilić. They had sons Mijo, Jozo and Ivan, and a daughter Mara. Jozo was killed in World War II, and Mijo’s family today lives in Bektež near Kutjevo.

Mijo, son of Jozo (1940– )

He lives in Bektež near Kutjevo and has three daughters.

Jozo, son of Jozo (1943– )

He has a son Marijan and a daughter Romana. Marijan and Marinela have a son Martin and daughters Tamara and Petricija.

Ante, son of Križan (1870–1945)

The third son of Križan, he married Iva Trogrlić. They had a son Stipan and daughters Jela and Mara. Stipan moved to Serbia; it is not known whether he had descendants or what happened to him later.

Ivan, son of Križan (1881–1920)

The eighth son of Križan, he married Ruža Serdarušić. They had sons Ante, who died at the age of five, Križan, and Marijan, who died as a child, and daughters Mara and Ana.

Križan, son of Ivan (1916–1944)

In marriage with Marica Pušić he had a son Ivan. Križan was killed in Zagreb in 1944.

Ivan, son of Križan (1938–2007)

He married twice.

From his first marriage with Dragica Dragun he had a son Križan (1956–).

Križan, son of Ivan (1956–), and his wife Ljerka Maričić have sons Toni and Dinko. Toni (1979–) and Nada Jergović have daughters Veronika and Magdalena, and a son Filip (2010–). The family lives in Vrpolje.

From his second marriage with Anka Nikolić he had daughters Biljana and Belina.

Jure, son of Križan (1883–1947)

He was the ninth son of Križan and married three times.

His first marriage was with Pera Matišić, who came from a well-known Croatian musical family. From that marriage he had a son Josip. Pera died in 1919 from Spanish flu. The second marriage of Jure, son of Križan, was with a girl from the Budić family. They had a son Martin, who was killed in 1942 near Bihać and did not marry. The third marriage was with Joza Dragun, with no children.

Josip, son of Jure (1911–1946)

In marriage with Anica Dragun he had sons Ivan and Milan, and daughters Slava, Mara, Neda and Maca.

Ivan, son of Josip (1933–2025) – my father

He married twice.

From his first marriage with Matija Pušić he had sons Jure and Vladimir, and a daughter Mihaela (baptismal name Milena).

Jure married Ruža Kosić; they have sons Ivica Julio and Marin Tin. Vladimir married Ljubica Kosić; they have sons Filip and Leo. So this is a marriage of two brothers and two sisters.

From his second marriage with Verka Nikolovski, Ivan had no children. Ivan died in Zagreb in 2025 at the age of 93. Rest in peace, my dear father!

Mijo, son of Anto (1818–1878) – nickname Tucići

Mijo, the second son of Anto, married Manda Martinović. They had nine children: Jozo, Ante, Mato, Mijo, Petar, and daughters Iva, Matija, Ruža and Antica.

There are no further records about the son Mijo apart from his birth. It is assumed that he died young or moved away in an unknown direction. The descendants through sons Jozo, Mato and Petar bear the nickname Tucići, given because members of the family were inclined to romantic adventures.

Jozo, son of Mijo (1851– )

He married Iva Bilojelić. They had sons Mato, Jozo and Nikola (who died at the age of six), and daughters Matija, Jela, Mara and Iva.

Jozo, son of Jozo (1884– )

He did not marry.

Ante, son of Jozo (1881–1942)

Marijan, son of Anto (1908–2003) (Đika)

Ivan, son of Marijan (1939–2024) (Đikin)
