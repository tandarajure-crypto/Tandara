Visitors' Messages of the Tandara Family

This is not an ordinary guestbook. It is a curated place where, with the sender's permission, messages, testimonies and family traces connected with the Tandara surname are preserved for future generations.

How to send a message for publication

You can send your message through the Contact & Collaboration page or directly to tandarajure@outlook.com.

Please clearly state whether you allow your message, name and location to be published. Without clear consent, the message will not be published publicly.

Send a message for publication

What may be published?

A personal message

A short message from a person with the Tandara surname, a descendant, relative or collaborator who wishes to leave a trace in the archive.

A family memory

A story about an ancestor, a family home, a migration, an old document, a photograph or an event of value to family history.

Searching for relatives

A note from someone looking for descendants of a particular person or trying to connect with one of the Tandara family branches.

An archive contribution

A correction, addition or new clue that may help future research into the Tandara surname and family history.

Digital Archive of the Tandara Family · Croatia and the world

This section will publish messages from people with the Tandara surname and others connected with the Tandara family after they have been received, reviewed and approved for public publication.

Only content with the sender's clear permission will be published.

Publication rules

  1. A message is published only if the sender has given clear consent.
  2. Private contact details, e-mail addresses and phone numbers are not published.
  3. The text may be language-edited or shortened without changing its meaning.
  4. Content that offends others, violates privacy or is unrelated to the project will not be published.
