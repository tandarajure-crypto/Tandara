Visitor Messages

Purpose of Visitor Messages

Visitor Messages preserves the personal voices of members of the Tandara family and researchers who wish to leave a memory, expression of gratitude, short testimony or family story. It complements the genealogy, but does not replace verified civil, parish and archival records.

Publication Only with Permission

A message is published only with the author's explicit permission. Information that is not necessary for understanding the message is not published, and the rules governing the protection of personal information are described in greater detail in the Privacy Policy.

What Should an Entry Contain?

A proposed entry should include a short title, the message itself and the author’s preferred credit. A place and date can help readers understand the context, but include them only when known and suitable for publication. A layout example is not a visitor testimony or proof that permission has been given.

How to Prepare a Message

Describe the event, person or custom you remember. Give the place and approximate date if known, and explain whether you witnessed the event or heard about it from a family member. If you are unsure of a year or name, say so.

  • For a story about a person, include the family branch and genealogy code if you know it.
  • For a photograph, note who or what it shows, where and when it was taken, and the photographer if known.
  • For a document, give its title or type, date and the place where it is held.
  • Clearly separate the text proposed for publication from information supplied only for verification.

State whether you permit publication and how you wish to be credited. Sending material does not replace permission to publish; do not include personal or sensitive family information in text intended for the public.

A Message, a Correction or a Research Lead?

For a memory or family story, propose an entry in Visitor Messages. For an incorrect name, date or family relationship, identify the page, the current information, the proposed correction and its source. This allows personal testimony to be preserved while genealogical information is checked separately.

From a Message to an Archival Record

A personal testimony may provide a valuable clue for further research, but genealogical information from a message becomes part of the family archive only after it has been verified and connected with other sources.

The Contact and Collaboration page is used to send a message, document or proposal for publication in Visitor Messages.
