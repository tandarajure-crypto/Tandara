MATE BRANCH

Mate Tandara, son of Ivan, and Filipa née Kopač

Mate Tandara: ★ c. 1750  –  ✝ between 1806 and 1819

Wife Filipa née Kopač: ★ unknown  –  ✝ before 1806

Mate Tandara, son of Ivan, was the fourth son of Ivan Tandara. In the 1806 parish census Status Animarum, he is recorded as the head of a household of six members, while a note beside his wife indicates that she had already died.

Mate and Filipa had one son, Ivan, and four daughters: Matija, Manda, Jaka and Cecilija. The male line continued through their son Ivan.

Mate’s descendants became known among the Tandara families by the nickname Cesarevići, also recorded as Sesarovići. Both forms can be found on older gravestones.

The oldest parish register of deaths for Ričice, covering the period from 1787 to 1827, has largely been destroyed. It has therefore not been possible to find a direct record of Mate’s death.

Nevertheless, there is no doubt that Mate belonged to the line of Ivan Tandara, because only one Tandara family—the family of Ivan—lived in Ričice during Mate’s lifetime.

How to use the page and diagram

  1. To return to the main family-tree page, select the home button in the left-hand menu.

  3. Enter a name, code or detail in the search field, then select Find.
  4. Click a person’s box to open the available details about that person and family.
  5. Use the + or − symbol on a person’s box to open or close the descendants.
  6. Expand all displays the entire Mate branch, Collapse closes expanded sections, and Three generations displays the first three generations.
  7. Fit adjusts the complete diagram to the available area, while the + and − buttons zoom in or out.
  8. On a computer, drag the diagram with the mouse. On a phone or tablet, drag it with your finger.
