MATE’S BRANCH

Mate Tandara, son of Ivan, and Filipa Kopač

Mate Tandara: ★ c. 1740  –  ✝ unknown

Wife Filipa Kopač: ★ unknown  –  ✝ unknown

Mate Tandara was the fourth son of Ivan Tandara. Mate and Filipa had five children: a son, Ivan, and daughters Matija, Manda, Jaka and Cecilija. The male line continued exclusively through their son Ivan.

Ivan married twice. In his first marriage, to Pava Sičenica, he had a son, Ante, the only male heir of Mate’s line. In his second marriage, to Jela Parlov, he had a daughter, Ruža.

This branch is traditionally known by the nickname Cesarovići. According to a preserved family story, some of Mate’s descendants performed agricultural work for a family of the Lažeta line. When members of the household and neighbours asked them, “What was the food like at the Lažeta home?”, they replied, “Like at the emperor’s table!”

It remains unclear whether the word cesar or sesar was originally used. Although both forms can be found on older gravestones and in oral tradition, most descendants of this family branch are now known as Cesarovići.

How to use the page and diagram

  1. To return to the main family-tree page, select the home button in the left-hand menu.
  2. Select the Croatian or English version by clicking the corresponding HR or EN flag.
  3. Enter a name, code or detail in the search field, then select Find.
  4. Click a person’s box to open the available details about that person and family.
  5. Use the + or − symbol on a person’s box to open or close the descendants.
  6. Expand all displays the complete Mate branch, Collapse closes expanded sections, and Three generations displays the first three generations.
  7. Fit adjusts the complete diagram to the available area, while the + and − buttons zoom in or out.
  8. On a computer, drag the diagram with the mouse. On a phone or tablet, drag it with your finger.
