The Livno Branch of the Tandara Family

A Distinct Branch of the Tandara Family Lineage

This page is dedicated to the branch of the Tandara family associated with the Livno region.

An Invitation to the Tandara Families of Livno

If any members of the Tandara family from Livno are reading this page and are interested in contributing to this project, I would be grateful if you could contact me via the e-mail address provided in the Contact and Cooperation section.

My wish is to compare DNA test results in order to confirm the assumption of our common ancestral origin in Ričice.

I sincerely thank everyone who is willing to contribute and help make this project even more complete, accurate, and valuable for future generations.
