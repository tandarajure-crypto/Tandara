Četvrta grana prve obitelji TANDARA

Mate Tandara (oko 1750. – oko 1806./1819.)

Mate Tandara i njegova supruga Filipa Kopač imali su petero djece: sina Ivana te kćeri Matiju, Mandu, Jaku i Ceciliju. Muška loza nastavila se isključivo preko sina Ivana. Ivan se ženio dva puta. U prvom braku s Pavom Sičenica dobio je sina Antu, jedinog muškog nasljednika Matine loze. U drugom braku s Jelom Parlov dobio je kćer Ružu, pa se iz te grane loza nije nastavila po muškoj liniji.

O podrijetlu toga nadimka među živućim pripadnicima ove rodoslovne grane plemena Tandara sačuvala se zanimljiva predaja. Prema njoj, neki su Matini potomci radili poljoprivredne poslove kod obitelji iz roda Lažeta. Kada su ih ukućani i susjedi upitali: „Kakva vam je hrana bila u Lažeta?”, odgovorili su: „Ko' u cesara!”

Je li pritom izgovorena riječ cesar ili sesar, ni danas nije posve razjašnjeno. Iako se oba oblika mogu pronaći na starijim nadgrobnim spomenicima i u predaji, danas se većina potomaka ove grane obitelji naziva Cesarovićima.

Ivan Matin

Ivan Matin ženio se dvaput. Prvi put se oženio 1817. godine Pavom Sičenica i s njom imao sina Antu. U drugom braku s Jelom Parlov dobio je kćer Ružu.

Ante Ivanov

Ante se 1853. vjenčao s Jozom Pušić. Imali su sinove Ivana, Juru, Marka i Matu te kćeri Ivu, Tomicu, Maru i Jelu. Za Juru i Marka nisam našao drugih zapisa osim onih o njihovu rođenju. Ili su umrli mladi ili su se nekamo odselili.

Ivan, najstariji Antin sin

Oženio se Ružom Matišić i s njom imao sinove Marijana, Jozu i Ivana te kćeri Anicu, Ivu i Jelu. Za Marijana nisam našao drugih zapisa osim onoga o njegovu rođenju. Ili je umro mlad ili se nekamo odselio.

Jozo Ivanov

Jozo, drugi Ivanov sin, uzeo je za ženu Ivu Bilić. Imali su sinove Marijana i Mirka (koji je umro kao malo dijete) te kćeri Jozu i Anu.

Marijan Jozin

Marijan Jozin oženio se Matijom Mršić i s njom dobio sina Josipa te kćeri Nadu, Danicu, Branku, Anu, Dragicu i Mirjanu. Josip je uzeo za ženu Dragicu Pavić. Imaju sinove Marijana, Antu i Kristijana te kćer Silviju.

Ivan, najmlađi sin Ivana i Ruže rođ. Matišić

Ženio se dvaput. Prvi put oženio se Jakom Tavra i s njom dobio sinove Franu i Stanka, koji je umro u dobi od četrnaest godina.

Frane Ivanov

Frane Ivanov uzeo je za ženu Tomicu Pavić i s njom dobio sinove Stanka, Cvjetka, Miroslava (koji je umro kao malo dijete) i Dragomira te kćeri Mariju, Rozu, Miroslavu i Anitu. Stanko je u braku s Ankicom Kovačević. Imaju kćeri Tatjanu i Tamaru. Žive u Zagrebu. Cvjetko i Dragomir nisu se ženili. Žive u Klokočevcu kod Bjelovara.

Mate, najmlađi Antin sin

Oženio se Ružom Manenica. Imali su sinove Antu (koji je umro kao malo dijete), Josipa, Cvitana, Antu (ponovno) i Nikolu te kćeri Tomicu i Maru. Za Josipa nisam našao drugih zapisa osim onoga o njegovu rođenju. Ili je umro mlad ili se nekamo odselio.

Cvitan Matin

Cvitan, treći Matin sin, uzeo je za ženu Tomicu Pavić. Imali su sinove Matu (koji je umro kao malo dijete) i Nikolu te kćeri Luciju, Anu, Ružu, Stanu, Nevenku i Branku. Za Nikolu nisam našao drugih zapisa osim onoga o njegovu rođenju. Ili je umro mlad ili se nekamo odselio.

Ante, četvrti Matin sin

Oženio se Anom Pavić. Dobili su sinove Marijana, Dinka (koji je umro kao malo dijete), Mirka, Matu i Cvitana te kćeri Anu, Maru i Tonku.

Marijan Antin

Uzeo je za ženu Pavu Pušić. Odgojili su sinove Antu i Dinka te kćer Nedu. Marijan je 1963. poginuo u Njemačkoj.

Ante Tandara Marijanov

Ante Marijanov rođen je 1961. u Ričicama. S majkom Pavom, sestrom Nedom i bratom Dinkom od 1964. živio je u selu Klokočevac kod Bjelovara. Oženio se Mirjanom Čeh i s njom dobio sinove Marijana i Marka. Početkom devedesetih godina, kao i većina hrvatskih domoljuba, uključio se u obranu Hrvatske od agresije bivše Jugoslavenske narodne armije i srpskih paravojnih skupina.

U Domovinskom ratu Ante sudjeluje od kolovoza 1991. kao pripadnik 105. brigade HV-a. U rujnu iste godine Ante i devetnaest drugih hrvatskih branitelja, većinom iz Bjelovara, bivaju zarobljeni u Kusonjama kod Pakraca, mučeni i zvjerski pogubljeni. Njihovi posmrtni ostaci ekshumirani su iz masovne grobnice Rakov Potok u siječnju 1992.

Antini posmrtni ostaci pokopani su 5. veljače 1992. na bjelovarskom groblju. Vječna mu slava i hvala za njegov doprinos stvaranju Republike Hrvatske. Počivao u miru Božjem.

Dinko, mlađi Marijanov sin

Uzeo je za ženu Dubravku Lončar. Imaju sinove Luku i Pavla. Žive u Zagrebu.

Mirko, treći sin Ante i Ane rođ. Pavić

Oženio se Anđom Parlov i s njom dobio sinove Marijana i Ivicu. Marijan se nije ženio. Mirko, Anđa i Marijan žive u Splitu.

Ivica Mirkov

Vjenčao se s Matijom Bilobrk. Imaju sina Ivana i kćer Ivanu. Žive u SAD-u.

Cvitan, četvrti Antin sin

Uzeo je za ženu Katicu Kršinić. Imaju sina Marijana i kćer Zrinku. Marijan se oženio Leidom Kezele i s njom dobio sinove Luku i Lovru. Cvitanova obitelj živi u Splitu.

Mate, najmlađi Antin sin

Uzeo je za ženu Zlatu Mihoci. Odgojili su sina Daria i kćer Danijelu. Dario se oženio Ivanom Tomić. Nemaju djece.

Ivan, sin Mate i Filipe rođ. Kopač

Oženio se drugi put Jelom Parlov. Imali su kćer Ružu.

The Fourth Branch of the First TANDARA Family

Mate Tandara (c. 1750 – c. 1806/1819)

Mate Tandara and his wife, Filipa Kopač, had five children: a son, Ivan, and four daughters, Matija, Manda, Jaka, and Cecilija. The male line of descent continued exclusively through Ivan. He married twice. In his first marriage, to Pava Sičenica, he had a son, Ante, the only male descendant through whom Mate’s line continued. In his second marriage, to Jela Parlov, he had a daughter, Ruža, and therefore that branch did not continue through the male line.

An interesting tradition concerning the origin of this family branch’s nickname has been preserved among its living descendants. According to local tradition, some of Mate’s descendants worked on the farms of the Lažeta family. When relatives and neighbors later asked them, “How was the food at the Lažeta household?”, they replied: “Fit for an emperor!”

Whether the original word was pronounced cesar or sesar remains uncertain to this day. Although both forms appear on old gravestones and in oral tradition, most descendants of this branch are now known as the Cesarović family.

Ivan, Son of Mate

Ivan married twice. In 1817 he married Pava Sičenica, and they had a son, Ante. In his second marriage, to Jela Parlov, he had a daughter, Ruža.

Ante, Son of Ivan

In 1853, Ante married Joza Pušić. They had four sons—Ivan, Jure, Marko, and Mate—and four daughters: Iva, Tomica, Mara, and Jela. I have found no records of Jure and Marko beyond their birth entries. They may have died young or moved elsewhere.

Ivan, Ante’s Eldest Son

Ivan married Ruža Matišić. They had three sons—Marijan, Jozo, and Ivan—and three daughters: Anica, Iva, and Jela. I have found no records of Marijan beyond his birth entry. He may have died young or moved away.

Jozo, Son of Ivan

Jozo married Iva Bilić. They had two sons, Marijan and Mirko, who died in early childhood, and two daughters, Joza and Ana.

Marijan, Son of Jozo

Marijan married Matija Mršić. They had a son, Josip, and six daughters: Nada, Danica, Branka, Ana, Dragica, and Mirjana. Josip later married Dragica Pavić. They had three sons—Marijan, Ante, and Kristijan—and a daughter, Silvija.

Ivan, Youngest Son of Ivan and Ruža Matišić

Ivan married twice. His first wife was Jaka Tavra, with whom he had two sons, Frane and Stanko. Stanko died at the age of fourteen.

Frane, Son of Ivan

Frane married Tomica Pavić. They had four sons—Stanko, Cvjetko, Miroslav, and Dragomir— and four daughters: Marija, Roza, Miroslava, and Anita. Miroslav died in early childhood. Stanko married Ankica Kovačević, and they have two daughters, Tatjana and Tamara. They live in Zagreb. Cvjetko and Dragomir never married and live in Klokočevac near Bjelovar.

Mate, Ante’s Youngest Son

Mate married Ruža Manenica. They had five sons—Ante (who died in infancy), Josip, Cvitan, Ante (named again after the death of the first child), and Nikola—and two daughters, Tomica and Mara. I have found no records of Josip beyond his birth entry. He may have died young or moved elsewhere.

Cvitan, Son of Mate

Cvitan married Tomica Pavić. They had two sons, Mate and Nikola, and six daughters: Lucija, Ana, Ruža, Stana, Nevenka, and Branka. Mate died in infancy. I have found no records of Nikola beyond his birth entry. He may have died young or moved elsewhere.

Ante, Fourth Son of Mate

Ante married Ana Pavić. They had five sons—Marijan, Dinko, Mirko, Mate, and Cvitan— and three daughters: Ana, Mara, and Tonka. Dinko died in infancy.

Marijan, Son of Ante

Marijan married Pava Pušić. They raised two sons, Ante and Dinko, and a daughter, Neda. Marijan was killed in Germany in 1963.

Ante Tandara, Son of Marijan

Ante Marijanov was born in 1961 in Ričice. From 1964 onward, he lived with his mother Pava, his sister Neda, and his brother Dinko in the village of Klokočevac near Bjelovar. He married Mirjana Čeh, and they had two sons, Marijan and Marko.

In the early 1990s, he joined the defense of Croatia during the Croatian War of Independence.

Ante served from August 1991 as a member of the 105th Brigade of the Croatian Army. In September of that year, he and nineteen other Croatian defenders, most of them from Bjelovar, were captured in Kusonje near Pakrac, tortured, and brutally executed. Their remains were recovered from the Rakov Potok mass grave in January 1992.

Ante’s remains were laid to rest on 5 February 1992 at the cemetery in Bjelovar. May his memory endure, and may he be remembered for his contribution to the creation of the Republic of Croatia. Rest in peace.

Dinko, Marijan’s Younger Son

Dinko married Dubravka Lončar. They have two sons, Luka and Pavao, and live in Zagreb.

Mirko, Third Son of Ante and Ana Pavić

Mirko married Anđa Parlov. They had two sons, Marijan and Ivica. Marijan never married. Mirko, Anđa, and Marijan live in Split.

Ivica, Son of Mirko

Ivica married Matija Bilobrk. They have a son, Ivan, and a daughter, Ivana. The family lives in the United States.

Cvitan, Fourth Son of Ante

Cvitan married Katica Kršinić. They have a son, Marijan, and a daughter, Zrinka. Marijan married Leida Kezele, and they have two sons, Luka and Lovro. Cvitan’s family lives in Split.

Mate, Youngest Son of Ante

Mate married Zlata Mihoci. They raised a son, Dario, and a daughter, Danijela. Dario married Ivana Tomić. They have no children.

Ivan, Son of Mate and Filipa Kopač

Ivan’s second marriage was to Jela Parlov. They had a daughter, Ruža.
