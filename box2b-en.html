PETAR BRANCH

Petar Tandara and Jela née Budić

Petar Tandara (★ c. 1734 – ✝ 1817) was married to Jela née Budić.

They had sons Grgo, Jakov, Martin, Marijan, Mijo, Tadija and Marko, and daughters Manda and Jela.

Jakov, Marijan and Marko do not appear in the 1806 Census of Souls, while Tadija was omitted from the 1819 census. According to the available records, the family line was continued by Grgo, Martin and Mijo. Their descendants are shown in the interactive genealogy diagram.

How to use the diagram

  1. Enter a name, code, nickname or place in the search field and press Find.
  2. Click a person’s box to open details and show or hide that person’s descendants.
  3. For Petar, Grgo, Martin and Mijo, the details panel contains a button linking to the related box page.
  4. Use Expand all, Collapse and First 3 generations to control the displayed branches.
  5. Fit adjusts the whole tree to the frame, while + and − change the zoom.
  6. Drag an empty part of the diagram with a mouse or finger to move the tree.
