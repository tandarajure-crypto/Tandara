THE FOURTH BRANCH OF THE FIRST TANDARA FAMILY

4.0. Mate Tandara – son of Ivan (★ c. 1750 – ✝ D.U.)

Mate Tandara was the fourth son of Ivan Tandara. Mate and his wife, Filipa née Kopač, had five children: a son, Ivan, and four daughters, Matija, Manda, Jaka and Cecilija. The male line continued exclusively through their son Ivan. Ivan married twice. In his first marriage to Pava née Sičenica, he had a son, Ante, the only male heir of Mate's line. In his second marriage to Jela née Parlov, he had a daughter, Ruža. This branch is traditionally known by the nickname Cesarovići.

An interesting oral tradition concerning the origin of this nickname has been preserved among members of this branch of the Tandara family. According to the tradition, some of Mate's descendants performed agricultural work for a family of the Lažeta line. When members of the household and their neighbours asked them, “What was the food like at the Lažeta household?”, they replied: “Like at the emperor's table!”

Whether the word they used was cesar or sesar remains unclear. Although both forms can be found on older gravestones and in oral tradition, most descendants of this family branch are now known as Cesarovići.

4.1.1.4.4.1.1. Ante, son of Marijan (★ 1961 – ✝ 1991)

In the early 1990s, he joined the defence of Croatia against the aggression of the former Yugoslav People's Army and Serbian paramilitary forces.

Ante served in the Croatian War of Independence from August 1991 as a member of the 105th Brigade of the Croatian Army. In September of the same year, Ante and nineteen other Croatian defenders, most of them from Bjelovar, were captured in Kusonje near Pakrac, tortured and executed. Their remains were exhumed from the Rakov Potok mass grave in January 1992.

4.1.1.4.4.1.2. Dinko, son of Marijan

Biographical note

Dinko is a volunteer veteran of the Croatian War of Independence. Together with his brother Ante, he joined the defence of Croatia from the beginning of the war as a member of the Bjelovar-Bilogora Police Special Unit “Omega”. During the war he served on several fronts across Croatia, including Pakrac, Bilogora, Maslenica and Velebit.

During the war he lost his older brother Ante, who was killed at Kusonje in September 1991. Dinko continued his wartime and post-war service with a strong focus on Croatian veterans and their families.

At the Ministry of Croatian Veterans he served for four years as an assistant minister under Tomo Medved and was later appointed Director of the Directorate for Advisory, Psychosocial and Health Assistance. He has also had a long association with taekwondo as a national-team competitor, multiple national champion, and founder and coach of the Bjelovar Omega Taekwondo Club.

Details of the other living members of his family are not published.
