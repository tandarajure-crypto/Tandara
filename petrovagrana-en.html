THE SECOND BRANCH OF THE FIRST TANDARA FAMILY

2.0.Petar Tandara (c.1734–1817)

Petar Tandara was the second or third son of Ivan in order, depending on the correlation with Filip from Livno as one of the potential descendants of Ivan. He died in 1817 at the age of eighty-three, so it is estimated that he was born c. 1734.

Petar was married to Jela Budić. They had sons Grgo, Jakov, Martin, Marijan, Mijo, Tadija and Marko, and daughters Manda and Jela. The sons Jakov, Marijan and Marko do not appear in the 1806 Census of Souls, and Tadija is omitted from the 1819 census, so it can be assumed that they were no longer alive or that one of them had moved away from Ričice. Mijo had no male descendants and his branch died out.

2.1. Grgo Petrov (1770– )

Petar’s eldest son married Ruža Pušić. They had a son Šimun and a daughter Iva. Šimun and his wife Jela Budimir had a son Mato, and daughters Iva, Matija and Marta. Mato had no male heir, so Grgo’s branch came to an end.

2.2. Martin Petrov (1775–1844)

Martin, Petar’s third son, married Ruža Lekić. They had six sons: Ivan, Jure, Mijo, Grgo, Filip and Josip, and daughters Matija and Ana. The sons of Mijo have no records of marriage, so his line died out.

No records have been found for Jure, Filip and Josip other than those of their baptism, so it is not known whether they died young or moved away. The sons Ivan and Grgo were the only ones to continue the line.

2.3. Mijo Petrov (no birth or death year)

Mijo Petrov was married to Ruža, maiden name unknown. They had a daughter Matija. They had no male heir, so Mijo’s branch died out.

2.2.1. Ivan Martinov (1807–1877)

Martin’s eldest son married Antica Lažeta in 1826. Ivan and Antica had three sons: Marijan, Toma and Jure, and daughters Ruža, Iva, Joza and Matija. Marijan died as a small child. No other records have been found for Jure apart from his birth. Toma was the only heir of Ivan’s line and the founder of the Tromići.

2.2.2. Mijo Martinov (1814– )

Martin’s third son and his wife Matija, née Parlov, had eight children: two sons, Martin and Mijo (who died as a small child), and daughters Luca, Manda, Boža, Joza, Marija and Matija. No records have been found for Martin other than his birth. It is not known whether he died or moved away. After Martin, Mijo’s line died out.

2.2.3. Grgo Martinov (1818– )

Martin’s fourth son married twice. From his first marriage to Rosa, née Budimir, he had a son Lovro and a daughter Jaka. In his second marriage to Jaka, née Knežević, Grgo had sons Mato (who died as a small child) and Mijo, and daughters Matija, Joza and Veronika. The son Mijo from the second marriage had four sons and three daughters, but none of the sons continued Mijo’s line.

Beginning of migrations outside Ričice

In 1819, the family of Martin Tandara (1775–1844), Lovro’s grandfather, was in Zavelim. In that census Martin’s sons Ivan, Mijo and Grgo are also listed. When the Austrian cadastre was drawn up in 1835, Martin Tandara, son of the late Petar, was registered as the owner of real estate in Ričice. At that time most clans from Ričice owned property in Ričice, Zavelim, Podbila and Vir. These villages shared a common parish in Ričice. Many families within the same kin group moved from one locality to another, even within Ričice itself. The population grew rapidly and new living space was sought. In this context we can understand and accept the fact that one family, or part of it, might be recorded in Ričice in one census and in Zavelim in the next, or vice versa. However, it is reliably known that Grgo returned from Zavelim to Ričice with his children and continued living there with his family.

Tromići

2.2.1.1. Toma Ivanov (1830– )

Toma, Ivan’s second son, was married to Tomica Kovač. They had sons Ivan, Mato, Jure and another Ivan, who died as a child. Toma’s only heir was his son Jure.

Toma’s line in Zavelim was known by the nickname Tromići, which, according to tradition, arose from their height and slow, heavy gait. The present-day descendants of the Domine branch are the opposite—temperamental and very lively.

The following account refers exclusively to the line of Jure, son of Toma.

2.2.3.1. Lovro Grgin (1842–1922)

Grgo’s eldest son married Iva Parlov and with her had sons Ante, Ivan and Marijan, and daughters Iva and Luca. No records have been found for Ivan other than his birth. In the parish death register of Ričice it is recorded that Lovro’s daughter Luca was “killed by rocks” at the age of thirteen.

2.2.3.2. Mijo Grgin (1856–1933)

Grgo’s third son married Iva Tandara and they had seven children: sons Martin (who died at the age of 19), Nikola and Mato (who died as a small child) and another Nikola, and daughters Iva and Mara. No records have been found for the younger Nikola other than his birth, while the elder Nikola died as a child. Mijo’s children had no heirs, so Mijo’s branch died out.

2.2.3.1. Lovro’s branch (1842–1922)

Lovro and his wife Iva Parlov had sons Ante and Marijan, and daughters Lucija (who died as a child), Iva and another Lucija. Ante and Marijan were the only heirs of the line. Their sons had thirty-three children.

2.2.3.1.1. Ante Lovrin (1871–1933)

Ante, son of Lovro, was married to Tomica Lukić and with her had sons Ivan, Marijan, Mato the first (who died as a child), Mato the second and Nikola, and daughters Marta, Iva and Matija. The heirs of the line were Ivan, Marijan, Mato the second, and Nikola.

2.2.3.1.2. Marijan Lovrin (1881– )

He married Luca Parlov and with her had sons Mijo, Marin, Petar and Ivan (who died as a small child), and daughters Matija, Iva, Ana and Tomica.

2.2.3.1.1.1. Ivan Antin (1896– )

Ante’s eldest son Ivan married Mara Mršić. They had sons Lovro, Mirko, Mato, Ante Vlado and Nikola, and a daughter Tomica. Ivan moved to Slatina, and his descendants now live in Predavac near Bjelovar, Slatina, Obrovac, Zagreb and Germany.

2.2.3.1.1.2. Marijan Antin (1898–1933)

Ante’s second son Marijan was married to Milica, née Mikulić, and had sons Frane (who was killed in Vienna in 1945 at the age of eighteen) and Ante (who died as a small child), and daughters Marija and Stana. Marijan died of pneumonia at the age of thirty-five. He had no grandchildren through sons and his line died out.

2.2.3.1.1.3. Mate Antin (1910–1991)

Ante’s fourth son Mate was married to Mara Knežević. They had sons Ante Bruno and Frane, and daughters Ana, Dinka and Olga.

2.2.3.1.1.4. Nikola Antin (1919–1981)

Ante’s fifth son Nikola was married to Tomica Budimir and they had sons Ante (who died as a small child), Drago, Jerko and Ivan (who also died as a small child), and daughters Matija, Branka and Marija. Jerko was married to Stanka Racetin and they had only a daughter Ana. Jerko had no heir and his line died out. Drago is the only heir of the line and his sons now live in Ričice.

2.2.3.1.1.1.1. Lovre Ivanov (1925– )

Lovre was Ivan’s eldest son. With his wife Rozika Peška he has two daughters, Štefica and Ana. There are no male heirs of the line.

2.2.3.1.1.1.2. Mirko Ivanov (1928– )

Ivan’s second son Mirko was married to Danica Lukić and they had sons Ivan and Veselko, and a daughter Mirjana. Mirko’s elder son Ivan married Nada Kegalj and with her had sons Robert and Kristijan. The family lives in Predavac near Bjelovar. Mirko’s younger son Veselko married Martina Hohmann and they have a son Mirko and a daughter Janina.

2.2.3.1.1.1.3. Mate Ivanov (1932–1970)

Ivan’s third son Mate married Marija Srebrnjak and they have only a daughter Sandra. There are no male descendants.

2.2.3.1.1.1.4. Ante Vlade Ivanov (1934– )

Ivan’s fourth son Ante Vlade married twice. In his first marriage to Karina Mesch he had sons Johan and Franz, and a daughter Veronika. The elder son Johan and his wife Katarina Koniks have a son Gerd and a daughter Madita. In his second marriage to Tonka Zanne he has no children.

2.2.3.1.1.3.1. Ante Bruno Tandara, son of Mate (1937–1989)

Doctor Bruno was born on 7 October 1937 in Ričice. In 1968 he married Vanja Brkljača and they had a son Zvonimir and daughters Zrinka and Tomislava. Zvonimir and his wife Aneta Gacek have a son Oskar.

Dr. Ante Bruno Tandara (1937–1989) was a Croatian physician, member of parliament and social activist who took an active part in the Croatian Spring movement in the late 1960s and early 1970s. As a member of the Croatian reform movement he advocated greater rights for Croatia within the then Yugoslav federation, and participated in the work of Matica hrvatska and other public activities aimed at promoting Croatian national and cultural identity.

After the suppression of the Croatian Spring in 1971 he was arrested, convicted and imprisoned for his political activities. In Croatian public memory he remains one of the prominent participants of the Croatian Spring and a symbol of the political persecutions of that period.

2.2.3.1.2. Branch of Marijan, son of Lovro (1881–1939)

2.2.3.1.2.1. Mijo Marijanov (1908– )

Marijan’s eldest son Mijo, in marriage with Slavka, née Sablić, had sons Vinko, Ivan, Milan, Marijan and another Ivan (who died at the age of two), and daughters Mila-Iva, Anica, Nada and Maca. Only the sons Vinko and Milan continued Mijo’s line, while Marijan had no grandchildren through a son. The descendants of Vinko and Milan now live in Ričice, Split, Zagreb, Austria and Germany.

2.2.3.1.2.2. Marin Marijanov (1912–1989)

Marijan’s second son Marin married Matija Žuljić in 1938. They had sons Marijan (who died at the age of two), Alojz, Luka (who died as a one-year-old), Vice and Blaž, and daughters Dragica, Nevenka and Anka. Marin’s descendants now live in Ričice, Split, Zagreb and Germany.
