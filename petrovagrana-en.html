The Second Branch of the First TANDARA Family

Petar Tandara (1734–1817)

Petar Tandara was undoubtedly a son of Ivan Tandarić, most likely the second or third son in birth order, depending on the correlation with Filip of Livno as one of Ivan’s possible descendants. He died in 1817 at the age of eighty-three, which places his birth around 1734.

Petar was married to Jela Budić. They had sons Grgo, Jakov, Martin, Marijan, Mijo, Tadija and Marko, as well as daughters Manda and Jela. Sons Jakov, Marijan and Marko do not appear in the 1806 Census of Souls, while Tadija was omitted from the 1819 census, suggesting that they had either died or moved away from Ričice.

Only the sons Grgo, Martin and Mijo continued the family line.

Grgo, son of Petar (1770– )

Petar’s eldest son married Ruža Pušić. They had a son, Šimun, and a daughter, Iva. Since Šimun had no male heir, Grgo’s branch of the family became extinct.

Martin, son of Petar (1775–1844)

Petar’s son Martin married Ruža Lekić. They had sons Ivan, Jure, Mijo, Grgo, Filip and Josip, as well as daughters Matija and Ana.

No records have been found for Jure, Filip or Josip other than their baptismal entries, so it is unknown whether they died young or moved away. Only Ivan, Mijo and Grgo continued the family line.

Mijo, son of Petar (birth and death years unknown)

Mijo, son of Petar, was married to Ruža, whose maiden name is unknown. They had a daughter named Matija. Since there was no male heir, Mijo’s branch of the family ended.

Ivan, son of Martin (1807–1877)

Martin’s eldest son married Antica Lažeta in 1826. Ivan and Antica had sons Marijan, Toma and Jure, as well as daughters Ruža, Iva, Joza and Matija. Marijan died as a small child. No information has been found about Jure other than his birth record. Toma became the sole heir of Ivan’s line.

Mijo, son of Martin (1814– )

Martin’s third son and his wife Matija, née Parlov, had sons Martin and Mijo, who died in early childhood, as well as daughters Luca, Manda, Boža, Joza, Marija and Matija. No records have been found for Martin other than his birth record. It is not known whether he died or moved away. With Martin, Mijo’s line came to an end.

Grgo, son of Martin (1818– )

Martin’s fourth son married twice. From his first marriage to Rosa, née Budimir, he had a son Lovro and a daughter Jaka. In his second marriage to Jaka, née Knežević, Grgo had sons Mate, who died in childhood, and Mijo, as well as daughters Matija, Joza and Veronika.

TROMIĆI

Toma, son of Ivan (1830– )

Toma was Ivan’s second son and was married to Tomica Kovač. They had sons Ivan, Mate, Jure and another Ivan who died as a child. Of all their sons, only Jure left descendants.

Toma’s descendants in Zavelim were known by the nickname Tromići. According to local tradition, they were exceptionally tall and moved slowly. Because of these characteristics they earned the nickname Tromići.

The following section deals only with Jure’s line, as he was Toma’s sole heir.

LOVRO IN RIČICE

In 1819, the family of Martin Tandara, Lovro’s grandfather, was recorded in Zavelim. Martin’s sons Ivan, Mijo and Grgo also appear on that census. When the Austrian cadastral survey was conducted in 1835, Martin Tandara, son of Petar, was listed as a property owner in Ričice.

Most families from the Ričice clans owned property in Ričice, Zavelim, Podbila and Vir during that period. These villages belonged to the same parish in Ričice.

Many families within the same kinship group moved from one locality to another, even within Ričice itself. As the population grew rapidly, new living space was needed. In this context, it is understandable that a family, or part of a family, might appear in Ričice on one census and in Zavelim on the next, or vice versa.

Lovro, son of Grgo (1842–1922)

Grgo’s eldest son married Iva Parlov. They had sons Ante, Ivan and Marijan, as well as daughters Iva and Luca. No information has been found about Ivan other than his birth record.

The parish death register of Ričice records that Lovro’s daughter Luca was “killed by falling rocks” at the age of thirteen.

Mijo, son of Grgo (1856–1933)

Grgo’s third son married Iva Tandara. They had sons Martin, who died at the age of nineteen, Nikola, Mate, who died in childhood, and another Nikola, as well as daughters Iva and Mara.

No records have been found for the younger Nikola other than his birth record, while the elder Nikola died as a child. Mijo’s children left no descendants to continue the family line.

Jure, son of Toma (1874–1931)

Toma’s third son and sole heir married Iva Lažeta. They had sons Toma and Ivan, both of whom died in childhood, followed by Pio, Dominik and Mate, and a daughter, Matija.

Pio, son of Jure (1916–1990)

Jure’s third son married Terezija Budimir. They had sons Ivan, Jure and Joza, as well as a daughter Mara.

The eldest son, Ivan (1936–2025), and his wife Marijana Petter had a son, Dino, and a daughter, Romana. Dino and his wife Matilda Klepikapić have daughters Hedviga, Leonarda and Lucija. The family now lives in Zagreb.

Dominik, son of Jure (1919–1987)

Jure’s fourth son and his wife Mara Knezović had sons Ante, Bariša, Nikola, Pio, Sveto and Ivan, as well as a daughter Olga.

Only Ivan had sons, Dominik and David, who continue Dominik’s branch of the family.

Mate, son of Jure (1922–1963)

Jure’s fifth son married Mara Budić. They had sons Tomislav, Mijo and another son whose name is unknown from the available record, as well as a daughter Matija.

The two youngest sons died at the age of five.

Tomislav married Božica Golubiček and they had sons Martin and Mate. Mate married Ivana Trošelj and they had a daughter, Tena.

The descendants of Mate, son of Jure, now live in Zagreb.

CONTINUATION OF THE LARGEST BRANCH OF THE TANDARA SURNAME – LOVRO'S LINE (1842–1922)

As previously mentioned, Lovro had two sons, Ante and Marijan, who were the only continuers of the family line. Together, their sons had thirty-three children.

Ante, son of Lovro (1871–1933)

Ante, son of Lovro, was married to Tomica Lukić. They had sons Ivan, Marijan, Mate and Nikola, as well as daughters Marta, Iva and Matija.

Marijan, son of Lovro (1881–1939)

He married Luca Parlov. They had sons Mijo, Marin, Petar and Ivan, who died in childhood, as well as daughters Matija, Iva, Ana and Tomica.

BRANCH OF ANTE, SON OF LOVRO (1871–1933)

Ivan, son of Ante (1896– )

Ante’s eldest son married Mara Mršić. They had sons Lovro, Mirko, Mate, Ante Vlado and Nikola, as well as a daughter, Tomica.

Ivan moved to Slatina, and his descendants today live in Predavac near Bjelovar, Slatina, Obrovac, Zagreb and Germany.

Marijan, son of Ante (1898– )

Ante’s second son was married to Milica, née Mikulić. They had sons Frano, who was killed in Vienna in 1945 at the age of eighteen, and Ante, who died in childhood, as well as daughters Marija and Stana.

Marijan died of pneumonia at the age of thirty-five. His branch did not continue beyond his children.

Mate, son of Ante (1910–1991), father of DR. BRUNO TANDARA

Ante’s fourth son, Mate, was married to Mara Knežević. They had sons Ante Bruno, known as Dr. Bruno Tandara, and Frano, as well as daughters Ana, Dinka and Olga.

Nikola, son of Ante (1919–1981)

Ante’s fifth son was married to Tomica Budimir. They had sons Ante, who died in childhood, Drago, Jerko and Ivan, who also died in childhood, as well as daughters Matija, Branka and Marija.

The sons of Drago continue to live in Ričice.

Lovro, son of Ivan (1925– )

Lovro was Ivan’s eldest son. Together with his wife Rozika Peška, he had two daughters, Štefica and Ana.

There are no male descendants to continue the family line.

Mirko, son of Ivan (1928– )

Ivan’s second son was married to Danica Lukić. They had sons Ivan and Veselko, as well as a daughter, Mirjana.

Mirko’s elder son Ivan married Nada Kegalj, and they had sons Robert and Kristijan. The family lives in Predavac near Bjelovar.

Mirko’s younger son Veselko married Martina Hohmann. They have a son, Mirko, and a daughter, Janina.

Mate, son of Ivan (1932–1970)

Ivan’s third son married Marija Srebrnjak. They had only one daughter, Sandra.

There are no male descendants.

Ante Vlado, son of Ivan (1934– )

Ivan’s fourth son married twice. In his first marriage to Karina Mesch, he had sons Johan and Franz, as well as a daughter, Veronika.

The elder son Johan and his wife Katarina Koniks have a son, Gerd, and a daughter, Madita.

In his second marriage to Tonka Zanne, Ante Vlado had no children.

Ante Bruno Tandara, son of Mate (1937–1989)

Dr. Bruno was born on 7 October 1937 in Ričice. In 1968 he married Vanja Brkljača, and they had a son, Zvonimir, and daughters Zrinka and Tomislava.

Zvonimir and his wife Aneta Gacek have a son, Oskar.

Dr. Ante Bruno Tandara (1937–1989) was a Croatian physician, member of parliament and public figure who actively participated in the Croatian Spring movement during the late 1960s and early 1970s.

As a member of the Croatian reform movement, he advocated greater rights for Croatia within the former Yugoslav federation and took part in the work of Matica hrvatska and other public initiatives aimed at promoting Croatian national and cultural identity.

Following the suppression of the Croatian Spring in 1971, he was arrested, convicted and imprisoned because of his political activities.

In Croatian public memory, he remains one of the prominent participants of the Croatian Spring and a symbol of the political persecutions of that era.

Frano, son of Mate (1947– )

Mate’s younger son married Matija Topalušić. They have sons Mate and Ante, and a daughter, Elza.

Mate married Ana Marija Vlahović. They have sons Ante and Frano, as well as a daughter, Leonarda.

The family lives in Ričice.

Drago, son of Nikola (1952– )

Nikola’s second son and his wife Zdenka Budimir had two sons, Nikola and Ivica.

Ivica and his wife Jelena Perić have a son, Nikola, and a daughter, Laura.

The family lives in Ričice.

Jerko, son of Nikola (1953– )

Nikola’s third son married Stanka Racetin. They have a daughter, Ana.

There are no male descendants to continue the family line.

BRANCH OF MARIJAN, SON OF LOVRO (1881–1939)

Mijo, son of Marijan (1908– )

Marijan’s eldest son was married to Slavka, née Sablić. They had sons Vinko, Ivan, Milan, Marijan and another Ivan, who died at the age of two, as well as daughters Mila-Iva, Anica, Nada and Maca.

Today, the descendants of Vinko, Milan and Marijan live in Ričice, Split, Zagreb, Austria and Germany.

Marin, son of Marijan (1912–1989)

Marijan’s second son married Matija Žuljić in 1938. They had sons Marijan, who died at the age of two, Alojz, Luka, who died as a one-year-old child, Vice and Blaž, as well as daughters Dragica, Nevenka and Anka.

Today, Marin’s descendants live in Ričice, Split, Zagreb and Germany.

Vice, son of Marin (02 October 1949– )

Vice, son of Marin, is married to Ružica Barnjak, and they have no children.

Together with Milan Lažeta, Vice made one of the greatest contributions to the realization of the Tandara Clan Genealogy project, for which I remain eternally grateful.

Petar, son of Marijan (1915– )

Marijan’s third son was married to Zorka, née Pavić. They had a son, Ante, who died in his second year of life, and daughters Mila, Marija, Ankica, Mira, Zorka and Rosa.

Petar left no male heir to continue the family line.

Vinko, son of Mijo (1930– )

Mijo’s eldest son was married to Mara Dragica Budimir. They had sons Milenko, who died at the age of one, and Svetko, as well as daughters Dragica and Milosava.

Svetko married Nevenka Tavra, and they had a son, Kristijan.

Milan, son of Mijo (1938– )

Milan, son of Mijo, married Stana Tandara. They had three sons, Nediljko, Željko and Tihomir, and a daughter, Slavica.

Nediljko and his wife Vesna Mandić had a son, Milan, and daughters Ana Marija and Elizabeta.

Željko, son of Milan, married Valerija Glavota. They had three daughters: Mihaela, Marija and Stana.

Tihomir, son of Milan, married Anica Miloš. They have a son, Milan, and a daughter, Ivana.

Marijan, son of Mijo (1942– )

Marijan married Milenka Budimir. They have a son, Mario, and a daughter, Silvana.

Marijan has no descendants who continue the family line.

Alojz, son of Marin (1946– )

Alojz married Dragica Jurčević. They have a son, Robert, and a daughter, Zrinka.

Robert and his wife Sabine Patzak have a son, Marc Marin, and daughters Caroline Victoria and Laura Nadine.

Blaž, son of Marin (1953– )

Blaž married Zorana Čarija. They had three sons: Mario, Ante and Vice.

Mario and his wife Josipa Artić have a son, Lovro, and a daughter, Tonka.

Ante and his wife Tanja Koren have a son, Petar.
