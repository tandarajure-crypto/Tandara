THE SECOND BRANCH OF THE FIRST TANDARA FAMILY

2.0. Petar Tandara (1734–1817)

Petar Tandara was Ivan's second son. He died in 1817 at the age of eighty-three, so it is estimated that he was born around 1734. Petar was married to Jela Budić. They had seven sons—Grgo, Jakov, Martin, Marijan, Mijo, Tadija and Marko—and two daughters, Manda and Jela. The sons Jakov, Marijan and Marko do not appear in the 1806 Register of Souls, while Tadija is absent from the 1819 register. It may therefore be assumed that they were no longer alive or that one or more of them had moved away from Ričice. Mijo, son of Petar, had no male descendants, and his branch died out.

2.1. Grgo, son of Petar (1770– )

Grgo, Petar's eldest son, married Ruža Pušić. They had a son, Šimun, and a daughter, Iva.

2.1.1. Šimun, son of Grgo (1800–1874)

Šimun was married to Jela Budimir, with whom he had a son, Mate, and three daughters: Iva, Matija and Marta. Only Mate's year of birth is known, so neither the course of his later life nor the circumstances explaining the absence of further surviving records can be established.

2.2. Martin, son of Petar (1775–1844)

Martin, Petar's third son, married Ruža Lekić. They had six sons—Ivan, Jure, Mijo, Grgo, Filip and Josip—and two daughters, Matija and Ana. No records of Jure, Filip or Josip have been found apart from their baptismal entries, so it is not known whether they died young or moved away. Only Ivan and Grgo continued the family line.

2.3. Mijo, son of Petar (years of birth and death unknown)

Mijo, son of Petar, was married to Ruža, whose maiden name is unknown. They had a daughter named Matija. They had no son to continue the male line, and Mijo's branch therefore died out.

2.2.1. Ivan, son of Martin (1807–1877)

Martin's eldest son, Ivan, married Antica Lažeta in 1826. Ivan and Antica had three sons—Marijan, Toma and Jure—and four daughters: Ruža, Iva, Joza and Matija. Marijan died as a young child. No information about Jure has been found apart from the record of his birth. Toma was the only son to continue Ivan's line and became the founder of the Tromići branch.

2.2.2. Mijo, son of Martin (1814– )

Martin's third son, Mijo, and his wife Matija, née Parlov, had eight children: two sons, Martin and Mijo, the latter of whom died as a young child, and six daughters—Luca, Manda, Boža, Joza, Marija and Matija. No records of Martin have been found apart from the record of his birth. It is not known whether he died or moved away. After Martin, Mijo's male line died out.

2.2.3. Grgo, son of Martin (1818– )

Grgo married twice. From his first marriage to Rosa, née Budimir, he had a son, Lovro, and a daughter, Jaka. In his second marriage to Jaka, née Knežević, Grgo had two sons, Mate, who died as a young child, and Mijo, as well as three daughters: Matija, Joza and Veronika. His son Mijo from the second marriage had four sons and three daughters; however, none of Mijo's sons continued the male line.

THE BEGINNING OF MIGRATION FROM RIČICE

In 1819, the family of Martin Tandara, Lovro's grandfather, was living in Zavelim. Martin's sons Ivan, Mijo and Grgo were also listed there. When the Austrian cadastral survey was conducted in 1835, Martin Tandara, son of the late Petar, was recorded as a property owner in Ričice. At that time, most clans from Ričice owned property in Ričice, Zavelim, Podbila and Vir. These villages belonged to the same parish in Ričice. Many families within the same clan moved from one locality to another, even within Ričice itself. The population was growing rapidly, and new living space was needed. In this context, it is understandable that a family, or part of a family, could be recorded in Ričice in one census and in Zavelim in the next, or vice versa. It is known with certainty, however, that Grgo returned with his children from Zavelim to Ričice, where he continued his family life.

TROMIĆI

2.2.1.1. Toma, son of Ivan (1830– )

Toma was married to Tomica Kovač. They had four sons: Ivan, Mate, Jure and another son also named Ivan, who died as a child. Toma's only son who continued the family line was Jure. Toma's line in Zavelim was known by the nickname Tromići, which, according to family tradition, referred to their tall stature and slow, heavy gait.

LINE OF JURE, SON OF TOMA

2.2.1.1.1. Jure, son of Toma (1874–1931)

Toma's third son and only successor to the family line, Jure, married Iva Lažeta. They had five sons—Toma and Ivan, who both died as young children, followed by Pio, Dominik and Mate—and a daughter named Matija.

2.2.1.1.1.1. Pio, son of Jure (1916–1990)

Pio, Jure's third son, married Terezija Budimir. They had three sons—Ivan, Jure and Jozo—and a daughter named Mara.

2.2.1.1.1.1.1. Ivan, son of Pio (1935–2025)

Ivan married Marijana Petter. They had a son, Dino, and a daughter, Romana. The family lives in Zagreb.

2.2.1.1.1.1.1.1. Dino, son of Ivan

Dino married Matilda Klepikapić. They have three daughters: Hedviga, Leonarda and Lucija. Dino has no male descendants. The family lives in Zagreb.

2.2.1.1.1.1.2. Jure, son of Pio (1943–2004)

Jure was married to Mila Škorić. They had a son, Tomislav, and three daughters: Marija, Angela and Nediljka. Tomislav never married. The family lives in Split.

2.2.1.1.1.1.3. Jozo, son of Pio (1949– )

Jozo married Mirjana Generalić. They have a son, Domagoj, and two daughters, Tereza and Zrinka. The family lives in Split.

2.2.1.1.1.2. Dominik, son of Jure (1919–1987)

Dominik, Jure's fourth son, married Mara Knezović. They had six sons—Ante, Bariša, Nikola, Pio, Sveto and Ivan—and a daughter named Olga. Ante, Nikola, Pio and Sveto have no sons. No information about Bariša's marriage has been found. Only Ivan and his sons continued Dominik's male line.

2.2.1.1.1.2.1. Ante, son of Dominik (1948– )

Ante married Dubravka Pencinger. They had two daughters, Ana Marija and Ozana. The family lives in Križevci.

2.2.1.1.1.2.2. Bariša, son of Dominik (1950– )

No further information about Bariša is currently available.

2.2.1.1.1.2.3. Nikola, son of Dominik (1953– )

Nikola married Nada Jurčević. They had four daughters: Marijana, Iva, Brigita and Anita. Nikola returned to the hamlet of Tandare in Zavelim.

2.2.1.1.1.2.4. Pio, son of Dominik (1954– )

Pio married Ljubina Krinulović. They had two daughters, Tina and Pia. The family lives in Vienna, Austria.

2.2.1.1.1.2.5. Sveto, son of Dominik (1957– )

Sveto married twice. In his first marriage to Petra Lukić, he had a daughter named Daniela. In his second marriage to Vera Rakin, he had a daughter named Eni.

2.2.1.1.1.2.6. Ivan, son of Dominik (1963– )

Ivan married Mirjana Tandara. They have two sons, Dominik and David. The family lives in the hamlet of Tandare in Zavelim.

2.2.1.1.1.3. Mate, son of Jure (1922–1963)

Mate was Jure's fifth son. He married Mara Budić. They had a son named Tomislav, two other sons who died at the age of five, and a daughter named Matija.

2.2.1.1.1.3.1. Tomislav, son of Mate (1947–2014)

Tomislav married Božica Golubiček. They have a son, Mate, and a daughter, Martina.

2.2.1.1.1.3.1.1. Mate, son of Tomislav (1980– )

Mate married Ivana Trošelj. They have a daughter named Tena. Mate's descendants live in Zagreb.

LINE OF LOVRO, SON OF GRGO

2.2.3.1. Lovro, son of Grgo (1842–1922)

Lovro married Iva Parlov. They had two sons, Ante and Marijan, and three daughters: Lucija, who died as a child, Iva, and another daughter also named Lucija. Ante and Marijan were the only sons who continued the family line. Their sons had a total of thirty-three children. The Ričice parish death register records that Lovro's daughter Luca was “killed by stones” at the age of thirteen.

2.2.3.2. Mijo, son of Grgo (1856–1933)

Mijo, Grgo's third son, married Iva Tandara. They had seven children: Martin, the first Nikola, Mate, the second Nikola, Iva, the first Mara and the second Mara. Martin died at the age of nineteen, Mate died as a young child, and the older Nikola also died as a child. No information about the younger Nikola has been found apart from the record of his birth. There is no evidence that any of Mijo's sons married or had children, and Mijo's branch therefore died out.

2.2.3.1.1. Ante, son of Lovro (1871–1933)

Ante was married to Tomica Lukić. They had five sons—Ivan, Marijan, the first Mate, who died as a child, the second Mate, and Nikola—and three daughters: Marta, Iva and Matija. Ivan, Marijan, the second Mate and Nikola continued the family line.

2.2.3.1.1.1. Ivan, son of Ante (1896– )

Ivan, Ante's eldest son, married Mara Mršić. They had five sons—Lovro, Mirko, Mate, Ante Vlade and Nikola—and a daughter named Tomica. Ivan moved to Slatina. His descendants now live in Predavac near Bjelovar, Slatina, Obrovac, Zagreb and Germany.

2.2.3.1.1.2. Marijan, son of Ante (1898–1933)

Marijan, Ante's second son, was married to Milica, née Mikulić. They had two sons—Frane, who was killed in Vienna in 1945 at the age of eighteen, and Ante, who died as a young child—and two daughters, Marija and Stana. Marijan died of pneumonia at the age of thirty-five. Neither of his sons produced descendants, and Marijan's male line died out.

2.2.3.1.1.3. Mate, son of Ante (1910–1991)

Mate, Ante's fourth son, was married to Mara Knežević. They had two sons, Ante Bruno and Frane, and three daughters: Ana, Dinka and Olga.

2.2.3.1.1.4. Nikola, son of Ante (1919–1981)

Nikola, Ante's fifth son, was married to Tomica Budimir. They had four sons—Ante, who died as a young child, Drago, Jerko and Ivan, who also died as a young child—and three daughters: Matija, Branka and Marija. Jerko was married to Stanka Racetin, and they had only one daughter, Ana. Drago was the only son to continue Nikola's male line, and his sons now live in Ričice.

2.2.3.1.1.1.1. Lovre, son of Ivan (1925– )

Lovre was Ivan's eldest son. He and his wife Rozika Peška had two daughters, Štefica and Ana.

2.2.3.1.1.1.2. Mirko, son of Ivan (1928– )

Mirko married Danica Lukić. They had two sons, Ivan and Veselko, and a daughter named Mirjana. Mirko's elder son Ivan married Nada Kegalj, and they had two sons, Robert and Kristijan. The family lives in Predavac near Bjelovar. Mirko's younger son Veselko married Martina Hohmann, and they have a son, Mirko, and a daughter, Janina.

2.2.3.1.1.1.3. Mate, son of Ivan (1932–1970)

Mate, Ivan's third son, married Marija Srebrnjak. They have one daughter, Sandra, and no male descendants.

2.2.3.1.1.1.4. Ante Vlade, son of Ivan (1934– )

Ante Vlade, Ivan's fourth son, married twice. In his first marriage to Karina Mesch, he had two sons, Johan and Franz, and a daughter named Veronika. His elder son Johan and Johan's wife, Katarina Koniks, have a son, Gerd, and a daughter, Madita. Ante Vlade had no children from his second marriage to Tonka Zanne.

2.2.3.1.1.3.1. Ante Bruno Tandara, son of Mate (1937–1989)

Dr Bruno was born in Ričice on 7 October 1937. In 1968, he married Vanja Brkljača. They had a son, Zvonimir, and two daughters, Zrinka and Tomislava. Zvonimir and his wife Aneta Gacek have a son named Oskar.

Dr Ante Bruno Tandara (1937–1989) was a Croatian physician, member of parliament and public figure who took an active part in the Croatian Spring movement in the late 1960s and early 1970s. As a member of the Croatian reform movement, he advocated greater rights for Croatia within the Yugoslav federation of the time. He also participated in the work of Matica hrvatska and in other public activities aimed at promoting Croatian national and cultural identity.

After the suppression of the Croatian Spring in 1971, he was arrested, convicted and imprisoned because of his political activities. He is remembered in Croatian public life as one of the prominent participants in the Croatian Spring and as a symbol of the political persecution of that period.

Frane, Mate's younger son, married Matija Topalušić. They have two sons, Mate and Ante, and a daughter named Elza. Mate married Ana Marija Vlahović. They have two sons, Ante and Frane, and a daughter named Leonarda. The family lives in Ričice.

LINE OF MARIJAN, SON OF LOVRO

Marijan married Luca Parlov. They had four sons—Mijo, Marin, Petar and Ivan—and four daughters: Matija, Ana, Iva and Tomica. Ivan died shortly after birth.

2.2.3.1.2.1. Mijo, son of Marijan (1908– )

Marijan's eldest son, Mijo, was married to Slavka, née Sablić. They had five sons—Vinko, Ivan, Milan, Marijan and another son also named Ivan, who died at the age of two—and four daughters: Mila-Iva, Anica, Nada and Maca. Only Vinko and Milan continued Mijo's male line. Vinko's and Milan's descendants now live in Ričice, Split, Zagreb, Austria and Germany.

2.2.3.1.2.1.2.2. Željko, son of Milan (1967–2020)
