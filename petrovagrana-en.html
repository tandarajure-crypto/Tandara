THE SECOND BRANCH OF THE FIRST TANDARA FAMILY

Petar Tandara (c.1734–1817)

Petar Tandara was Ivan’s second or third son, depending on the possible connection with Filip from Livno as one of Ivan’s potential descendants. He died in 1817 at the age of eighty-three, so it is estimated that he was born around 1734.

Petar was married to Jela Budić. They had sons Grgo, Jakov, Martin, Marijan, Mijo, Tadija and Marko, and daughters Manda and Jela. The sons Jakov, Marijan and Marko do not appear in the 1806 Register of Souls, while Tadija is absent from the 1819 register, so it may be assumed that they were no longer alive or that one of them had moved away from Ričice. Mijo had no male descendants, and his branch died out.

2.1. Grgo, son of Petar (1770– )

Petar’s eldest son married Ruža Pušić. They had a son, Šimun, and a daughter, Iva. Šimun and his wife Jela Budimir had a son, Mate, and daughters Iva, Matija and Marta. Mate had no male heir, and therefore Grgo’s branch came to an end.

2.2. Martin, son of Petar (1775–1844)

Martin, Petar’s third son, married Ruža Lekić. They had six sons: Ivan, Jure, Mijo, Grgo, Filip and Josip, and daughters Matija and Ana. There are no marriage records for Mijo’s sons, and therefore his family line died out.

No other records have been found for Jure, Filip and Josip apart from those concerning their baptisms, so it is not known whether they died young or moved away. The sons Ivan and Grgo were the only ones who continued the family line.

2.3. Mijo, son of Petar (years of birth and death unknown)

Mijo, son of Petar, was married to Ruža, whose maiden surname is unknown. They had a daughter, Matija. They had no male heir, and therefore Mijo’s branch died out.

2.2.1. Ivan, son of Martin (1807–1877)

Martin’s eldest son married Antica Lažeta in 1826. Ivan and Antica had three sons: Marijan, Toma and Jure, and daughters Ruža, Iva, Joza and Matija. Marijan died as a young child. No other information about Jure has been found apart from the record of his birth. Toma was the only heir to Ivan’s family line and the founder of the Tromići branch.

2.2.2. Mijo, son of Martin (1814– )

Martin’s third son and his wife Matija, née Parlov, had two sons: Martin and Mijo, who died as a young child, and daughters Luca, Manda, Boža, Joza, Marija and Matija. There are no other records of Martin apart from the record of his birth. It is not known whether he died or moved away. After Martin, Mijo’s family line died out.

2.2.3. Grgo, son of Martin (1818– )

Martin’s fourth son married twice. From his first marriage to Rosa, née Budimir, he had a son, Lovro, and a daughter, Jaka. In his second marriage to Jaka, née Knežević, Grgo had sons Mate, who died as a young child, and Mijo, and daughters Matija, Joza and Veronika.

The Tromići

2.2.1.1. Toma, son of Ivan (1830– )

Toma, Ivan’s second son, was married to Tomica Kovač. They had sons Ivan, Mate, Jure and another son named Ivan, who died as a child. Toma’s only heir was his son Jure.

Toma’s family line in Zavelim was known by the nickname Tromići, which, according to tradition, arose from their height and their slow, heavy gait. Today’s descendants from Dominik’s branch are the complete opposite—spirited and highly active.

The following account relates exclusively to the family line of Jure, son of Toma.

2.2.1.1.1. Jure, son of Toma (1874–1931)

Toma’s third son and only heir married Iva Lažeta. They had sons Toma and Ivan, who died as young children, followed by Pio, Dominik and Mate, and a daughter, Matija.

2.2.1.1.1.1. Pio, son of Jure (1916–1990)

Jure’s third son married Terezija Budimir, with whom he had sons Ivan, Jure and Jozo, and a daughter, Mara. The eldest son, Ivan (1936–2025), and his wife Marijana Petter had a son, Dino, and a daughter, Romana. Dino and his wife Matilda Klepikapić have daughters Hedviga, Leonarda and Lucija, and no male heir. The family lives in Zagreb today.

2.2.1.1.1.2. Dominik, son of Jure (1919–1987)

Jure’s fourth son and his wife Mara Knezović had sons Ante, Bariša, Nikola, Pio, Sveto and Ivan, and a daughter, Olga. Of all Dominik’s children, only Ivan has sons, Dominik and David, who are the only ones continuing Dominik’s branch.

2.2.1.1.1.3. Mate, son of Jure (1922–1963)

Jure’s fifth son married Mara Budić, with whom he had sons Tomislav, Mijo and........, and a daughter, Matija. The two youngest sons died at the age of five. Tomislav married Božica Golubiček, with whom he has sons Martin and Mate. Mate married Ivana Trošelj, with whom he had a daughter, Tena. The descendants of Mate, son of Jure, live in Zagreb today.

The Beginning of Migration beyond Ričice

In 1819, the family of Martin Tandara (1775–1844), Lovro’s grandfather, was living in Zavelim. Martin’s sons Ivan, Mijo and Grgo also appear in that register. When the Austrian cadastral survey was conducted in 1835, Martin Tandara, son of the late Petar, was recorded as the owner of real estate in Ričice. At that time, most Ričice clans owned property in Ričice, Zavelim, Podbila and Vir. These villages belonged to the same parish in Ričice. Many families belonging to the same clan moved from one locality to another, even within Ričice itself. The population was growing rapidly, and new places to live were being sought. In this context, we can understand and accept the fact that a family, or part of a family, was recorded in Ričice in one census and in Zavelim in the next, or vice versa. However, it is known with certainty that Grgo returned from Zavelim to Ričice with his children and continued living there with his family.

2.2.3.1. Lovro, son of Grgo (1842–1922)

Grgo’s eldest son married Iva Parlov and had sons Ante, Ivan and Marijan with her, and daughters Iva and Luca. There is no other information about Ivan apart from the record of his birth. The Death Register of the Parish of Ričice records that Lovro’s daughter Luca was “killed by rocks” at the age of thirteen.

2.2.3.2. Mijo, son of Grgo (1856–1933)

Grgo’s third son married Iva Tandara, with whom he had seven children: sons Martin, who died at the age of nineteen, Nikola and Mate, who died as a young child, and another son named Nikola, as well as daughters Iva and Mara. There is no other information about the younger Nikola apart from the record of his birth, while the elder Nikola died as a child. Mijo’s children had no descendants, and therefore Mijo’s branch died out.

2.2.3.1. Lovro’s Branch (1842–1922)

Lovro and his wife Iva Parlov had sons Ante and Marijan, and daughters Lucija, who died as a child, Iva and another daughter named Lucija. Ante and Marijan were the only heirs to the family line. Their sons had thirty-three children.

2.2.3.1.1. Ante, son of Lovro (1871–1933)

Ante, son of Lovro, was married to Tomica Lukić, with whom he had sons Ivan, Marijan, the first Mate, who died as a child, the second Mate and Nikola, and daughters Marta, Iva and Matija. The heirs to the family line were Ivan, Marijan, the second son Mate, and Nikola.

2.2.3.1.2. Marijan, son of Lovro (1881– )

He married Luca Parlov, with whom he had sons Mijo, Marin, Petar and Ivan, who died as a young child, and daughters Matija, Iva, Ana and Tomica.

2.2.3.1.1.1. Ivan, son of Ante (1896– )

Ante’s eldest son married Mara Mršić. They had sons Lovro, Mirko, Mate, Ante Vlade and Nikola, and a daughter, Tomica. Ivan moved to Slatina, and his descendants now live in Predavac near Bjelovar, Slatina, Obrovac, Zagreb and Germany.

2.2.3.1.1.2. Marijan, son of Ante (1898–1933)

Ante’s second son was married to Milica, née Mikulić, and had sons Frane, who was killed in Vienna in 1945 at the age of eighteen, and Ante, who died as a young child, as well as daughters Marija and Stana. Marijan died of pneumonia at the age of thirty-five. Marijan had no grandchildren through his sons, and his family line died out.

2.2.3.1.1.3. Mate, son of Ante (1910–1991)

Ante’s fourth son, Mate, was married to Mara Knežević. They had sons Ante Bruno and Frane, and daughters Ana, Dinka and Olga.

2.2.3.1.1.4. Nikola, son of Ante (1919–1981)

Ante’s fifth son was married to Tomica Budimir, and they had sons Ante, who died as a young child, Drago, Jerko and Ivan, who also died as a young child, and daughters Matija, Branka and Marija. Jerko was married to Stanka Racetin, and they had only one daughter, Ana. Jerko had no male heir, and his family line died out. Drago is the only heir to the family line, and his sons live in Ričice today.

2.2.3.1.1.1.1. Lovre, son of Ivan (1925– )

Lovre was Ivan's eldest son. Together with his wife Rozika Peška, he had two daughters, Štefica and Ana. There were no male heirs to continue the family line.

2.2.3.1.1.1.2. Mirko, son of Ivan (1928– )

Ivan's second son married Danica Lukić. They had sons Ivan and Veselko, and a daughter, Mirjana. Mirko's elder son Ivan married Nada Kegalj, and they had sons Robert and Kristijan. The family lives in Predavac near Bjelovar. Mirko's younger son Veselko married Martina Hohmann, and they have a son, Mirko, and a daughter, Janina.

2.2.3.1.1.1.3. Mate, son of Ivan (1932–1970)

Ivan's third son married Marija Srebrnjak. They had only one daughter, Sandra. There were no male descendants.

2.2.3.1.1.1.4. Ante Vlade, son of Ivan (1934– )

Ivan's fourth son married twice. In his first marriage to Karina Mesch, he had sons Johan and Franz, and a daughter, Veronika. The elder son Johan and his wife Katarina Koniks have a son, Gerd, and a daughter, Madita. His second marriage to Tonka Zanne produced no children.

2.2.3.1.1.3.1. Ante Bruno Tandara Matin (1937–1989)

Dr. Bruno was born on 7 October 1937 in Ričice. In 1968 he married Vanja Brkljača, with whom he had a son, Zvonimir, and daughters Zrinka and Tomislava. Zvonimir and his wife Aneta Gacek have a son, Oskar.

Dr. Ante Bruno Tandara (1937–1989) was a Croatian physician, Member of Parliament and public figure who actively participated in the Croatian Spring movement during the late 1960s and early 1970s. As a member of the Croatian reform movement, he advocated greater rights for Croatia within the former Yugoslav federation and took part in the activities of Matica hrvatska and other public initiatives promoting Croatian national and cultural identity.

Following the suppression of the Croatian Spring in 1971, he was arrested, convicted and imprisoned because of his political activities. In Croatian public life, he is remembered as one of the prominent participants of the Croatian Spring and as a symbol of the political persecutions of that period.

2.2.3.1.1.2.2. Frane, son of Mate (1947– )

Mate's younger son married Matija Topalušić. They had sons Mate and Ante, and a daughter, Elza. Mate married Ana Marija Vlahović, and they have sons Ante and Frane, and a daughter, Leonarda. They live in Ričice.

2.2.3.1.1.3.1. Drago, son of Nikola (1952– )

Nikola's second son and his wife Zdenka Budimir had two sons, Nikola and Ivica. Ivica and his wife Jelena Perić have a son, Nikola, and a daughter, Laura. They live in Ričice.

2.2.3.1.1.3.2. Jerko, son of Nikola (1953– )

Nikola's third son married Stanka Racetin. They have one daughter, Ana. There are no male descendants.

2.2.3.1.2. The Branch of Marijan, son of Lovro (1881–1939)

2.2.3.1.2.1. Mijo, son of Marijan (1908– )

Marijan's eldest son married Slavka, née Sablić. They had sons Vinko, Ivan, Milan, Marijan and another son named Ivan, who died at the age of two, as well as daughters Mila-Iva, Anica, Nada and Maca. Only the sons Vinko and Milan continued Mijo's family line, while Marijan had no grandsons through his son. Today, the descendants of Vinko and Milan live in Ričice, Split, Zagreb, Austria and Germany.

2.2.3.1.2.2. Marin, son of Marijan (1912–1989)

Marijan's second son married Matija Žuljić in 1938. They had sons Marijan, who died at the age of two, Alojz, Luka, who died as a one-year-old child, Vice and Blaž, as well as daughters Dragica, Nevenka and Anka. Marin's descendants now live in Ričice, Split, Zagreb and Germany.

2.2.3.1.2.3. Petar, son of Marijan (1915– )

Marijan's third son married Zorka, née Pavić. They had a son, Ante, who died in his second year of life, and daughters Mila, Marija, Ankica, Mira, Zorka and Rosa. Petar had no male heir to continue the family line.

2.2.3.1.2.1.1. Vinko, son of Mijo (1930– )

Mijo's eldest son was married to Mara Dragica Budimir. They had sons Milenko, who died at the age of one, and Svetko, together with daughters Dragica and Milosava. Svetko married Nevenka Tavra, and they have a son, Kristijan.

2.2.3.1.2.1.2. Milan, son of Mijo (1938– )

Milan married Stana Tandara. They had three sons—Nediljko, Željko and Tihomir—and a daughter, Slavica. Nediljko and his wife Vesna Mandić had a son, Milan, and daughters Ana Marija and Elizabeta.

2.2.3.1.2.1.3. Marijan, son of Mijo (1942– )

Marijan married Milenka Budimir. They have a son, Mario, and a daughter, Silvana. Mario has no descendants to continue the family line.

2.2.3.1.2.2.1. Alojz, son of Marin (1946– )

Alojz married Dragica Jurčević. They have a son, Robert, and a daughter, Zrinka. Robert and his wife Sabine Patzak have a son, Marc Marin, and daughters Caroline Victoria and Laura Nadine.

2.2.3.1.2.2.2. Vice, son of Marin (02 October 1949– )

Vice is married to Ružica Barnjak and they have no children. He is mentioned here in recognition of his important contribution to the launch of this project.

2.2.3.1.2.2.3. Blaž, son of Marin (1953– )

Blaž married Zorana Čarija. They had three sons—Mario, Ante and Vice. Mario and his wife Josipa Artić have a son, Lovro, and a daughter, Tonka. Ante and his wife Tanja Koren have a son, Petar.

2.2.3.1.2.1.1.1. Svetko, son of Vinko (1960– )

Svetko married Nevenka Tavra. They have a son, Kristijan.

2.2.3.1.2.1.2.1. Nediljko, son of Milan (1962– )

Nediljko married Vesna Mandić. They have a son, Milan, and daughters Ana Marija and Elizabeta.

2.2.3.1.2.1.2.2. Željko, son of Milan (1967–2020)

Željko married Valerija Glavota. They had three daughters: Mihaela, Marija and Stana. There are no male descendants to continue the family line.

2.2.3.1.2.1.2.3. Tihomir, son of Milan (1969– )

Tihomir is married to Anica Miloš. They have a son, Milan, and a daughter, Ivana.

2.2.3.1.2.2.1.1. Robert, son of Alojz (1970– )

Robert is married to Sabine Patzak. They have a son, Marc Marin, and daughters Caroline Victoria and Laura Nadine.

Mario is married to Josipa Artić. They have a son, Lovro, and a daughter, Tonka.

2.2.3.1.2.2.3.2. Ante, son of Blaž (1980– )
