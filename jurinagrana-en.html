THE FIRST BRANCH OF THE FIRST TANDARA FAMILY

1.0. Jure Tandara – son of Ivan (★ c. 1725 – ✝ D.U.)

Jure Tandara, born between 1720 and 1725, was the eldest son of Ivan Tandarić, under whom the family surname later became established as Tandara. Jure married Matija née Lekić. They had one son, Frane, and two daughters, Anđa and Ana.

Family member codes: 0.0 Ivan Tandara (★ D.U. – ✝ D.U.) | 1.0-SP-1 Matija née Lekić (★ D.U. – ✝ D.U.) | 1.1 Frane, son of Jure (★ D.U. – ✝ D.U.) | 1.0-K1 Anđa, daughter of Jure (★ D.U. – ✝ D.U.) | 1.0-K2 Ana, daughter of Jure (★ D.U. – ✝ D.U.) .

1.1. Frane, son of Jure (★ D.U. – ✝ D.U.)

Frane, son of Jure, married Boža née Ćosić. In 1792 they had a son, Nikola.

Family member codes: 1.1-SP-1 Boža née Ćosić (★ D.U. – ✝ D.U.) | 1.1-S1 Nikola, son of Frane (★ 1792 – ✝ D.U.) .

1.1-S1. Nikola, son of Frane (★ 1792 – ✝ D.U.)

Nikola was the only known child of Frane, son of Jure, and Boža née Ćosić. It is not known whether he married or had children.

No member of Jure's family was recorded in the 1806 census of Ričice. What happened to this family line afterward is unknown. It is assumed that the family moved away, after which no reliable documented trace of the line has been found.

It would be valuable if a descendant of this branch were one day to come forward and help confirm the continuation of Jure's family line.
