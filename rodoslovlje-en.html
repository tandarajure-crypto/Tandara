Genealogy of the Tandara Family

Introduction

The Tandara lineage belongs to older family groups of the Imotski region, whose development can be traced from the first half of the 18th century to the present day. Thanks to preserved parish registers, land books, cadastral records, and family oral traditions, it is possible to reconstruct the development of the family over more than three centuries.

This genealogy is not intended only as a presentation of family trees, but as a permanent collection of data about the people who built and maintained the Tandara lineage throughout different historical circumstances. Its aim is to connect the past and the present and to preserve a reliable record for future generations.

Purpose of the Genealogy

Every genealogy has a dual value. The first is historical, as it allows the reconstruction of a family’s development over time. The second is personal, as it enables each descendant to learn about their ancestors, understand family connections, and preserve the memory of people whose lives would otherwise gradually be forgotten.

The Tandara genealogy project was created with exactly this intention — to combine available historical sources and family materials into a single unified whole.

Sources of Research

The genealogy is based on several groups of sources. The most important among them are:

  • parish registers of baptisms, marriages, and deaths
  • land registers and cadastral records
  • archival documents
  • family documents
  • oral tradition
  • research by Milan Lažeta

By comparing different sources, it was possible to confirm most family relationships and correct certain inaccuracies often found in oral tradition.

The Oldest Known Generations

The earliest reliable data about the lineage dates back to the first half of the 18th century. From that time onward, parish records allow for a nearly continuous reconstruction of the family across generations.

Each new generation introduced new branches, families, and surnames through marriage connections, creating a complex network of relationships that expanded beyond the original area over time.

Development of Family Branches

As the number of members grew, individual families established separate households. From one common family, distinct branches gradually emerged while remaining connected through shared ancestry.

Although they lived in different parts of the village or later in other regions, the awareness of belonging to the same lineage was preserved across generations.

Family and Everyday Life

During the 18th and 19th centuries, most members of the lineage lived from agriculture and livestock farming, which was typical for the population of the Imotski region.

Households often included multiple generations under one roof, allowing the preservation of shared property, mutual assistance, and the transfer of knowledge between older and younger members.

Family Trees

The central part of this genealogy consists of family trees. They provide an overview of all known generations and clearly illustrate the relationships between different branches of the lineage.

For each individual, when available, the following data are included: year of birth, year of death, spouse, descendants, and place of residence.

Photographs and Family Heritage

A special value of the genealogy lies in preserved photographs, old family documents, and other historical materials.

Such sources complement written records and preserve not only names and dates but also a part of the everyday life of previous generations.

Each new photograph or document represents a valuable contribution to the further development of this project.

An Ongoing Project

The Tandara genealogy is not a finished study. With the discovery of new documents, photographs, or archival sources, existing data can be expanded or corrected.

Therefore, this project is designed as an open and continuously developing archive.

Conclusion

Over more than three centuries, the Tandara lineage evolved from a single family into a broad network of descendants living in different parts of Croatia and the world.

This genealogy represents an attempt to preserve that shared history in one place — not only as a list of names and dates, but as a testimony to the lives of the people who shaped the identity of the Tandara lineage.
