Prva grana prve obitelji TANDARA

Jure Tandara – Ivanov (rođen oko 1725 – )

Jure Tandara, rođen između 1720. i 1725., bio je najstariji sin Ivana Tandarića koji je kasnije ustalio obiteljsko prezime u Tandara. Oženio se s Matijom Lekić i imali su sina Franu i dvije kćeri, Anđu i Anu.

Frane je bio oženjen s Božom Ćosić i 1792. godine su dobili sina Nikolu.

Kod popisa stanovništva u Ričicama iz 1806. godine nije zabilježen niti jedan član Jurine obitelji i nije poznato što se dogodilo s njegovom lozom. Pretpostavka je da su odselili, nakon čega im se do danas izgubio svaki trag.

The First Branch of the TANDARA Family Tree

Jure Tandara, son of Ivan (born around 1725 – )

According to archival records, Jure Tandara (born between 1720–1725) was the eldest son of Ivan Tandarić, who later established the family surname as Tandara. He was married to Matija Lekić and they had a son, Frane, and two daughters, Anđa and Ana.

Frane was married to Boža Ćosić and in 1792 they had a son, Nikola.

In the 1806 census of Ričice, no member of Jure’s family was recorded, and it is not known what happened to his family. It is assumed that they moved away, after which all trace of them was lost.
