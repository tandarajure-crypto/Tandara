Ivan Tandara

TANDARA-SURNAME – A digital archive dedicated to the Tandara surname, genealogy, family tree, historical records, surname origins, and the migrations of the Tandara family in Croatia and Bosnia and Herzegovina.

Ivan Tandara, born c. 1695

Ivan’s page is now the central overview of the four main family branches continued through his sons Jure, Petar, Ante and Mate. The opening box contains Ivan Tandara alone, and from him all four filial lines branch out.

The combined diagram retains all existing information, codes and descendants from the four completed diagrams. Each of the four branches is connected to Ivan as the common starting point, and the sons’ family branches also include their wives. Clicking a person opens the description and the next generations, while the buttons above the diagram can isolate a single branch.

The separate pages for all four branches remain available in the left-hand menu.

Instructions for using the diagram

  1. Enter the known information about a person in the search field and select Find.
  2. Selecting a person’s box opens their information, while the + and − symbols open or close descendants.
  3. The expand, collapse, three-generation and fit buttons adjust how the diagram is displayed.
  4. Because many people share the same first name, enter the person’s name, father’s name and mother’s name for a precise match. You may also add the surname Tandara, but a first name and surname alone are often not enough to identify one person; the search includes both parents’ names.

Combined family-tree diagram of Ivan Tandara

The diagram begins with Ivan alone in the starting box and joins the four existing branch diagrams into one searchable family tree, including wives, daughters, unmarried sons and other recorded family members.
