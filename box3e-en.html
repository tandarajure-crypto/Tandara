Ante’s sub-branch

Ante Tandara and Matija born Jukić

Ante Tandara: ★ 1769  –  ✝ 1849

Wife Matija born Jukić: ★ unknown  –  ✝ unknown

They had eight children: sons Lovro, Mijo and Petar, and daughters Pera, Ruža, Antica, Iva and Marija.

No information about Petar has been found other than his birth record, so it is not known whether he died young or moved away from Zavelim. The family line was continued by the sons Lovro and Mijo.

Mijo’s descendants received the nickname Tucići. Lovro’s descendants through his son Marijan became known as Lovrinovići, while those through his son Križan became known as Križanovići.

According to the 1819 census, Ante lived in Zavelim, while his brother Filip lived in Ričice. Their descendants later moved throughout Croatia, Europe, the United States and Canada.

How to use the diagram

  1. Enter the name or code of a family member in the Name or code field, then select Find.
  2. Expand opens Ante’s entire sub-branch and displays all recorded descendants.
  3. Collapse closes the expanded parts of the diagram.
  4. Up to 3 generations displays the first three generations of the family sub-branch.
  5. Fit adjusts the complete diagram to the available space on the screen.
  6. To trace your family line, first find a known family member and then follow the connecting lines towards that person’s ancestors and descendants.
