TANDARA FROM LIVNO – FILIP’S BRANCH

5.0. Filip Tandara – Founder of the Livno Branch

The Filip branch may be connected with the family of Ivan Tandara from Ričice, but the available documents do not yet confirm such a relationship. To prove this assumption, DNA samples from descendants of both Tandara branches would need to be analysed.

Filip is therefore assigned the designation 5.0 as a possible fifth son of Ivan Tandara, who was born around 1695 in Ričice. His children bear the designations 5.1–5.4, and each subsequent generation receives an additional number based on its parent.

Note: The original text states that Jakov had daughters named Josipa, Pera and Kata. Therefore, the designation 5.3.1 is assigned to Josipa — daughter of Jakov, not to a male Josip — son of Jakov.

Origin of Filip’s Line

Filip Tandarić is mentioned in 1768 as a resident of the village of Lipa in the Livno parish, where he lived with his family of eight. It is not known where he came from or when he arrived in Lipa, because no direct written records concerning this have yet been found.

Nevertheless, several indications support the assumption that the Tandara families of Livno belong to the family tree of Ivan Tandara of Ričice, born around 1695.

Ivan of Ričice had sons named Jure, Petar, Ante and Mate, while Filip of Livno named his sons Mate, Jure, Jakov and Ivan. Filip’s son Ivan may have been named after his possible grandfather Ivan of Ričice, and Filip’s son Mate may have been named after Mate of Ričice. The similarity continues in subsequent generations, in which the names Ivan, Petar and Filip recur.

Because of these similarities, Filip is designated in this genealogical account as 5.0 – a possible fifth son of Ivan Tandara of Ričice. However, because no document has yet been found that directly connects Filip with Ivan Tandara, this relationship must be presented as a genealogical hypothesis rather than a fully confirmed fact.

The first record of the Tandara family in the parish registers of Livno was found in 1773, when Frane, the son of Jakov Novokmet and Ivanica Tandara of Lipa, was baptised.

The parish registers for this period are incomplete. There were no records of Tandara births from 1797 to 1802, while no birth and baptism records for children have survived for the period from 1781 to 1797. Nevertheless, the marriage and death registers made it possible to reconstruct a large part of Filip’s genealogy.

Genealogy of Filip’s Line

5.0. Filip Tandara

Filip Tandara – 5.0 is regarded as the founder of the Livno branch of the Tandara family. He was married to Jela Bralić.

They had four sons:

  • 5.1. Mate — son of Filip
  • 5.2. Jure — son of Filip
  • 5.3. Jakov — son of Filip
  • 5.4. Ivan — son of Filip

5.1. Mate — son of Filip

Mate – 5.1, Filip’s eldest son, married Kata Jazvić.

They had a daughter:

  • 5.1.1. Jela — daughter of Mate

5.2. Jure — son of Filip

Jure – 5.2, Filip’s second son, married Manda Matić.

They had a son:

  • 5.2.1. Josip — son of Jure

In 1818, Josip, Jure’s son, married Cvita Knežević.

They had three sons:

  • 5.2.1.1. Jakov — son of Josip
  • 5.2.1.2. Ivan — son of Josip
  • 5.2.1.3. Antun — son of Josip

5.2.1.1. Jakov — son of Josip

Jakov, Josip’s eldest son, married Ruža Rimac.

They had a son:

  • 5.2.1.1.1. Josip — son of Jakov

Josip, Jakov’s son, married Anđa Gabrić.

They had two sons:

  • 5.2.1.1.1.1. Jozo — son of Josip
  • 5.2.1.1.1.2. Mato — son of Josip

They had three daughters:

  • 5.2.1.1.1.3. Ana — daughter of Josip
  • 5.2.1.1.1.4. Mara — daughter of Josip
  • 5.2.1.1.1.5. Anđa — daughter of Josip

5.2.1.1.1.1. Jozo — son of Josip

Jozo, Josip’s son, married twice.

In his first marriage, to Marica Mioč, he had a son:

  • 5.2.1.1.1.1.1. Ante Božidar — son of Jozo

In his second marriage, to Kata Pastuh, he had a son:

  • 5.2.1.1.1.1.2. Jakov — son of Jozo

In the same marriage, he also had a daughter:

  • 5.2.1.1.1.1.3. Ana — daughter of Jozo

5.2.1.2. Ivan — son of Josip

Ivan, the second son of Josip and Cvita, née Knežević, married twice.

He first married Ruža Laštrić in 1859. There is no record that they had children.

He later married Anđa Erceg.

They had two sons:

  • 5.2.1.2.1. Jakov — son of Ivan
  • 5.2.1.2.2. Stipo — son of Ivan

5.2.1.2.1. Jakov — son of Ivan

Jakov, Ivan’s son, married Ruža Meštrović in Livno in 1894.

They had four sons:

  • 5.2.1.2.1.1. Josip — son of Jakov
  • 5.2.1.2.1.2. Stipo — son of Jakov
  • 5.2.1.2.1.3. Ivo — son of Jakov
  • 5.2.1.2.1.4. Matko — son of Jakov

They had four daughters:

  • 5.2.1.2.1.5. Mara — daughter of Jakov
  • 5.2.1.2.1.6. Joza — daughter of Jakov
  • 5.2.1.2.1.7. Milka — daughter of Jakov
  • 5.2.1.2.1.8. Anđa — daughter of Jakov

5.2.1.2.1.4. Matko — son of Jakov

Matko, Jakov’s youngest son, married Anica Župčić.

They had a son:

  • 5.2.1.2.1.4.1. Krešimir — son of Matko

They had a daughter:

  • 5.2.1.2.1.4.2. Davorka — daughter of Matko

Krešimir, Matko’s son, married Biserka Kučak.

They had a son:

  • 5.2.1.2.1.4.1.1. Igor — son of Krešimir

They had a daughter:

  • 5.2.1.2.1.4.1.2. Gordana — daughter of Krešimir

Igor, Krešimir’s son, married Edina Hadžić.

They had two sons:

  • 5.2.1.2.1.4.1.1.1. Izidor — son of Igor
  • 5.2.1.2.1.4.1.1.2. Edgar — son of Igor

5.2.1.2.2. Stipo — son of Ivan

Stipo, the younger son of Ivan and Anđa, née Erceg, married Ana Sučić.

They had three sons:

  • 5.2.1.2.2.1. Ivo — son of Stipo
  • 5.2.1.2.2.2. Pero — son of Stipo
  • 5.2.1.2.2.3. Zvonimir — son of Stipo

They had three daughters:

  • 5.2.1.2.2.4. Iva — daughter of Stipo
  • 5.2.1.2.2.5. Anđa — daughter of Stipo
  • 5.2.1.2.2.6. Ljubica — daughter of Stipo

5.2.1.2.2.3. Zvonimir — son of Stipo

Zvonimir, Stipo’s youngest son, married Adela Szepesi in Belgrade in 1930.

They had a son:

  • 5.2.1.2.2.3.1. Vladimir — son of Zvonimir

They had a daughter:

  • 5.2.1.2.2.3.2. Aneta — daughter of Zvonimir

Vladimir, Zvonimir’s son, married Ingrid Schmarsch in Berlin in 1965.

They had a son:

  • 5.2.1.2.2.3.1.1. Milan-Aleksandar — son of Vladimir

They had a daughter:

  • 5.2.1.2.2.3.1.2. Andrea — daughter of Vladimir

5.3. Jakov — son of Filip

Jakov – 5.3, Filip’s third son, married twice.

In his first marriage, to Luca, née Kuprešanin, he had two daughters:

  • 5.3.1. Josipa — daughter of Jakov
  • 5.3.2. Pera — daughter of Jakov

In his second marriage, to Kata, née Perić, he had a daughter:

  • 5.3.3. Kata — daughter of Jakov

5.4. Ivan — son of Filip

Ivan – 5.4, Filip’s youngest son, was married to Iva, née Knežević.

They had a son:

  • 5.4.2. Petar — son of Ivan

They had a daughter:

  • 5.4.1. Agata — daughter of Ivan

5.4.2. Petar — son of Ivan

Petar, Ivan’s son, married twice.

He first married Luca Smoljić. It is not known whether they had children.

In his second marriage, to Matija, née Čelan, he had a son:

  • 5.4.2.1. Nikola — son of Petar

In the same marriage, he also had two daughters:

  • 5.4.2.2. Agata — daughter of Petar
  • 5.4.2.3. Ana — daughter of Petar
