TANDARAS OF LIVNO – FILIP BRANCH

5.0. Filip Tandara – founder of the Livno branch (no information is available about his years of birth and death)

The Filip branch may be connected with the family of Ivan Tandara from Ričice, but the available documents do not yet confirm such a relationship. To prove this assumption, DNA samples from descendants of both Tandara branches would need to be analysed.

Filip is therefore assigned the designation 5.0 as a possible fifth son of Ivan Tandara, who was born around 1695 in Ričice. His children bear the designations 5.1–5.4, and each subsequent generation receives an additional number based on its parent.

Note: The original text states that Jakov had daughters named Josipa, Pera and Kata. Therefore, the designation 5.3.1 is assigned to Josipa Jakovljeva, not to a male Josip Jakovljev.

Origin of the Filip Lineage

Filip Tandarić is mentioned in 1768 as a resident of the village of Lipa in the Livno parish, where he lived with his family of eight. It is not known where he came from or when he arrived in Lipa, because no direct written records concerning this have yet been found.

Nevertheless, several indications support the assumption that the Tandara families of Livno belong to the family tree of Ivan Tandara of Ričice, born around 1695.

Ivan of Ričice had sons named Jure, Petar, Ante and Mate, while Filip of Livno named his sons Mate, Jure, Jakov and Ivan. Filip's son Ivan may have been named after his possible grandfather Ivan of Ričice, and Filip's son Mate may have been named after Mate of Ričice. The similarity continues in subsequent generations, in which the names Ivan, Petar and Filip recur.

Because of these similarities, Filip is designated in this genealogical account as 5.0 – a possible fifth son of Ivan Tandara of Ričice. However, because no document has yet been found that directly connects Filip with Ivan Tandara, this relationship must be presented as a genealogical hypothesis rather than a fully confirmed fact.

The first record of the Tandara family in the parish registers of Livno was found in 1773, when Frane, the son of Jakov Novokmet and Ivanica Tandara of Lipa, was baptised.

The parish registers for this period are incomplete. There were no records of Tandara births from 1797 to 1802, while no birth and baptism records for children have survived for the period from 1781 to 1797. Nevertheless, the marriage and death registers made it possible to reconstruct a large part of Filip's genealogy.

Genealogy of the Filip Lineage

5.0. Filip Tandara

Filip Tandara – 5.0 is regarded as the founder of the Livno branch of the Tandara family. He was married to Jela Bralić.

They had four sons:

  • 5.1. Mate
  • 5.2. Jure
  • 5.3. Jakov
  • 5.4. Ivan

5.1. Mate Filipov

Mate – 5.1, Filip's eldest son, married Kata Jazvić.

They had a daughter:

  • 5.1.1. Jela Matina

5.2. Jure Filipov

Jure – 5.2, Filip's second son, married Manda Matić.

They had a son:

  • 5.2.1. Josip Jurin

In 1818, Josip married Cvita Knežević.

They had sons:

  • 5.2.1.1. Jakov Josipov
  • 5.2.1.2. Ivan Josipov
  • 5.2.1.3. Antun Josipov

5.2.1.1. Jakov Josipov

Jakov, Josip's eldest son, married Ruža Rimac.

They had a son:

  • 5.2.1.1.1. Josip Jakovljev

Josip Jakovljev married Anđa Gabrić.

They had sons and daughters:

  • 5.2.1.1.1.1. Jozo Josipov
  • 5.2.1.1.1.2. Mato Josipov
  • 5.2.1.1.1.3. Ana Josipova
  • 5.2.1.1.1.4. Mara Josipova
  • 5.2.1.1.1.5. Anđa Josipova

5.2.1.1.1.1. Jozo Josipov

Jozo married twice.

In his first marriage, to Marica Mioč, he had a son:

  • 5.2.1.1.1.1.1. Ante Božidar Jozin

In his second marriage, to Kata Pastuh, he had:

  • 5.2.1.1.1.1.2. Jakov Jozin
  • 5.2.1.1.1.1.3. Ana Jozina

5.2.1.2. Ivan Josipov

Ivan, the second son of Josip and Cvita, née Knežević, married twice.

He first married Ruža Laštrić in 1859. There is no record that they had children.

He later married Anđa Erceg. They had sons:

  • 5.2.1.2.1. Jakov Ivanov
  • 5.2.1.2.2. Stipo Ivanov

5.2.1.2.1. Jakov Ivanov

In 1894, Jakov married Ruža Meštrović in Livno.

They had children:

  • 5.2.1.2.1.1. Josip Jakovljev
  • 5.2.1.2.1.2. Stipo Jakovljev
  • 5.2.1.2.1.3. Ivo Jakovljev
  • 5.2.1.2.1.4. Matko Jakovljev
  • 5.2.1.2.1.5. Mara Jakovljeva
  • 5.2.1.2.1.6. Joza Jakovljeva
  • 5.2.1.2.1.7. Milka Jakovljeva
  • 5.2.1.2.1.8. Anđa Jakovljeva

5.2.1.2.1.4. Matko Jakovljev

Matko, Jakov's youngest son, married Anica Župčić.

They had:

  • 5.2.1.2.1.4.1. Krešimir Matkov
  • 5.2.1.2.1.4.2. Davorka Matkova

Krešimir married Biserka Kučak.

They had:

  • 5.2.1.2.1.4.1.1. Igor Krešimirov
  • 5.2.1.2.1.4.1.2. Gordana Krešimirova

Igor married Edina Hadžić.

They had sons:

  • 5.2.1.2.1.4.1.1.1. Izidor Igorov
  • 5.2.1.2.1.4.1.1.2. Edgar Igorov

5.2.1.2.2. Stipo Ivanov

Stipo, the younger son of Ivan and Anđa, née Erceg, married Ana Sučić.

They had children:

  • 5.2.1.2.2.1. Ivo Stipin
  • 5.2.1.2.2.2. Pero Stipin
  • 5.2.1.2.2.3. Zvonimir Stipin
  • 5.2.1.2.2.4. Iva Stipina
  • 5.2.1.2.2.5. Anđa Stipina
  • 5.2.1.2.2.6. Ljubica Stipina

5.2.1.2.2.3. Zvonimir Stipin

Zvonimir, Stipo's youngest son, married Adela Szepesi in Belgrade in 1930.

They had:

  • 5.2.1.2.2.3.1. Vladimir Zvonimirov
  • 5.2.1.2.2.3.2. Aneta Zvonimirova

In 1965, Vladimir married Ingrid Schmarsch in Berlin.

They had:

  • 5.2.1.2.2.3.1.1. Milan-Aleksandar Vladimirov
  • 5.2.1.2.2.3.1.2. Andrea Vladimirova

5.3. Jakov Filipov

Jakov – 5.3, Filip's third son, married twice.

In his first marriage, to Luca Kuprešanin, he had daughters:

  • 5.3.1. Josipa Jakovljeva
  • 5.3.2. Pera Jakovljeva

In his second marriage, to Kata Perić, he had a daughter:

  • 5.3.3. Kata Jakovljeva

5.4. Ivan Filipov

Ivan – 5.4, Filip's youngest son, was married to Iva Knežević.

They had:

  • 5.4.1. Agata Ivanova
  • 5.4.2. Petar Ivanov

5.4.2. Petar Ivanov

Petar married twice.

He first married Luca Smoljić. It is not known whether they had children.

In his second marriage, to Matija Čelan, he had:

  • 5.4.2.1. Nikola Petrov
  • 5.4.2.2. Agata Petrova
  • 5.4.2.3. Ana Petrova
