The Tandaras of Livno – Filip’s Branch

5.0 Filip Tandara – Founder of the Livno Branch

Filip’s branch may be connected to the family of Ivan Tandara from Ričice, although the available documents do not currently confirm such a relationship. To establish this hypothesis, DNA samples from descendants of both Tandara branches would need to be analysed.

Filip is therefore assigned the designation 5.0 as a possible fifth son of Ivan Tandara, who was born around 1695 in Ričice. His children are designated 5.1–5.4, and each subsequent generation receives an additional number based on its parent.

Origins of Filip’s Line

Filip Tandarić is mentioned in 1768 as a resident of the village of Lipa in the Parish of Livno, where he lived with his family of eight. It is not known where he came from or when he arrived in Lipa because no direct written records have yet been found.

Nevertheless, several indications support the hypothesis that the Tandaras of Livno belong to the family tree of Ivan Tandara of Ričice, who was born around 1695.

Ivan of Ričice had four sons—Jure, Petar, Ante, and Mate—while Filip of Livno named his sons Mate, Jure, Jakov, and Ivan. Filip’s son Ivan may have been named after his possible grandfather, Ivan of Ričice, while Filip’s son Mate may have been named after Mate of Ričice. This similarity continues in later generations, in which the names Ivan, Petar, and Filip recur.

Because of these similarities, Filip is designated 5.0 in this genealogical account as a possible fifth son of Ivan Tandara of Ričice. However, because no document directly linking Filip to Ivan Tandara has yet been found, this connection must be presented as a genealogical hypothesis rather than a fully established fact.

The first record of the Tandara family in the parish registers of Livno dates from 1773, when Frane, the son of Jakov Novokmet and Ivanica Tandara of Lipa, was baptised.

The parish registers for this period are incomplete. There were no records of Tandara births from 1797 to 1802, while no records of births and baptisms have survived for the period from 1781 to 1797. Nevertheless, the marriage and death registers made it possible to reconstruct a substantial part of Filip’s genealogy.

Genealogy of Filip’s Line

5.0 Filip Tandara (dates unknown)

Filip Tandara (5.0) is regarded as the founder of the Livno branch of the Tandara family. He was married to Jela Bralić.

They had four sons:

  • 5.1 Mate
  • 5.2 Jure
  • 5.3 Jakov
  • 5.4 Ivan

5.1 Mate, son of Filip

Mate, Filip’s eldest son, married Kata Jazvić.

They had one daughter:

  • 5.1.1 Jela (no information available)

5.2 Jure, son of Filip (d. 1804)

Jure, Filip’s second son, married Manda Matić.

They had one son:

  • 5.2.1 Josip (no information available)

5.2.1 Josip, son of Jure

Josip married Cvita Knežević in 1818.

They had three sons:

  • 5.2.1.1 Jakov
  • 5.2.1.2 Ivan
  • 5.2.1.3 Ante

5.2.1.1 Jakov, son of Josip (no information available)

Jakov, Josip’s eldest son, married Ruža Rimac.

They had one son:

  • 5.2.1.1.1 Josip (no information available)

5.2.1.1.1 Josip, son of Jakov

Josip, son of Jakov, married Anđa Gabrić.

They had two sons:

  • 5.2.1.1.1.1 Jozo
  • 5.2.1.1.1.2 Mate (b. 1891)

and three daughters:

  • 5.2.1.1.1.3 Ana (b. 1887)
  • 5.2.1.1.1.4 Mara (b. 1889)
  • 5.2.1.1.1.5 Anđa (b. 1893)

5.2.1.1.1.1 Jozo, son of Josip (b. 1884)

Jozo married twice.

In his first marriage to Marica Mioč, he had one son:

  • 5.2.1.1.1.1.1 Ante Božidar (1909–1933)

In his second marriage to Kata Pastuh, he had one son:

  • 5.2.1.1.1.1.2 Jakov (1914–1915)

and one daughter:

  • 5.2.1.1.1.1.3 Ana (1916–1916)

5.2.1.1.1.1.1 Ante Božidar, son of Jozo (1909–1933)

Ante Božidar was married to Slavka Perić Mandić. No information is available as to whether they had children.

5.2.1.2 Ivan, son of Josip

Ivan, the second son of Josip and Cvita née Knežević, married twice.

He first married Ruža Laštrić in 1859. There is no record of whether they had children.

He later married Anđa Erceg. They had two sons:

  • 5.2.1.2.1 Jakov
  • 5.2.1.2.2 Stipo

5.2.1.2.1 Jakov, son of Ivan (no information available)

Jakov married Ruža Meštrović in Livno in 1894.

They had four sons:

  • 5.2.1.2.1.1 Josip (b. 1898)
  • 5.2.1.2.1.2 Stipo (1904–1985)
  • 5.2.1.2.1.3 Ivo (b. 1907)
  • 5.2.1.2.1.4 Matko

and four daughters:

  • 5.2.1.2.1.5 Mara (b. 1895)
  • 5.2.1.2.1.6 Joza Milka (b. 1900)

  • 5.2.1.2.1.8 Anđa (b. 1909)

5.2.1.2.1.2 Stipo, son of Jakov

He was married to Elza Marjanović, with whom he had no children.

5.2.1.2.1.4 Matko, son of Jakov (b. 1911)

Matko, Jakov’s youngest son, married Anica Župčić.

They had one son:

  • 5.2.1.2.1.4.1 Krešimir (b. 1953)

and one daughter:

  • 5.2.1.2.1.4.2 Davorka (b. 1951)

5.2.1.2.1.4.1 Krešimir, son of Matko (b. 1953)

  • 5.2.1.2.1.4.1.1 Igor (b. 1974)

5.2.1.2.2 Stipo, son of Ivan

Stipo, the younger son of Ivan and Anđa née Erceg, married Ana Sučić.

They had three sons:

  • 5.2.1.2.2.1 Ivo (b. 1896)
  • 5.2.1.2.2.2 Pero (b. 1898)

  • 5.2.1.2.2.4 Iva (b. 1901)
  • 5.2.1.2.2.5 Anđa (b. 1903)

5.2.1.2.2.3 Zvonimir, son of Stipo (1906–1944)

They had one son:

  • 5.2.1.2.2.3.1 Vladimir (1936–1992)

and one daughter:

  • 5.2.1.2.2.3.2 Aneta (b. 1934)

5.3 Jakov, son of Filip (d. 1815)

Jakov (5.3), Filip’s third son, married twice.

In his first marriage to Luca née Kuprešanin, he had two daughters:

  • 5.3.1 Josipa (no information available)
  • 5.3.2 Pera (no information available)

In his second marriage to Kata née Perić, he had one daughter:

  • 5.3.3 Kata (1807–1915)

5.4 Ivan, son of Filip

Ivan, Filip’s youngest son, was married to Iva née Knežević.

They had one son:

  • 5.4.2 Petar (no information available)

and one daughter:

  • 5.4.1 Agata (no information available)

5.4.2 Petar, son of Ivan

Petar married twice.

He first married Luca Smoljić. It is not known whether they had children.

In his second marriage to Matija née Čelan, he had one son:

  • 5.4.2.1 Nikola (b. 1828)
